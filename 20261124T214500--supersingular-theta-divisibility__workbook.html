§15.1. Gaussian moments Tag 22FH

Let \(\zeta = e^{2\pi\mathrm{i}/8}\), and for integers \(a, w \ge 0\) consider the moments \[m(a,w) := \sum_{x \in \mathbb{Z}[\mathrm{i}]^2} (x_1 + \zeta x_2)^a\, (x_1 - \zeta x_2)^w\, e^{-\pi(\lvert x_1 \rvert^2 + \lvert x_2 \rvert^2)} .\] After a normalization they are rational numbers. How divisible are they by a prime \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\), that is, by a prime that is inert in \(\mathbb{Q}(\mathrm{i})\)?

Let \(\operatorname{sl}\) be the lemniscate sine, the meromorphic function with \(\operatorname{sl}(0) = 0\), \(\operatorname{sl}'(0) = 1\) and \(\operatorname{sl}'^2 = 1 - \operatorname{sl}^4\), and let \(\varpi := 2\int_0^1 (1 - t^4)^{-1/2} \, d t\). Let \(\wp\) and \(\sigma\) be the Weierstrass functions attached to \(\Lambda = \varpi\mathbb{Z}[\mathrm{i}]\), whose invariants are \(g_2 = 4\) and \(g_3 = 0\) (Problem 11.1.9). Let \[F := \sigma^2 \wp .\]

For instance \(t(3,1) = 8\), \(t(4,4) = 448\) and \(t(8,0) = -576\). The table lists \(v_{11}(t(a,w))\) for \(w \le 2\) and the first nine values of \(a \ge 8\) allowed by Problem 15.1.5.

\(v_{11}\) 0 1 2 2 3 4 5 5 6

This chapter treats one variable: what \(p\) divides the derivatives of a function on the lemniscate, and why \(F\) alone does not gain. The sequel, The isotropic plane, proves a lower bound of the shape suggested by the \(11\)-adic valuations in the table at every prime \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 8\).

§15.2. The lemniscate and its formal group Tag 22FP

Let \(E\) be the smooth projective model of \(y^2 = 1 - X^4\), with origin \((0,1)\). It is a curve of genus one with good reduction at every odd prime. The map \(u \mapsto (\operatorname{sl}u, \operatorname{sl}' u)\) identifies \(\mathbb{C}/L\) with \(E(\mathbb{C})\), where \(L \subset \Lambda\) is the period lattice of \(\operatorname{sl}\). The function \(X\) is a parameter at the origin, \(du = dX/y\) is an invariant differential, and the addition law is \[\operatorname{sl}(u + v) = \frac{\operatorname{sl}u\, \operatorname{sl}' v + \operatorname{sl}v\, \operatorname{sl}' u}{1 + \operatorname{sl}^2 u\, \operatorname{sl}^2 v} .\] Let \(D := d/du = y \, d/dX\) be the invariant derivation, and let \(\ell(X) := \int_0^X (1 - v^4)^{-1/2} \, dv\) be the logarithm, so that \(\operatorname{sl}(\ell(X)) = X\).

Tag 22FQ

Problem 15.2.1. \(\operatorname{sl}u = u - \tfrac1{10}u^5 + \tfrac1{120}u^9 - \tfrac{11}{15600}u^{13} + \dotsb\). Only exponents \(\equiv 1 \pmod 4\) occur, \(\operatorname{sl}(\mathrm{i}u) = \mathrm{i}\, \operatorname{sl}u\), and the lattice \(L\) satisfies \(\mathrm{i}L = L\).

Tag 22FR

Problem 15.2.2. The formal group law \(X \oplus X'\), defined by \(\operatorname{sl}u \oplus \operatorname{sl}v = \operatorname{sl}(u + v)\), has coefficients in \(\mathbb{Z}[\tfrac12]\), and after extension of scalars to \(\mathbb{Z}[\tfrac12,\mathrm{i}]\) it has the endomorphism \(X \mapsto \mathrm{i}X\).

Used in: Chapter 19: Problem 19.1.3
Tag 22FS

Problem 15.2.3. \(D\) maps \(\mathbb{Z}[\tfrac12][[X]]\) into itself, and \((D^n f)(0) = n!\, [u^n]\, f(\operatorname{sl}u)\) for \(f \in \mathbb{Q}[[X]]\).

Used in: Chapter 19

Fix an odd prime \(p\) and an embedding \(\mathbb{Q}(\zeta) \hookrightarrow \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p\), and write \(\zeta\) and \(\mathrm{i}= \zeta^2\) also for their images. Let \(\mathcal{O}:= \mathbb{Z}_p[\mathrm{i}] \subseteq \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_p\), and let \(v_p\) be the \(p\)-adic valuation normalized by \(v_p(p) = 1\). For \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\), \(\mathcal{O}\) is the ring of integers of the unramified quadratic extension of \(\mathbb{Q}_p\), and it contains \(\zeta\), since \(8\) divides \(p^2 - 1\). We write \(\hat E\) for the formal group of Problem 15.2.2 over \(\mathcal{O}\).

Tag 22FT

Problem 15.2.4. For each \(\alpha \in \mathcal{O}\) the series \([\alpha](X) := \operatorname{sl}(\alpha \ell(X))\) lies in \(\mathcal{O}[[X]]\). It is the unique endomorphism of \(\hat E\) with \([\alpha](X) = \alpha X + O(X^2)\).

Used in: Chapter 16

When \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\), this action of \(\mathcal{O}\) makes \(\hat E\) a formal \(\mathcal{O}\)-module of the kind constructed by Lubin and Tate [LT]; we use only Problem 15.2.4. For these primes \(E\) is supersingular and \(\hat E\) has height two [Si1, Thm. V.3.1]. Indeed \(E\) is isogenous to \(y^2 = x^3 - x\) by \((X, y) \mapsto (X^{-2}, -yX^{-3})\), and that curve is supersingular exactly when \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\) [Si1]. Section 15.3 shows what height two does to \(D\).

§15.3. The \(p\)th power of the invariant derivation Tag 22FU

Let \(f\) be a function on \(E\), regular at the origin, with expansion in \(\mathcal{O}[[X]]\). Its derivatives \((D^n f)(0)\) are integral by Problem 15.2.3. We show that they are divisible by \(p^{\lfloor n/p \rfloor}\) when \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\). The smallest case is \(p = 3\): \(DX = y\), \(D^2X = -2X^3\) and \(D^3X = -6X^2y\).

For a series \(f\), let \(\tau_Y f(X) := f(X \oplus Y)\), a series in two variables.

Tag 22FV

Problem 15.3.1. The operator induced by \(D^p\) on \((\mathcal{O}/p\mathcal{O})[[X]]\) is a derivation.

Used in: Chapter 13
Tag 22FW

Problem 15.3.2. Let \(k\) be a field of characteristic \(p\), and write \(D\) and \(\tau_Y\) for the operators obtained by reducing the coefficients of the invariant derivation and the formal group law from \(\mathbb{Z}[\tfrac12]\) to \(k\). Let \(\delta\) be a continuous \(k\)-linear derivation of \(k[[X]]\), acting on \(k[[X,Y]]\) with \(Y\) held fixed. If \(\delta \tau_Y = \tau_Y \delta\), then \(\delta = (\delta X)(0)\, D\). The operator \(D\) itself commutes with \(\tau_Y\).

Used in: Chapter 13

Thus \(D^p \equiv \lambda_p D \pmod p\), with \(\lambda_p \equiv (D^p X)(0) = p!\, [u^p] \operatorname{sl}u\).

Tag 22FX

Problem 15.3.3. If \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\), then \(\lambda_p \equiv 0\), so that \(D^p\, \mathcal{O}[[X]] \subseteq p\, \mathcal{O}[[X]]\).

Tag 22FY

Problem 15.3.4. Let \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\), let \(f \in \mathcal{O}[[X_1, X_2]]\), and let \(D_1\), \(D_2\) be the invariant derivations in the two variables. Then \((D_1^a D_2^w f)(0) \in p^{\lfloor a/p \rfloor}\mathcal{O}\).

Used in: Chapter 16

At \(p \equiv 1 \pmod 4\) there is no such gain: \(\lambda_p\) is then a unit, since \(\lambda_p \equiv p\,[X^p]\,\ell = 4^{-m}\binom{2m}{m}\) for \(p = 4m+1\) by the argument of Problem 13.2.3, and for instance \((D^5X)(0) = -12\). Katz studies congruences for invariant differential operators on Lubin–Tate formal groups of arbitrary finite height [Ka].

§15.4. The higher odd levels Tag 22FZ

At \(p = 3\), the integer \((D^{29}X)(0)\) is divisible exactly by \(3^{10}\), one more than \(\lfloor 29/3 \rfloor = 9\). Where does the extra factor come from? Throughout this section \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\) and \(q := p^2\), and we write \[E_p(n) := \sum_{\substack{i \ge 1 \\ i \text{ odd}}} \Bigl\lfloor \frac{n}{p^i} \Bigr\rfloor .\] The answer is that \(D^n\) gains \(p^{E_p(n)}\).

Tag 22G2

Problem 15.4.1. \([X^{4m+1}]\, \ell = \binom{2m}{m} / \bigl(4^m (4m+1)\bigr)\), and \(\ell\) has \(p\)-integral coefficients in all degrees less than \(q\).

Tag 22G3

Problem 15.4.2. \(p\, [X^q]\, \ell \equiv -1 \pmod p\). (For \(p = 3\): \([X^9]\, \ell = 1/24\).)

Used in: Chapter 13

By Problems 15.4.1 and 15.4.2, the truncation \(T\) of \(\ell(X) + X^q/p\) to degree at most \(q\) lies in \(X + X^2\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}[X]\). It is therefore a parameter on \(\hat E\) over \(\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}\), and \(\mathcal{O}[[T]] = \mathcal{O}[[X]]\). In this parameter \[\ell = T - \frac{T^q}{p} + O(T^{q+1}), \qquad g(z) := \ell^{-1}(z) = z + \frac{z^q}{p} + O(z^{q+1}),\] where \(O(\cdot)\) allows arbitrary rational coefficients. Write the group law in \(T\) again as \(\oplus\), and define operators \(\Delta_j\) on \(\mathcal{O}[[T]]\) by \(f(T \oplus Y) = \sum_{j \ge 0} (\Delta_j f)(T)\, Y^j\). They commute with each other and with \(D\), and \(\Delta_1 = D\).

Tag 22G4

Problem 15.4.3. For \(f \in \mathcal{O}[[T]]\), \(f(T \oplus g(z)) = \sum_{n \ge 0} (D^n f)(T)\, z^n/n!\). Consequently \(D^p = p!\, \Delta_p\) and \(D^q = q!\, (\Delta_q + D/p)\).

Tag 22G5

Problem 15.4.4. \(\varepsilon := q!/p^{p+1}\) is a \(p\)-adic unit, and \(D^q = p^p \varepsilon\, H\) with \(H := D + p\Delta_q\).

Used in: Chapter 19
Tag 22G6

Problem 15.4.5. Let \(A\), \(B\) be commuting \(\mathcal{O}\)-linear operators on a torsion-free \(\mathcal{O}\)-module \(V\) with \((A - B)V \subseteq pV\). Then \((A^p - B^p)V \subseteq p^2V\).

Let \(e_j := E_p(p^j) = p^{j-1} + p^{j-3} + \dotsb\), the sum over the exponents \(j - i\) with \(i \le j\) odd. Thus \(e_0 = 0\) and \(e_1 = 1\). For \(j \ge 2\) one has \(e_j = p^{j-1} + e_{j-2}\), and for \(j \ge 1\) one has \(e_j \le p\,e_{j-1} + 1\).

Tag 22G7

Problem 15.4.6. For every \(j \ge 0\), as operators on \(\mathcal{O}[[T]]\), \[D^{p^j} \equiv 0 \pmod{p^{e_j}}, \qquad H^{p^j} \equiv D^{p^j} \pmod{p^{e_j + 1}} .\]

Used in: Chapter 19

§15.5. One theta function Tag 22GA

The function \(F\) of Section 15.1 is not a function on \(E\). Does it nevertheless gain? At \(p = 3\) the valuations \(v_3(F^{(k)}(0))\) for \(k = 4, 8, \dotsc, 60\) are \[0,\ 0,\ 1,\ 1,\ 2,\ 2,\ 4,\ 4,\ 4,\ 4,\ 4,\ 5,\ 5,\ 7,\ 7,\] far below \(\lfloor k/3 \rfloor\).

Tag 22GB

Problem 15.5.1. \((\log F)'' = 2\operatorname{sl}^2\).

Used in: Chapter 16

Let \(b_n := (D^n X^2)(0) = n!\, [u^n]\, \operatorname{sl}^2 u\), an element of \(\mathbb{Z}[\tfrac12]\). By Problem 15.5.1, \[\log F(u) = 2 \sum_{n \ge 0} b_n \frac{u^{n+2}}{(n+2)!} .\]

Tag 22GC

Problem 15.5.2. Let \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\) and \(m := (p-3)/4\). Then \(b_{p-1} \equiv -4^{-m}\binom{2m}{m} \pmod p\). In particular \(b_{p-1}\) is a \(p\)-adic unit. (For \(p = 3\): \(b_2 = 2\).)

Tag 22GD

Problem 15.5.3. Let \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\). Then \(F^{(p+1)}(0) \equiv 2 b_{p-1} \pmod p\). Hence \(F\), written as a series in \(X = \operatorname{sl}u\), does not have \(p\)-integral coefficients. (For \(p = 3\): \(F^{(4)}(0) = 4\).)

Used in: Chapter 13; Chapter 16; Chapter 23

For \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\), the coefficient of \(u^{p+1}\) in \(\log F\) is \(c_p/p\) with \(c_p = 2b_{p-1}/\bigl((p+1)(p-1)!\bigr)\) a unit. For \(p = 3, 7, 11\) this coefficient is \(\tfrac16, -\tfrac1{140}, \tfrac1{2475}\), so \(c_p = \tfrac12, -\tfrac1{20}, \tfrac1{225}\). By the Exercise below, for \(g(u) := \exp(c_p u^{p+1}/p)\) one has \(v_p(g^{(k)}(0)) = k/(p^2-1) + O(\log k)\) as \(k\) tends to infinity through multiples of \(p+1\). At \(p=3\) the comparison gives \(60/8=7.5\), while \(v_3(F^{(60)}(0))=7\). We do not know whether \(v_p(F^{(k)}(0)) = k/(p^2-1) + O(\log k)\) holds for each fixed prime \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\) as \(k\) tends to infinity through multiples of \(4\); the other derivatives vanish at \(0\) by Problem 15.1.2.

For \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\), the first term of the \(u\)-series of \(\log F\) with a non-\(p\)-integral coefficient is \(c_p u^{p+1}/p\). We next consider the product \(\Theta\) in two variables. Chapter 16, The isotropic plane, shows that for \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 8\) its restrictions to the axes in the directions \(e_\pm\) are integral in the lemniscate parameter (Theorem 16.2.1). After division by the two axis restrictions, the remaining factor equals \(1\) on both axes (Problem 16.3.2). At \(p = 7\) the axis restriction is not integral (Problem 16.2.6).

Exercise

Let \(c\) be a \(p\)-adic unit and let \(g(u) := \exp(c u^{p+1}/p)\). For an integer \(m \ge 0\), write \(k := (p+1)m\). Then \[v_p\bigl(k!\, [u^k]\, g\bigr) = \frac{k}{p^2 - 1} + \frac{s_p(m) - s_p(k)}{p-1},\] where \(s_p\) is the sum of base-\(p\) digits.
