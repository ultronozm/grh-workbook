In the twisting theorem (Theorem 85.1.2 of Chapter 85), fix the data \(D\) of Chapter 36, a prime \(\ell\), \(\iota \colon \mathbb{C}\simeq \bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\), and a finite family \(R_i\) of main-summand-like \(\ell\)-adic representations of \(G_E\) (Definition 80.1.7). Write \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell := (4f + 3)r\,\operatorname{val}_\ell(\mathcal{N}) + 2^{-\ell}\), as in Definition 80.1.1. The theorem gives one \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\) such that, for each \(1 \le j \le m\), \(\vartheta^j\) is auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\) (Definition 80.1.5), every twisted representation \(\widehat{\vartheta^j}R_i\) has Selmer bound \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\) (Definition 80.1.9), and \(\operatorname{val}_+(\iota(R_P(\vartheta^j, \epsilon))) \le \mathrm{tw}_\ell\) for every ramified critical pair \(P = (\nu, z)\) and each sign \(\epsilon = \pm 1\). The proof uses a Stickelberger system for each ramified pair and sign, the polynomial input of Theorem 87.6.1, and the simultaneous choice of roots of unity in Chapter 85.

How do twisted partial Hecke \(L\)-values give a measure (Definition 89.2.1) whose coefficients at modulus \(Q\) are integral away from \(\mathrm{Nm}Q\)? How does this measure give elements \(\mathrm{St}_Q \in \bar{\mathbb{Z}}_\ell[\mathrm{Cl}_Q]\) obeying Euler-factor insertion, and where does the construction use ramification at the chosen place \(w_0\)? This chapter works out unramified and ramified models over \(\mathbb{Q}(\mathrm{i})\), defines the measure of a critical pair, and states Theorem 89.3.1.

Throughout, \(\ell\) is a prime, \(\operatorname{val}\) is the valuation of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\) with \(\operatorname{val}(\ell) = 1\) and \(\operatorname{val}(0) = \infty\), \(\bar{\mathbb{Z}}_\ell\) is its valuation ring, \(\operatorname{val}_+ := \max(\operatorname{val}, 0)\), \(\lVert y\rVert := \ell^{-\operatorname{val}(y)}\), and \(\operatorname{val}_\ell\) is the \(\ell\)-adic valuation on \(\mathbb{Q}\); inner products \(\langle\cdot, \cdot\rangle\) are conjugate-linear in the first variable.

§89.1. Unramified and ramified models over \(\mathbb{Q}(\mathrm{i})\) Tag 23BB

Since \(\mathbb{Q}(\mathrm{i})\) is not a field of the data of Chapter 36, the constructions in this section are models. Write \(\mathrm{i}:= \sqrt{-1}\), \(\mathrm{N}(z) := z\bar z\) on \(\mathbb{Q}(\mathrm{i})\), and \(\Omega := 2\int_0^1 (1 - t^4)^{-1/2}\,dt\) for the lemniscate period (\(\varpi\) in Chapter 11, distinct from \(\Omega\) in Chapter 36), so \(\sum_{0 \ne \lambda \in \mathbb{Z}[\mathrm{i}]} \lambda^{-4} = \Omega^4/15\) by Hurwitz’s formula (Problem 11.3.1; status: classical). For a prime \(q \equiv 1 \pmod 4\), fix \(\pi \in \mathbb{Z}[\mathrm{i}]\) with \(q = \pi\bar\pi\), identify \(\mathbb{Z}[\mathrm{i}]/(\pi) \cong \mathbb{F}_q\), and put \(\mathrm{Cl}_q := \mathbb{F}_q^\times / \{\pm 1\}\), cyclic of order \((q-1)/2\); for \(\lambda \in \mathbb{Z}[\mathrm{i}]\) with \((\lambda, q) = 1\), let \([\lambda] \in \mathrm{Cl}_q\) be the coset of \(\lambda/\bar\lambda \bmod \pi\). For a finite set \(Q\) of places \(w = (\pi_w)\) above distinct primes \(q_w \equiv 1 \pmod 4\), put \(\mathrm{Nm}Q := \prod_{w \in Q} q_w\), \([\lambda]_Q := ([\lambda]_{q_w})_{w \in Q}\), and \(\mathrm{St}_Q := (15/\Omega^4) \sum_{\substack{0 \ne \lambda \in \mathbb{Z}[\mathrm{i}] \\ (\lambda, \mathrm{Nm}Q) = 1}} \lambda^{-4}\,[\lambda]_Q \in \mathbb{C}\bigl[\prod_{w \in Q} \mathrm{Cl}_{q_w}\bigr]\), so \(1(\mathrm{St}_\varnothing) = 1\) at the trivial character \(1\); for \(Q = \{(\pi)\}\) write \(\mathrm{St}_q = \sum_{u \in \mathrm{Cl}_q} c_u\,[u]\) with \(c_u := (15/\Omega^4) \sum_{(\lambda, q) = 1,\, [\lambda] = u} \lambda^{-4} \in \mathbb{C}\).

Recall (Definition 87.5.1) that a Stickelberger system for \((\Sigma_0, g_0)\) over \(E\) has a continuous character \(\alpha \colon G_E \to \bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell^\times\) unramified outside \(\Sigma_0\) with \(\alpha(g_0) \ne 1\), a finite list \(L\) of integer exponents, and an element \(\mathrm{St}_Q \in \bar{\mathbb{Z}}_\ell[\mathrm{Cl}_Q]\) for each finite set \(Q\) of finite places, with \(1(\mathrm{St}_\varnothing) \ne 0\), obeying Euler-factor insertion when \(v \notin Q\) and \(Q \cup \{v\}\) is admissible for \(\Sigma_0\). The first problem checks that \([\lambda]\) and \(c_u\) are well defined, that \(\chi(\mathrm{St}_q)\) is real, and how \(\mathrm{Cl}_q\) maps onto \(\mathrm{Cl}_{\{w\}}\).

§89.2. Measures and Stickelberger systems Tag 23BL

We return to the data \(D\) of Chapters 36 and 37: the CM field \(E\) with infinite places \(\Phi\), \(M = p_0p_1\), \(N\), \(\mathcal{M}\), \(\mathcal{N}\), tame characters \(\tau\) of modulus \(Q_\tau\) and norm \(\mathrm{Nm}Q_\tau\) (§37.6; \(\tau\) is a tame character below), twists \(\xi\), and critical pairs \(P = (\nu, z)\) of negative weight \(\mathrm{wt}_P \colon \Phi \to \mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}\) (ramified when the tame part of \(\nu\) is nontrivial precisely on \(\mathcal{M}\)), with \(L^S(z, \mu)\) and \(R_P(\xi, \epsilon) = \lvert L^{\mathcal{M}\cup Q_\xi}(z, (\nu\xi^{\epsilon})^{-1})\rvert / \lvert L^{\mathcal{M}}(z, \nu^{-1})\rvert\) (Definition 37.10.1). Put \((L_z, n_z) := ([0, 1], 2)\) if \(z = 1\) and \(([z], 1)\) if \(z \ge 2\); for \(v \notin \mathcal{M}\cup \bar{\mathcal{M}}\cup Q_\tau \cup \bar Q_\tau\), let \(c_v(\nu\tau)\) be the value of \(x \mapsto (\nu\tau)(x/\bar x)\) at a uniformizer at \(v\). The following definition states the axioms of a measure.

Definition 89.2.1. Let \(P = (\nu, z)\) be a critical pair. A measure of \(P\) is a function \(\mathrm{ms}_P\) from tame characters to \(\mathbb{C}\) such that:

  1. \(\mathrm{ms}_P(1) \ne 0\), where \(1\) is the trivial tame character (\(Q_1 = \varnothing\));

  2. for every modulus \(Q\) of a tame character, there are finitely many norm-one ideles \(u_i\) of \(E\) and \(c_i \in \mathcal{O}^{[\mathrm{Nm}Q]}\) such that \(\mathrm{ms}_P(\tau) = \sum_i c_i\,\tau(u_i)\) whenever \(Q_\tau = Q\);

  3. if \(\tau, \tau'\) have the same underlying character and tame part and \(Q_{\tau'} = Q_\tau \cup \{v\}\) for \(v \notin Q_\tau\), then \[\mathrm{ms}_P(\tau') = \mathrm{ms}_P(\tau) \prod_{e \in L_z} \prod_{\epsilon = \pm 1} \bigl(1 - c_v(\nu\tau)^{\epsilon}\,(\#k_v)^{-e}\bigr) ;\]

  4. for every twist \(\xi\), \(\mathrm{ms}_P(\xi) = \mathrm{ms}_P(1)\,(\mathrm{Nm}Q_\xi)^{n_z - 1}\,R_P(\xi, 1)^{n_z}\).

Fix \(\iota \colon \mathbb{C}\simeq \bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\), let \(\Sigma_0\) be the finite places of \(E\) dividing \(\ell MN\), let \(w_0 \in \mathcal{M}\) not divide \(\ell\), and let \(g_0 = s_{w_0}\) be its tame generator (§80.1). A character \(\chi\) for \(Q\) has \(\chi([y/\bar y]) = \chi(\mathrm{Art}\,y)\) on \(\mathrm{Cl}_Q\) (§87.5), and \(\hat\xi(\mathrm{Art}\,y) = \iota(\xi(y/\bar y))^{-1}\) (Definition 80.1.5); for \(\epsilon = \pm 1\), \(\xi^{\epsilon}\) denotes the tame character of modulus \(Q_\xi\) with character \(\xi^{\epsilon}\) and tame part \((\psi_w^{\epsilon})\). The next problem bounds \(\mathrm{bd}(\mathrm{St}) = \operatorname{val}(1(\mathrm{St}_\varnothing)) + 2\,\#L\,\operatorname{val}(\alpha(g_0) - 1)\) at a root of unity \(\alpha(g_0) \ne 1\).

§89.3. Norms of lifts, a period at \(1\), and the systems of the ratios Tag 23BQ

Let \(P = (\nu, z)\) be a critical pair with \(z \ge 2\), with lift \(\phi_{P,\tau}\) in rank \(2z\), denominator \(d_P\), and \(\mathrm{alg}_P(\tau) = \mathcal{N}\,d_P^2\,\lVert\phi_{P,\tau}\rVert^2/(\mathrm{Nm}Q_\tau)^{2z-1} \in \mathcal{O}^{[\mathrm{Nm}Q_\tau]}\) satisfying \(R_P(\tau, 1) = \mathrm{alg}_P(\tau)/\mathrm{alg}_P(1)\) (Theorem 77.4.1). For tame characters \(\tau\) of a fixed modulus \(Q\), the proof writes the value of the lift at each finite adelic point \(h\) of the target as \(\phi_{P,\tau}(h) = \sum_i \tau(\det g_i)^{-1} K_i(h)\). Here the \(g_i\) are adelic points of \(\mathrm U(1)\) with infinite component \(1\), and \(K_i(h)\) is the scaled theta kernel at \((g_i, h)\) for the trivial character regarded as a tame character of modulus \(Q\). The points and kernel functions are fixed once \(P\) and \(Q\) are fixed. At every \(h\) and every ring homomorphism \(\eta \colon \mathbb{C}\to \mathbb{C}_\ell\) for a prime \(\ell \nmid \mathrm{Nm}Q\), the value \(d_P K_i(h)\) has gauge at most \(1\). The next problem gives the pointwise inner product identity.

Problem 89.3.1. For \(K_1, \dots, K_n\) in a complex inner product space \(V\), \(g_1, \dots, g_n\) in the adelic points of a unitary group for \(E/E^+\), where \(E^+\) is the maximal real subfield of \(E\), and a unitary character \(\tau\) of the norm-one ideles of \(E\), \(T(\tau) := \sum_{i=1}^n \tau(\det g_i)^{-1} K_i\) has \(\lVert T(\tau)\rVert^2 = \sum_{i, j = 1}^n \langle K_j, K_i\rangle\,\tau(\det g_j / \det g_i)\).

At fixed representatives of the classes for the level of the lift, let \(B_{ji}\) be the class sum of the Gram pairings \(\langle K_j(h), K_i(h)\rangle\), weighted by the inverse orders of the class stabilizers. The proof uses the coefficients \(\mathcal{N}\,d_P^2 B_{ji}/(\mathrm{Nm}Q)^{2z-1}\), which are integral away from \(\mathrm{Nm}Q\), for clause (2) of Definition 89.2.1. Together with Theorem 77.4.1 and the deletion of the Euler factors at \(v\) and \(\bar v\) when the same tame character is regarded at modulus \(Q_\tau \cup \{v\}\), this gives that \(\mathrm{ms}_P := \mathrm{alg}_P\) is a measure of \(P\) for \(z \ge 2\). Status: read from the proof; not proved here.

When \(z = 1\), rank \(2z = 2\) falls outside Theorem 77.4.1. For a ramified critical pair \(P = (\nu, 1)\), let \(\Lambda(\tau)\) be the determinant theta period at the standard level on the definite unitary group of rank \(2\), for the character \(\nu\tau\upsilon\), the determinant exponents \(\mathrm{wt}_P(\sigma) - 1\) (\(\sigma \in \Phi\)), and the product of the tame parts of \(\nu\) and \(\tau\). from sage.all import ComplexIntervalField

C = ComplexIntervalField(240)
R, i = C.real_field(), C.gen()
p = R.pi()
tail = 162*p**4*(-49*p).exp() / (1-(-2*p).exp())
e = tail.upper()
error = C(R(-e, e), R(-e, e))

def row(z):
    return p**4*(2+(2*p*z).cos()) / (3*(p*z).sin()**4)

def column(z, omit_zero=False):
    return error + sum(p**4/45 if omit_zero and n == 0
                       else row(z+n*i) for n in range(-24, 25))

G4 = column(C(0), True).real()
