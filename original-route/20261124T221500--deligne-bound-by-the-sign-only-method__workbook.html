§S2.1. The lowest degree Tag 22YE

Nothing in §S1.3 used \(\dim U = 1\) except the degrees of the cohomology groups, the finite map \(\pi\) to \(\mathbb{A}^1\) and the shape of the coupling \(\pi(u_i)\pi(u_{i+1})\). What does the sign-only method give for \(U\) of dimension \(d\)? Fix \(\iota \colon \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\to \mathbb{C}\). A constructible sheaf \(\mathcal{F}\) on \(X\) has weights at most \(w\) if at every closed point \(x\) all eigenvalues of the local Frobenius have absolute value at most \(\lvert \kappa(x) \rvert^{w/2}\). It is pure of weight \(w\) if all these absolute values equal \(\lvert \kappa(x) \rvert^{w/2}\).

Tag 22YF

Theorem S2.1.1 (Deligne). Let \(X\) be separated of finite type over \(\mathbb{F}_q\), and \(\mathcal{F}\) a constructible \(\overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\)-sheaf on \(X\).

  • If \(\mathcal{F}\) has weights at most \(w\), every eigenvalue of \(\mathrm{Fr}\) on \(H^i_c(X, \mathcal{F})\) has absolute value at most \(q^{(w+i)/2}\).

  • If \(X\) is smooth and proper and \(\mathcal{F}\) is lisse and pure of weight \(w\), every eigenvalue of \(\mathrm{Fr}\) on \(H^i(X, \mathcal{F})\) has absolute value \(q^{(w+i)/2}\).

These are theorems of Deligne [Del74], [Del80, Théorème 3.3.1]; there (i) is stated for mixed sheaves, those with a filtration whose quotients are pure. By a result of Laumon and Deligne’s dévissage, (i) follows from the case of a lisse sheaf on an open subset of \(\mathbb{A}^1\) [Zh, Remark 1.3], and that case is written out by the sign-only method in [Zh]. The proof below instead treats every dimension directly. It is that of [Ald], and as recorded in Section S1.1, no human has checked that text. Zhang states that the argument adapts to this setting, but he does not write it out [Zh, Remark 1.3].

Tag 22YG

Problem S2.1.1. For \(X = \mathbb{A}^d\) and \(\mathcal{F}= \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\), \(H^i_c(X, \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell) = 0\) for \(i \ne 2d\), and \(\mathrm{Fr}\) acts on the line \(H^{2d}_c(X, \overline{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)\) by \(q^d\). So (i) is attained for \(w = 0\), \(i = 2d\).

Let \(U\) be smooth and affine of pure dimension \(d \ge 1\) over \(\mathbb{F}_q\), and \(\mathcal{F}\) lisse on \(U\) with \(\lvert \iota\operatorname{Tr}(\mathrm{Fr}_u \mid \mathcal{F}_{\bar u}) \rvert \le C q^{nw/2}\) for all \(n\) and \(u \in U(\mathbb{F}_{q^n})\). Let \(\pi \colon U \to \mathbb{A}^d\) be finite (Noether normalization), \(B\) the number of generators of \(\mathcal O(U)\) as an \(\mathcal O(\mathbb{A}^d)\)-module, and \(z_1, \dots, z_s\) generators of the \(\mathbb{F}_q\)-algebra \(\mathcal O(U)\). Let \(b\) be a nondegenerate bilinear form on \(\mathbb{A}^d\), and for \(k \ge 2\), \(a = (a_{ij}) \in \mathbb{A}^{ks}\) and \(c \in \mathbb{A}^1\) let \(Y_{a,c} \subset U^k\) be the zero set of \[\sum_{i=1}^{k-1} b\bigl(\pi(u_i), \pi(u_{i+1})\bigr) + \sum_{i=1}^{k} \sum_{j=1}^{s} a_{ij}\, z_j(u_i) + c .\] This is the note’s “vectorize to go up dimensions” [Alp, footnote 3]. Let \(X := U^k\), \(D := kd\), \(\mathcal{F}_k := \mathcal{F}^{\boxtimes k}\), and, when \((a, c)\) has coordinates in \(\mathbb{F}_r\), \(r = q^e\), define \(M_n\), \(N_n\), \(R_n\) and \(K\) for \(Y = Y_{a,c}\) as in §S1.3, with sums over the points with values in \(\mathbb{F}_{r^n}\), and with \(H^{D-1}_c\) and \(H^{D+1}_c\) in the definition of \(K\).

Tag 22YH

Problem S2.1.2. Let \(E\) be a finite field with \(r\) elements, \(\psi\) a nontrivial additive character of \(E\), \(b_1, \dots, b_{k-1}\) nondegenerate bilinear forms on \(E^d\), and \(t_1, \dots, t_k \colon E^d \to \mathbb{C}\) bounded by \(T\). For \(h \in E^\times\) and \(c \in E\), \[\Bigl\lvert \sum_{x \in (E^d)^k} t_1(x_1) \cdots t_k(x_k)\, \psi\Bigl(h \sum_{i=1}^{k-1} b_i(x_i, x_{i+1}) + hc\Bigr) \Bigr\rvert \le T^k r^{d(k+1)/2}.\]

This is [Ald, Lemma 3].

Tag 22YP

Theorem S2.1.2 ([Ald, Theorem 9]). Every eigenvalue of \(\mathrm{Fr}\) on \(H^d_c(U, \mathcal{F})\) has absolute value at most \(q^{(w+d)/2}\).

Below degree \(d\) there is nothing to prove, since \(H^i_c(U, \mathcal{F}) = 0\) for \(i < d\). The degrees above \(d\) are reached by induction on the dimension.

§S2.2. Induction on the dimension Tag 22YQ

Tag 22YT

Problem S2.2.3. A bound \(\lvert \alpha \rvert \le q^{(w+i)/2}\) for the eigenvalues of \(\mathrm{Fr}\) on \(H^i_c(X, \mathcal{F})\) may be proved after replacing \(\mathbb{F}_q\) by \(\mathbb{F}_{q^e}\), with \(\mathrm{Fr}^e\) and \(q^e\) in place of \(\mathrm{Fr}\) and \(q\); weights at most \(w\) are preserved.

Tag 22YU

Tag 22YV

Tag 22YW

Problem S2.2.6. If \(\mathcal{F}\) is lisse of rank \(\rho\) on \(U\) with weights at most \(w\), then \(\lvert \iota\operatorname{Tr}(\mathrm{Fr}_u \mid \mathcal{F}_{\bar u}) \rvert \le \rho\, q^{nw/2}\) for all \(n\) and \(u \in U(\mathbb{F}_{q^n})\).

Tag 22YX

Problem S2.2.7. Let \(X\) be smooth and affine of pure dimension \(d \ge 1\), \(\mathcal{F}\) lisse, and \(i > d\). Let \(Y \subset X\) be a hyperplane section with property (WL) for \(\mathcal{F}\), defined over \(\mathbb{F}_q\) (after replacing \(\mathbb{F}_q\) by a finite extension). If the eigenvalues of \(\mathrm{Fr}\) on \(H^{i-2}_c(Y, \mathcal{F})\) have absolute value at most \(q^{(w+i-2)/2}\), then those on \(H^i_c(X, \mathcal{F})\) have absolute value at most \(q^{(w+i)/2}\).

Problems S2.2.2–S2.2.7 and Theorem S2.1.2, with Problem S1.3.4 applied to an embedding of \(X\) and to \(\mathcal{F}^\vee\) to produce the hyperplane sections of Problem S2.2.7, prove Theorem S2.1.1(i) by induction on \(\dim X\) [Ald].
