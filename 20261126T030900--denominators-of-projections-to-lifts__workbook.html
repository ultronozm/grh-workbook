§80.1. The statement

We use the data of Chapter 36: the CM field \(E\) with \(f\) infinite places, the rank \(r\), \(t = 5r\), the numbers \(m\) and \(k\), the level primes \(p_0, p_1\) with \(M = p_0p_1\), the level \(N\), the moduli \(q_1\) and \(d\), and the budget \(B\) of Definition 36.4.1. From Chapter 37 we use the chosen places \(\mathcal M\), labels, the good idempotent \(e_0\) and the integer \(\mathcal N\) (Section 37.1), the gap \(\Delta = 2r^8\) and the weights \(\kappa_j\) of the lifts (Section 37.2), tame characters \(\xi\) and twists \(\vartheta\) with their sets \(Q_\xi\), \(Q_\vartheta\) (Section 37.6), and the single lifts \(u_{\vartheta,j}\) on good forms (Section 37.9).

Fix a prime \(\ell\) and a ring isomorphism \(\iota \colon \mathbb{C}\simeq \bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\). As in Chapter 37, \(\operatorname{val}\) is the valuation of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\) with \(\operatorname{val}(\ell) = 1\) and \(\operatorname{val}(0) = \infty\), read through \(\iota\) on complex numbers, and \(\operatorname{val}_+ := \max(\operatorname{val}, 0)\). A form on a definite space is integral at \(\iota\) if its gauge (Definition 57.3.1), for \(\iota\) followed by \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell \subset \mathbb{C}_\ell\), is at most \(1\) at every finite adelic point. We use the choices of complex conjugation, tame generators and local embeddings fixed in the proof; they are called of the proof below. The field \(E\) is embedded in \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\), and \(G_E := \mathrm{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}/E)\); with \(c\) the complex conjugation of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\) fixed in the proof, \(g^c := cgc\) for \(g \in G_E\); and \(\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}\) is the \(\ell\)-adic cyclotomic character. For a place of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\) above a finite place \(w\) of \(E\) not dividing \(\ell\), \(s_w\) denotes its distinguished generator of tame inertia; it depends on the place of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\).

A coefficient ring \(\mathcal{O}\) is the ring of integers of a finite Galois extension of \(\mathbb{Q}_\ell\) in \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\), and a statement holds for all large \(\mathcal{O}\) if it holds for all coefficient rings containing a fixed one. The normalized length of an \(\mathcal{O}\)-module of finite length is its length divided by the ramification index \(e_{\mathcal{O}}\) of \(\mathcal{O}\) over \(\mathbb{Z}_\ell\); so \(\mathcal{O}/\ell\) has normalized length \(1\), and the normalized length does not change when \(\mathcal{O}\) is enlarged and the module extended. An \(\mathcal{O}\)-module of infinite length has normalized length \(+\infty\).

Definition 80.1.1. Let \(X_{\mathrm{odd}}\) be the positive integer of Theorem 27.3.2 for \(N = q_1d\), with \(X_{\mathrm{odd}} \le 2^{4(q_1d + t + 3)^{27}} h_E\) and \(h_E\) the class number of \(E\), and put \(n_\ell := \operatorname{val}_\ell(\mathcal N)\). Let \(\mathrm{Int}_b\) be the product of the integers \(i\) with \(\max(0, b - 30(\Delta/2)^3) < i \le b + 30(\Delta/2)^3\), for the weight scale \(b\), and put \[\mathrm{crys}_\ell := \operatorname{val}_\ell\bigl((2b)!\,\mathrm{Int}_b^{(\Delta/2)^3}\bigr), \qquad \mathrm{defect}_\ell := 10^6\,\frac mk\,\bigl(b\,\operatorname{val}_\ell(M) + \mathrm{crys}_\ell\bigr), \qquad \mathrm{loc}_\ell := 4f\,\mathrm{defect}_\ell ,\] \[\mathrm{tw}_\ell := (4f + 3)\,r\,n_\ell + 2^{-\ell}, \qquad \varepsilon_\ell := t\,\operatorname{val}_\ell(X_{\mathrm{odd}}) + 8ft\,n_\ell + 2n_\ell + \bigl((4t + 1)(\Delta/2)^{22} + \Delta/2\bigr)n_\ell .\] Define the clearing bound by \(\mathrm{cl}_\ell := \mathrm{loc}_\ell + (1 + 2t)\,\mathrm{tw}_\ell + \varepsilon_\ell + n_\ell\).

The terms of \(\mathrm{cl}_\ell\) pay for the following. \(\mathrm{loc}_\ell\): the crystalline condition above \(\ell\) (Chapter 82). \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\): the Selmer length of the twisted main summand and, with the factor \(2t\), the ramification of the twist (Problem 80.1.3). The four terms of \(\varepsilon_\ell\), in order: the odd classes and the class group (the section on lifts and the classes they produce in Clearing a projection: two classes); the places above \(M\) (Problem 83.4.3); the idempotent and the stabilizers; and the congruences between the characters \(\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}^{-e}\), \(0 \le e < t\) (the exercises on generators and general linear group orders in Clearing a projection: two classes; Definition 83.4.1). Problem 83.4.4 adds the four terms up. The last \(n_\ell\) is the cost of multiplying the clearing operator by \(\mathcal N\) to make its pairings integral. The character-separation term includes the cost of the tests for even classes at auxiliary places (Chapter 84).

Definition 80.1.2. A descent pair \(\mathfrak{O}\) has two finitely indexed families of finite places of \(E\), chosen places and twist places; all their entries are distinct, and none is the conjugate of any entry. It has a hermitian space \(W\) of rank \(r'\) with signs on its basis indices and a specified adelic frame (Section 37.3) in which its Gram matrix is the diagonal matrix of the signs at every infinite place and the identity at every finite place, a function from the basis indices of \(W\) to the natural numbers, an injection of those indices into the basis indices of the standard definite space \(V = I_{n'}\) with \(r' < n'\) (its image is the set of selected indices), column multiplicities \(\beta^\pm(\tau,i)\) at each infinite place \(\tau\) and height \(i \ge 0\) (the diagrams of the weight, as in Definition 37.2.1), and a continuous unitary character \(\mu\) of \(U(1)(\mathbb A)\), trivial on rational points, with specified integral weight at each infinite place and specified tame part, a character \(\psi_w\) of \(k_w^\times\) at each finite place \(w\), trivial off the two families. Write \(K_0\) for the stabilizer of the standard integral lattice of \(V\) in the finite adelic group. The level of \(\mathfrak{O}\) consists of the elements of \(K_0\) whose reductions fix the selected basis vectors at the chosen places and preserve the span of the other basis vectors at the twist places. Its level character is the product over the twist places \(w\) of \(\psi_w^{-1}\) applied to the determinant of the block on the selected indices. Its forms \(\mathcal A(\mathfrak{O})\) are the algebraic forms on \(V\) on which the level acts through the level character, of the weight with column multiplicities \(\beta^+(\tau,i)+\beta^-(\tau,n'-i)\) at \(\tau\) and each height \(0\le i<n'\), and with determinant exponent \(-\sum_{1\le i<n'} \beta^-(\tau,i)\) at \(\tau\). We write \(t' := n' - r'\) for its rank difference.

Definition 80.1.3. Let \(e_1\) be an element of the complex group algebra of \(\prod_w \mathrm{GL}_{r'}(k_w)\) over the chosen places of \(\mathfrak{O}\), and \(A \subseteq \mathcal A(\mathfrak{O})\) a subspace. A tuple \(g = (g_w)_w\) of this group acts on \(\mathcal A(\mathfrak{O})\) by right translation by an element of \(K_0\) that reduces to \(\operatorname{diag}(g_w,1)\), in the order selected indices then complementary indices, at each chosen place \(w\), and to the identity at the twist places, multiplied by \(\prod_w \psi_w(\det g_w)\); write \(\mathrm{im}(e_1)\) for the image of the operator by which \(e_1\) then acts. In this sheet \(\upsilon_E\) is the splitting character of the Weil data on the ideles of \(E\) (Section 32.4), and \(\upsilon\) is its restriction to the norm-one ideles, as in Section 37.7. For a continuous unitary character \(a\) of the norm-one ideles of \(E\) that is trivial on the rational points of \(U(1)\), and a natural number \(N_0\ge 0\), the Frobenius value of \(a\) in rank \(N_0\) at a finite place \(w\) is \(q_w^{N_0/2}\) times the value, at the finite idele with the chosen uniformizer at \(w\) and \(1\) elsewhere, of the Hecke character \(x \mapsto a(x/\bar x)\,\upsilon_E(x)^{-N_0}\); here \(q_w = \#k_w\). For a split place \(w\) such that neither \(w\) nor \(\bar w\) is a place of \(\mathfrak{O}\), with the Hecke operators \(T_{w,i}\), \(0 \le i \le n'\), of Section 32.4 acting on \(\mathcal A(\mathfrak{O})\), the Hecke polynomial operator at \(w\) at the value \(T \in \mathbb{C}\) is \(\sum_{i=0}^{n'} (-T)^i q_w^{i(i-1)/2}\, T_{w,i}\). The false lifts at \(w\) are the forms in \(\mathrm{im}(e_1) \cap A\) killed by the Hecke polynomial operator of \(\mathfrak{O}\) at \(w\) at the value \(\bigl(\nu_w q_w^{n'-r'-1}\bigr)^{-1}\) expected of a lift, where \(\nu_w\) is the Frobenius value at \(w\) of \(\upsilon^{r_-}\) in rank \(r'\), with \(r_-\) the number of negative signs of \(W\). A form is orthogonal to the false lifts for \((\mathfrak{O}, e_1, A)\) if it is orthogonal to the false lifts at every such \(w\).

Definition 80.1.4. Let \(e_1\) be as in Definition 80.1.3, \(\mathcal F \subseteq \mathcal A(\mathfrak{O})\) a subspace, with orthogonal projection \(\mathrm{pr}\), and \(\mathrm{den} \in \mathbb R\). The projection onto \(\mathcal F\) has denominator at most \(\mathrm{den}\) at \(\iota\) if some operator \(C_\ell\) on \(\mathcal F\) has \(\lvert \iota(\det C_\ell) \rvert_\ell \ge \exp(-\dim \mathcal F \cdot r' \cdot \mathrm{den})\) and, for every form \(y\) integral at \(\iota\) and orthogonal to the false lifts for \((\mathfrak{O}, e_1, \mathcal F^\perp)\), \(C_\ell(\mathrm{pr}(y))\) is integral at \(\iota\) and \(\lvert \iota\langle C_\ell(\mathrm{pr}(y)), y' \rangle \rvert_\ell \le 1\) for every form \(y'\) integral at every ring map \(\mathbb{C}\to \mathbb{C}_\ell\). Here \(\langle\,,\rangle\) is the class sum of the pointwise Gram inner products, linear in the first variable; each term is weighted by the reciprocal of the order of the stabilizer of its double-coset representative.

Used in: Theorem 80.1.1; Chapter 83: Problem 83.4.5

For a twist \(\vartheta\) and \(1 \le j \le m\), take the \(j\)-th descent pair of \(\vartheta\), \(e_1 = e_0\) and \(\mathcal F\) the image of \(u_{\vartheta,j}\). Then the false lifts are those of Definition 75.3.2, and the condition of Definition 80.1.4, without the bound on the pairings \(\langle C_\ell(\mathrm{pr}(y)), y'\rangle\), says that \(\mathrm{den}\) clears the projection at \((\iota, \vartheta, j)\) in the sense of Definition 75.3.4.

Definition 80.1.5. The inverse avatar of a tame character \(\xi\) is the \(\ell\)-adic avatar \(\hat\xi \colon G_E \to \bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell^\times\), through \(\iota\), of the base change of \(\xi^{-1}\) to \(E\): the continuous character with \(\hat\xi(\mathrm{Art}\,x) = \iota\bigl(\xi(x/\bar x)\bigr)^{-1}\) for every finite idele \(x\) of \(E\), where \(\mathrm{Art}\) is the Artin map. We use the Artin map of the proof, with values in the topological abelianization \(G_E^{\mathrm{ab}}\) and with a local uniformizer mapping to geometric Frobenius modulo inertia. A character of \(G_E\) is evaluated there through the induced character. Let \(\mathrm{ram} \in [0, \infty]\). A twist \(\vartheta\) is auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{ram}\) (at \(\iota\)) if:

  1. every place of \(Q_\vartheta\) has prime norm, and neither it nor its conjugate divides \(\ell MN\) (its conjugate is not in \(Q_\vartheta\), as for every tame character);

  2. \(\hat\vartheta\) is locally constant, and there is a character \(\chi_0\) of the finite ideles of \(E\), with values in \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell^\times\), such that \(\hat\vartheta(\mathrm{Art}\,x) = \chi_0(x/\bar x)\) for every finite idele \(x\), and \(\chi_0\) is trivial on \(E^\times\) and on every idele that is a unit at the places not above \(\ell\) and is congruent to \(1\) at the places of \(Q_\vartheta\);

  3. for some choice of a place of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\) above each \(w \in Q_\vartheta\), with tame generators \(s_w\), \(\sum_{w \in Q_\vartheta} \operatorname{val}_+\bigl(\hat\vartheta(s_w) - 1\bigr) \le \mathrm{ram}\).

Definition 80.1.6. Let \(r'\) and \(t'\) be natural numbers. A family \(\mathrm{HT}= (\mathrm{HT}_\sigma)\) of finite multisets of integers, one for each embedding \(\sigma \colon E \to \mathbb{C}\), is admissible for \((r', t')\) if:

  1. \(1 \le r' \le r\) and \(t' \le t\);

  2. there is one \(0 \le j \le m\) such that, for every \(\sigma\), every \(u \in \mathrm{HT}_\sigma\) satisfies \(\Delta/4 \le \lvert u\rvert \le 2m\Delta\) or \(\lvert s_\sigma u + \kappa_j([\sigma])\rvert \le t\); here \([\sigma]\) is the infinite place of \(\sigma\), and \(s_\sigma = \pm 1\) is the sign of \(\sigma\) for the CM type, that is, \(\sigma(\sqrt{-d}) = s_\sigma\sqrt{-d}\);

  3. \(k \cdot \#\{u \in \mathrm{HT}_\sigma : \lvert u\rvert \le 2(\Delta/2)^2\} \le r'm\) for every \(\sigma\), the members counted with multiplicity;

  4. \(\mathrm{HT}_\sigma\) and \(\mathrm{HT}_{\bar\sigma}\) together have at most \(r'\) negative members, for every \(\sigma\).

The members of \(\mathrm{HT}_\sigma\) are called jumps.

Definition 80.1.7. Let \(t' \ge 0\) be an integer, \(\mathrm{HT}\) a family as in Definition 80.1.6, and \(R \colon G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_{r'}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)\) a homomorphism. A lattice of \(R\) over \(\mathcal{O}\) is a homomorphism \(G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_{r'}(\mathcal{O})\) conjugate to \(R\) by an element of \(\mathrm{GL}_{r'}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell)\). The representation \(R\) is main-summand-like for \((t', \mathrm{HT})\) if:

  1. \(R\) is continuous, the trace pairing on the span of its image is nondegenerate, and \(\mathrm{tr}\,R(g^c) = \chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(g)^{-\max(t'-1,0)}\,\mathrm{tr}\,R(g^{-1})\) for every \(g \in G_E\);

  2. at every place \(A\) of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\) above every finite place \(w\) of \(E\): if \(w \nmid \ell\), \(R\) is trivial on the elements of the inertia group in a neighbourhood of \(1\); if \(w \mid \ell\), the restriction of \(R\) to the decomposition group is potentially crystalline for every local datum at \(A\), that is, every embedding \(e\) of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\) in \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\) inducing \(A\), with the induced embedding of its decomposition group into \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell/\mathbb{Q}_\ell)\), and for every such datum and every \(g \in \mathrm{Gal}(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell/\mathbb{Q}_\ell)\) its Hodge–Tate jumps at the coefficient embedding induced by \(g\) are, with multiplicity, the multiset \(\mathrm{HT}_\sigma\) for \(\sigma = \iota^{-1}\circ g^{-1}\circ e|_E\); and if \(w \nmid M\), \(R\) is unramified (\(w \nmid \ell\)), respectively crystalline for every local datum (\(w \mid \ell\));

  3. \(\mathrm{HT}\) is admissible for \((r', t')\);

  4. at every place of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\) above every \(w \mid M\) with \(w \nmid \ell\) there are an element \(\delta\) of the inertia group and a natural number \(n_w\) prime to the residue characteristic such that \(R(s^{n_w}) = 1\) for every inertia element \(s\) there, \(\delta\) is the tame generator \(s_w\) if \(w \in \mathcal M\), and \(\operatorname{val}_+\det(R(\delta) - 1) \le r'n_\ell\), \(\operatorname{val}_+\det\bigl(R((\delta^c)^{-1}) - 1\bigr) \le r'n_\ell\);

  6. some element of an inertia group above a place dividing \(M\) and not dividing \(\ell\) acts through \(R\) by a scalar \(y\) with \(\lvert y^{\ell^a j} - 1\rvert_\ell = 1\) for all integers \(a \ge 0\) and \(1 \le j \le 4r' + 2\).

Definition 80.1.10. Definition 80.1.11. Let \(\vartheta\) be a twist, \(\mathfrak{O}\) a descent pair, \(e_1\) as in Definition 80.1.3, \(\mathcal F \subseteq \mathcal A(\mathfrak{O})\) a subspace and \(\mathrm{sel} \in [0, \infty]\). A lift generator for \((\vartheta, \mathfrak{O}, e_1, \mathcal F)\) with Selmer bound \(\mathrm{sel}\) is a form \(y \in \mathcal F\) such that:

  1. \(y\) lies in the range of the idempotent of a tuple of polynomials with which \(\mathfrak{O}\) is in lift setting for \(\vartheta\) (Definition 80.1.10), and the whole range of this idempotent is contained in \(\mathrm{im}(e_1)\);

  2. \(y\) lifts a family \(\mathfrak s\) for which there are a descent pair \(\mathfrak{O}_0\) with the same basis index sets for \(W\) and for \(V\) as \(\mathfrak{O}\), whose character \(\mu\) times \(\vartheta\) is that of \(\mathfrak{O}\), and a main summand \(R\) for \(\mathfrak{O}_0\) and \(\mathfrak s\) such that \(\hat\vartheta R\) has Selmer bound \(\mathrm{sel}\) (Definition 80.1.9).

Theorem 80.1.1. Let \(\ell\) and \(\iota\) be as above, and let \(\vartheta\) be a twist that is auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\). Let \(\mathfrak{O}\) be a descent pair of ranks \(r' < n'\), \(e_1\) an element of the complex group algebra of \(\prod_w \mathrm{GL}_{r'}(k_w)\) over its chosen places, and \(\mathcal F \subseteq \mathcal A(\mathfrak{O})\) a subspace contained in the span of the lift generators for \((\vartheta, \mathfrak{O}, e_1, \mathcal F)\) with Selmer bound \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\). Then the projection onto \(\mathcal F\) has denominator at most \(\mathrm{cl}_\ell \log \ell\) at \(\iota\), in the sense of Definition 80.1.4.
