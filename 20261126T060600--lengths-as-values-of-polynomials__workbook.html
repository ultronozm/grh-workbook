Prerequisites.

Modules over a discrete valuation ring: length, elementary divisors [La, III, §7]; cocycles of a profinite group [SLF, Ch. VII, §3 and Ch. X, §3]; inertia, tame inertia and Frobenius elements at a finite place [SLF, Ch. I, §8 and Ch. IV, §§1–2]. Chapter 86: the Gauss valuation \(v_\rho\) and Problems 86.1.1, 86.1.2 and 86.1.3. Section 80.1: \(G_E\), \(g^c\), \(\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}\), the tame generators \(s_w\), coefficient rings \(\mathcal{O}\) and normalized lengths; Definition 80.1.5 (the inverse avatar \(\hat\xi\)). Tame characters, twists, critical pairs and their ratios (Section 37.6, Definition 37.10.1). As background: Stickelberger’s theorem (Theorem 26.2.1) and Selmer classes (Chapter 30).

The twisting theorem of Chapter 85 asks for one twist that bounds the lengths of many Selmer modules and the positive parts of the \(\ell\)-adic valuations of many ratios of Hecke values. On the Galois side one constructs a character \(\chi_\zeta\) from roots of unity \(\zeta_w\) at auxiliary places \(w\) (Theorem 85.1.1). Why should the length of a Selmer module of the twist by \(\chi_\zeta\) be bounded by the valuation of one polynomial at the point \((\zeta_w - 1)_w\), and why should that polynomial have small valuation somewhere? This sheet develops the answers and states Theorem 87.6.1, the polynomial input of Section 85.1. Corollary 86.2.2 of Chapter 86 then gives one tuple of roots of unity that controls the polynomial values for the first \(m\) powers of the twist.

Throughout, \(\ell\) is a prime, \(\operatorname{val}\) is the valuation of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell\) with \(\operatorname{val}(\ell) = 1\), \(\bar{\mathbb{Z}}_\ell\) is its valuation ring, \(\operatorname{val}_+ := \max(\operatorname{val}, 0)\), and \(\operatorname{val}_\ell\) is the \(\ell\)-adic valuation of integers. A discrete valuation ring \(\mathcal{O}'\) has the valuation \(\mathrm{ord}_{\mathcal{O}'}\) with value \(1\) at a uniformizer; for a coefficient ring, \(\operatorname{val}= \mathrm{ord}_{\mathcal{O}'}/e_{\mathcal{O}'}\) on \(\mathcal{O}'\). A cocycle for a group \(G\) acting linearly on a module \(B\) is a map \(z \colon G \to B\) with \(z(gh) = z(g) + g\,z(h)\). In this sheet \(\rho\) is a rational radius, and for \(g \in G_E\) we write \(g^{+} := g\) and \(g^{-} := (g^c)^{-1}\).

§87.1. Lengths and minors

Problem 87.1.1. Let \(\ell = 3\) and \(X \in \mathbb{Z}_3\). The solutions \(u \in \mathbb{Z}/9\) of \(Xu = 0\) number \(9, 3, 9, 1\) for \(X = 0, 3, 9, 1\), and form a module of length \(\min(2, \operatorname{val}(X))\). The solutions \((u, u') \in (\mathbb{Z}/9)^2\) of \(Xu + u' = 0\), \(Xu' = 0\) number \(9, 9, 1\) for \(X = 0, 3, 1\), and form a module of length \(\min(2, 2\operatorname{val}(X))\).

Used in: Problem 87.1.2

Problem 87.1.2. Let \(\mathcal{O}'\) be a discrete valuation ring, \(0 \ne a \in \mathcal{O}'\), and \(A\) a matrix over \(\mathcal{O}'\) with \(n\) columns and any set of rows. Let \(A_a\) be \(A\) with the \(n\) rows of \(a I_n\) added. Then the length of the \(\mathcal{O}'\)-module \(\{u \in (\mathcal{O}'/a)^n : Au = 0\}\) is the least \(\mathrm{ord}_{\mathcal{O}'}(P)\), for \(P\) running over the \(n \times n\) minors of \(A_a\). For the two systems of Problem 87.1.1, with \(a = 9\), the sets of minors are \(\{X, 9\}\) and, up to sign, \(\{X^2, 9, 9X, 0, 81\}\).

Problem 87.1.3. Let \(\mathcal{O}\) be a coefficient ring, \(Q\) a finite set, \(A\) a matrix with \(n\) columns and entries in \(\mathcal{O}[X_w : w \in Q]\), \(0 \ne a \in \mathcal{O}\), and \(\mathcal D\) the set of \(n \times n\) minors of \(A_a\); they are polynomials. For a point \(x\) with coordinates in a coefficient ring \(\mathcal{O}' \supseteq \mathcal{O}\) let \(Z(x) := \{u \in (\mathcal{O}'/a)^n : A(x)u = 0\}\). (a) The normalized length of \(Z(x)\) is \(\min_{P \in \mathcal D} \operatorname{val}(P(x))\), and it is at most \(n \operatorname{val}(a)\). (b) Every \(P \in \mathcal D\) bounds the normalized length of \(Z(x)\) by \(\operatorname{val}(P(x))\) at every point \(x\); we say \(P\) is sharp at \(x_0\) if equality holds there. A minor sharp at one point therefore computes the length there and bounds it everywhere. (c) For \(\ell = 3\), \(Xu = 0\) and \(a = 9\): the minor \(9\) is sharp at \(X = 0\) and the minor \(X\) at \(X = 3\); the latter bounds the length at \(X = \zeta - 1\), \(\zeta\) of order \(9\), by \(1/6\).

§87.2. A character with variables

Fix a CM field \(E \subseteq \bar{\mathbb{Q}}\), a finite set \(\Sigma_0\) of finite places of \(E\) containing those above \(\ell\), and an element \(g_0\) of inertia at a place of \(\Sigma_0\) prime to \(\ell\).

Definition 87.2.1. A finite set \(Q\) of finite places of \(E\) is admissible for \(\Sigma_0\) if its places have prime norm, and \(Q\), its conjugate \(\bar Q\) and \(\Sigma_0\) are pairwise disjoint. A character for \(Q\) is a locally constant character \(\chi\colon G_E\to\bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell^\times\) for which there is a character \(\chi_0\) of the finite ideles of \(E\), trivial on the principal ideles and on every idele that is a unit at the places not above \(\ell\) and congruent to \(1\) at the places of \(Q\) not above \(\ell\), with \(\chi(\mathrm{Art}\,y)=\chi_0(y/\bar y)\) for every finite idele \(y\); here \(\mathrm{Art}\) is the Artin map of Definition 80.1.5. The same definition is used for homomorphisms to the additive group \(\mathbb{Z}/\ell^{h}\).

Let \(h_1 \ge 1\) and, for each \(w \in Q\), let \(\lg_w \colon G_E \to \mathbb{Z}/\ell^{h_1}\) be a locally constant homomorphism; write \([n] \in \{0, \dots, \ell^{h_1} - 1\}\) for the representative of \(n \in \mathbb{Z}/\ell^{h_1}\). For \(\ell^{h_1}\)-th roots of unity \(\zeta_w\) and for variables \(X_w\) put \[\chi_\zeta(g) := \prod_{w \in Q} \zeta_w^{[\lg_w(g)]}, \qquad \chi_X(g) := \prod_{w \in Q} (1 + X_w)^{[\lg_w(g)]} \in \mathbb{Z}[X_w : w \in Q] .\]

Used in: Definition 87.3.4; Chapter 85: Theorem 85.1.1; Chapter 88; Chapter 89

Problem 87.2.1. (a) At \(X_w = \zeta_w - 1\) the polynomial \(\chi_X(g)\) takes the value \(\chi_\zeta(g)\); \(\chi_\zeta\) is a character of \(G_E\), and \(\chi_\zeta^n = \chi_{\zeta^n}\) for \(n \ge 1\), where \(\zeta^n := (\zeta_w^n)_w\). (b) For \(g, h \in G_E\), \(\chi_X(gh) - \chi_X(g)\chi_X(h)\) lies in the ideal generated by the \((1 + X_w)^{\ell^{h_1}} - 1\), \(w \in Q\).

The next problem is a model with a finite group in place of \(G_E\): \(s\) plays a tame generator at an auxiliary place, acting through \(1 + X\); \(g_1\) plays a Frobenius element there, acting trivially; \(\gamma\) plays an inertia element acting by a scalar.

Problem 87.2.2. Let \(\ell = 5\), and let \(G\) be the group of order \(100\) generated by \(g_1, \gamma, s\) with \(g_1^5 = \gamma^4 = s^5 = 1\), \(\gamma g_1 \gamma^{-1} = g_1^2\) and \(s\) central. Let \(\zeta_5\) be a root of unity of order \(5\), \(\mathcal{O}' = \mathbb{Z}_5[\zeta_5]\) and \(B = \mathcal{O}'/5\), and let \(i \in \mathbb{Z}_5\) be the fourth root of unity with \(i \equiv 2 \pmod 5\). For \(0 \le j \le 4\) let \(G\) act on \(B\) by the scalars \(g_1 \mapsto 1\), \(\gamma \mapsto i\), \(s \mapsto \zeta_5^j\). The cocycles \(z \colon G \to B\) with \(z(\gamma) = 0\) are determined by \(u = z(g_1)\), and they are the \(u\) with \((\zeta_5^j - 1)u = 0\). They number \(625, 5, 5, 5, 5\) for \(j = 0, \dots, 4\); the normalized length is \(\min(1, \operatorname{val}(\zeta_5^j - 1)) = 1, \tfrac14, \tfrac14, \tfrac14, \tfrac14\). With \(1 + X\) for the scalar of \(s\), the minors of Problem 87.1.3 for the equation \(Xu = 0\) on \(B\) are \(X\) and \(5\).

Problem 87.2.3. Let a group \(G\) act linearly on a module \(B\) over a commutative ring, and let \(\gamma \in G\) act by a scalar \(\lambda\) with \(\lambda - 1\) invertible. Every cocycle \(G \to B\) is, in exactly one way, the sum of a coboundary \(g \mapsto gb - b\) and a cocycle vanishing at \(\gamma\).

The finite presentation. Let \(\mathcal{O}\) be a coefficient ring, \(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\colon G_E \to \mathrm{GL}_{r'}(\mathcal{O})\) a continuous homomorphism (for instance the action on a Selmer datum), \(c\) a nonnegative integer, \(S\) a finite set of finite places, and \(\mathcal L\) a set of relations \(\sum_j a_j z(g_j) = 0\) with finitely many \(g_j \in G_E\) and matrices \(a_j\) over \(\mathcal{O}\). For a point \(x\) with coordinates in a commutative ring \(\mathcal{O}'\) with a ring map from \(\mathcal{O}\), let \(\mathrm{Sel}(x)\) be the \(\mathcal{O}'\)-module of locally constant maps \(z \colon G_E \to (\mathcal{O}'/\ell^{c})^{r'}\) with \(z(gh) = z(g) + \chi_x(g)T_{\mathrm{Sel}}(g)z(h)\) that vanish on the inertia groups at the places outside \(S\) and satisfy the relations of \(\mathcal L\), with coefficients mapped to \(\mathcal{O}'\); here \(\chi_x(g)\) is the value of \(\chi_X(g)\) at \(x\). (These are equations; \(\chi_x\) need not be a character.) There is a matrix \(A\) over \(\mathcal{O}[X_w : w \in Q]\) with finitely many columns and possibly infinitely many rows such that, for every such \(\mathcal{O}'\) and \(x\), \(\mathrm{Sel}(x)\) is isomorphic to the kernel of \(A(x)\) on \((\mathcal{O}'/\ell^{c})^{n}\), \(n\) the number of columns. Hence there is one set of polynomials over \(\mathcal{O}\) such that for every discrete valuation ring \(\mathcal{O}'\) of characteristic zero with a ring map from \(\mathcal{O}\), and every \(x\), the \(\mathcal{O}'\)-length of \(\mathrm{Sel}(x)\) is the least \(\mathrm{ord}_{\mathcal{O}'}(P(x))\), \(P\) in the set; for coefficient rings \(\mathcal{O}' \supseteq \mathcal{O}\) the normalized length is the least \(\operatorname{val}(P(x))\). Status: read from the proof [GL], in the case of a coefficient ring and a continuous representation. The columns are indexed by the pairs of an element of a finite continuous quotient of \(G_E\) and a coordinate of \((\mathcal{O}'/\ell^c)^{r'}\); the quotient is the same for every \(\mathcal{O}'\) and \(x\), each \(\lg_w\) and the reduction of \(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\) modulo \(\ell^c\) are trivial on its kernel, and every \(z \in \mathrm{Sel}(x)\) vanishes there. That it exists rests on the following fact. For a finite discrete \(G_E\)-module with continuous action, only finitely many locally constant cocycles vanish on every inertia group at the places outside a fixed finite set. Status: classical, by the theorem of Hermite and Minkowski [Ne, Ch. III, §2].

At a point \(x = (\zeta_w - 1)_w\) the \(z\) are cocycles for the twist of \(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\) by the character \(\chi_\zeta\), by Problem 87.2.1. The Selmer modules of Definition 87.3.2 below have coefficients in \(\mathrm{Frac}(\mathcal{O}')/\mathcal{O}'\); their elements take finitely many values, so each is killed by a power of \(\ell\), and those killed by \(\ell^{c}\) form the module with coefficients in \(\ell^{-c}\mathcal{O}'/\mathcal{O}' \cong \mathcal{O}'/\ell^{c}\). The next problem compares the two.

Problem 87.2.4. Let \(\mathcal{O}'\) be a coefficient ring, \(c \ge 1\) an integer, and \(Z\) an \(\mathcal{O}'\)-module in which every element is killed by a power of \(\ell\). If the submodule \(Z[\ell^c]\) of elements killed by \(\ell^c\) has normalized length less than \(c\), then \(Z = Z[\ell^c]\). For \(\ell = 3\), \(Z = \mathbb{Z}/27\) over \(\mathbb{Z}_3\) and \(c = 2\): \(Z[9]\) has \(9\) elements and length \(2\), and \(Z \ne Z[9]\).

§87.3. Selmer data and tied primes

Definition 87.3.1. Let \(\mathcal{O}\) be a coefficient ring. A Selmer datum for \((\Sigma_0, g_0)\) over \(\mathcal{O}\) is a free \(\mathcal{O}\)-module \(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\) of finite rank \(\operatorname{rk}T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\ge 1\) with a continuous \(\mathcal{O}\)-linear action of \(G_E\), unramified outside a set \(\Sigma(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}) \subseteq \Sigma_0\) containing the places above \(\ell\); write \(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}(g)\) for the action of \(g\). The endomorphisms \(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}(g)\), \(g \in G_E\), span \(\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal{O}}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\) over \(\mathcal{O}\). For each finite place \(w\) of \(E\) fix a place of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\) above \(w\), and write \(G_w\) for its decomposition group. For each \(w \mid \ell\) the datum supplies a finitely generated \(\mathcal{O}\)-module \(\mathcal Z_w\) of cocycles \(G_w \to T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\) containing the coboundaries, with \(\sum_{w \mid \ell}(\operatorname{rk}\mathcal Z_w - \operatorname{rk}T_{\mathrm{Sel}}) \le f_E \operatorname{rk}T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\), where \(f_E\) is the number of infinite places of \(E\). The local relations at \(w\) are the relations \(\sum_j a_j z(g_j) = 0\), with finitely many \(g_j \in G_w\) and \(a_j \in \operatorname{End}_{\mathcal{O}}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\), satisfied by all \(z \in \mathcal Z_w\). The datum also supplies an element \(\gamma(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\) of inertia at a place of \(\Sigma(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\) prime to \(\ell\) and a scalar \(\lambda \in \mathcal{O}\) with \(\lambda^2 - 1 \in \mathcal{O}^\times\); both \(\gamma(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\) and \((\gamma(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})^c)^{-1}\) act on \(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\) by \(\lambda\).

A datum has three terms, in the nonnegative extended integers. For \(0 \ne a \in \mathcal{O}\) write \(\bar T_a := T_{\mathrm{Sel}}/aT_{\mathrm{Sel}}\), and \(\bar T_a^{*c}\) for the additive dual \(\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb{Z}}(\bar T_a, \mathbb{Q}/\mathbb{Z})\), on which \(\mathcal{O}\) acts by precomposition and \(g \in G_E\) acts by \(y \mapsto y \circ \bigl(\chi_{\mathrm{cyc}}(g^c)\,T_{\mathrm{Sel}}((g^c)^{-1})\bigr)\). Define the global term \(\mathrm{glob}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\) as the supremum, over \(0 \ne a \in \mathcal{O}\), of the \(\mathcal{O}\)-length of the module of \(\mathcal{O}\)-linear \(G_E\)-equivariant maps \(\bar T_a \to \bar T_a^{*c}\). At a place \(v \in \Sigma(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\) prime to \(\ell\), the local term is the supremum over the same \(a\) of the excess, taken to be \(0\) if negative, of the length of the module of restrictions to \(G_v\) of the locally constant cocycles \(G_E \to \bar T_a\) over the length of \(\bar T_a\); \(\mathrm{plc}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\) is the sum of these local terms over the places \(v \in \Sigma(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\) prime to \(\ell\). The third term is \(\mathrm{inr}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}) := \sum_g \mathrm{ord}_{\mathcal{O}}\det(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}(g) - 1)\), with one summand for each of \(g = g_0^{+}\) and \(g = g_0^{-}\) (two summands even if they coincide). For a datum, \(\mathrm{glob}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\) and \(\mathrm{plc}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}) + \mathrm{inr}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\) are finite. Put \[\mathrm{bd}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}) := \bigl(\mathrm{glob}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}) + \mathrm{plc}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}) + \mathrm{inr}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\bigr)/e_{\mathcal{O}} .\]

Definition 87.3.2. For a Selmer datum \(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\) over \(\mathcal{O}\), a finite set \(Q\) of finite places and a character \(\chi\colon G_E \to \bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell^\times\), an \(\mathcal{O}'\)-valued model of \(\chi\) is a homomorphism \(\chi' \colon G_E \to \mathcal{O}'^\times\) with the same values, and the extended twisted lattice is \(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\otimes_{\mathcal{O}} \mathcal{O}'\) with \(g\) acting by \(\chi'(g)T_{\mathrm{Sel}}(g)\). A bound \(\mathrm{len}\,\mathrm{Sel}_Q(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\chi) \le d\) means that for every sufficiently large coefficient ring \(\mathcal{O}' \supseteq \mathcal{O}\) there is an \(\mathcal{O}'\)-valued model of \(\chi\) for which the following module has normalized length at most \(d\): the locally constant cocycles with coefficients in the extended twisted lattice tensored over \(\mathcal{O}'\) with \(\mathrm{Frac}(\mathcal{O}')/\mathcal{O}'\) that are unramified outside \(\Sigma(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}) \cup Q \cup \bar Q\), satisfy the local relations with coefficients extended to \(\mathcal{O}'\), and vanish at \(\gamma(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\).

Used in: Chapter 85; Chapter 90: Problem 90.4.2

Definition 87.3.3. A natural number \(c_{\mathrm{tor}}\) is a torsion level for finitely many Selmer data over one coefficient ring \(\mathcal{O}\) if, for every datum, \(\mathrm{bd}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}) + 1 < c_{\mathrm{tor}}\) and \(2\operatorname{rk}T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\,\mathrm{ord}_{\mathcal{O}}(\ell^{c_{\mathrm{tor}}}) + 1 < \#k_{\mathcal{O}}\), where \(k_{\mathcal{O}}\) is the residue field.

Used in: Chapter 85; Chapter 90

Definition 87.3.4. Let \(T_{\mathrm{Sel},1}, \dots, T_{\mathrm{Sel},n}\) be Selmer data for \((\Sigma_0, g_0)\) over \(\mathcal{O}\), let \(c \ge 0\) and \(h_1 \ge 1\) be integers and \(W \subseteq G_E\). Tied primes at level \(\ell^{h_1}\), for \(c\) and \(W\), consist of a set \(Q\) admissible for \(\Sigma_0\) with a first prime \(w_* \in Q\); for each \(w \in Q\) a place of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\) above \(w\), an arithmetic Frobenius element \(\mathrm{Fr}_w \in G_E\) and the tame generator \(s_w\) at that place, taken in its inertia group, and a homomorphism \(\lg_w \colon G_E \to \mathbb{Z}/\ell^{h_1}\); and slopes \(\mathrm{sl}_i(w) \in \mathcal{O}\) for \(w \in Q\) and \(1 \le i \le n\); such that:

  1. for every \(w \in Q\): \(\#k_w \equiv 1 \pmod{\ell^{h_1}}\), where \(k_w\) is the residue field at \(w\), \(\lg_w\) is a character for \(\{w\}\) in the sense of Definition 87.2.1, and \(\lg_w(s_w) = 1\);

  2. for \(w \in Q\) with \(w \ne w_*\) and for \(w' \in Q\): \(\lg_{w'}(\mathrm{Fr}_w)\) is \(1\) if \(w' = w_*\) and \(0\) otherwise;

  3. \(\mathrm{Fr}_{w_*} \in W\); \(\mathrm{sl}_i(w_*) = 0\) for all \(i\); and no place of \(\Sigma_0\), and no place of \(Q\) other than \(w_*\), lies above the rational prime \(\#k_{w_*}\);

  4. if \(\mathrm{sl}_i(w) \ne 0\), then \(T_{\mathrm{Sel},i}(\mathrm{Fr}_w^{+})\) and \(T_{\mathrm{Sel},i}(\mathrm{Fr}_w^{-})\) are the identity modulo \(\ell^{c}\).

For \(Q' \subseteq Q\) and \(0 \ne a \in \mathcal{O}\), the module of \(T_{\mathrm{Sel},i}\) tied at \(Q'\) at level \(a\) consists of the locally constant cocycles \(z \colon G_E \to T_{\mathrm{Sel},i}/aT_{\mathrm{Sel},i}\) that vanish at \(\gamma(T_{\mathrm{Sel},i})\), are unramified outside \(\Sigma(T_{\mathrm{Sel},i}) \cup Q \cup \bar Q\), satisfy the local relations of \(T_{\mathrm{Sel},i}\), and satisfy the ties \(z(s_w^{\pm}) = \mathrm{sl}_i(w)\, z(\mathrm{Fr}_w^{\pm})\) for \(w \in Q'\) and both signs. Call the module obtained by taking \(Q' = Q\) the tied module.

Bounded tied primes. Let \(E \subseteq \bar{\mathbb{Q}}\) be a CM field, \(\Sigma_0\) a finite set of finite places of \(E\), and \(g_0\) an element of inertia at a place of \(\Sigma_0\) prime to \(\ell\). Let \(T_{\mathrm{Sel},1}, \dots, T_{\mathrm{Sel},n}\) be Selmer data for \((\Sigma_0, g_0)\) over one coefficient ring \(\mathcal{O}\), let \(c\) be a nonnegative integer with \(2\operatorname{rk}T_{\mathrm{Sel},i}\,\mathrm{ord}_{\mathcal{O}}(\ell^{c}) + 1 < \#k_{\mathcal{O}}\) for all \(i\), and let \(W\) be a neighbourhood of \(g_0\) in \(G_E\). There is a nonnegative integer \(n_Q\) such that for every integer \(h_1 > c\) there are tied primes at level \(\ell^{h_1}\), for \(c\) and \(W\), with \(\#Q \le n_Q\), for which the tied module of each \(T_{\mathrm{Sel},i}\) at level \(\ell^{c}\) has \(\mathcal{O}\)-length at most \(\mathrm{glob}(T_{\mathrm{Sel},i}) + \mathrm{plc}(T_{\mathrm{Sel},i})\). Status: the coefficient-ring case of a result read from the proof [GL]; not proved here. Theorem 88.4.1 of Chapter 88 treats the case in which \(\Sigma_0\) contains the places above \(\ell\).

§87.4. Untying along a line

In the tied module the value of a cocycle at each tame generator is given by its value at a Frobenius element, and the term \(\mathrm{inr}\) is missing from the bound. The twists we want are not tied. The ties are removed along a line through the origin of the space of the \(X_w\). In the construction below, \(\mathrm{Fr}_{w_*}^{+}\) and \(\mathrm{Fr}_{w_*}^{-}\) act modulo \(\ell^{c_{\mathrm{tor}}}\) as \(g_0^{+}\) and \(g_0^{-}\); at the origin, releasing the two ties at \(w_*\) then increases the \(\mathcal{O}\)-length by at most \(\mathrm{inr}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\). At the levels and radii specified below, the increase in length from the other primes is bounded by the valuation of a product of determinants evaluated on the line, and at a suitable point the normalized valuation of this product equals its Gauss valuation.

Problem 87.4.1. Let \(0 < \rho \le 1\), \(h\) a nonnegative integer, and \(\alpha \in \bar{\mathbb{Z}}_\ell\) with \(\operatorname{val}(\alpha - 1) \ge \rho\). Then \(\operatorname{val}\bigl(\sum_{0 \le j < \ell^{h}} \alpha^j\bigr) \ge h\rho\); for \(\ell = 3\), \(\alpha = 4\), \(h = 2\) the sum is \(87381 = 9 \cdot 9709\). Hence, let \(c\) be a nonnegative integer with \(h_1\rho \ge c\), and let \(x\) have coordinates in a coefficient ring \(\mathcal{O}'\) with \(\operatorname{val}(x_w) \ge \rho\) for every \(w \in Q\). Modulo \(\ell^c\mathcal{O}'\), one has \((1 + x_w)^{\ell^{h_1}} \equiv 1\) for every \(w \in Q\) and \(\chi_x(gu) \equiv \chi_x(g)\chi_x(u)\) for all \(g, u \in G_E\).

In the next two problems \(u\) and \(u'\) stand for the values of a cocycle at a tame generator \(s\) and at a Frobenius element \(\varphi\).

Problem 87.4.2. Modulo \(9\), with \(y = 3\): the pairs \((u, u') \in (\mathbb{Z}/9)^2\) with \(\bigl((1 + y) - 1\bigr)(u - 2u') = 0\) number \(27\), and those with the tie \(u = 2u'\) number \(9\). The lengths differ by \(1 = \operatorname{val}_3\bigl((1 + y) - 1\bigr)\).

Problem 87.4.3. Let a group \(G\) act linearly on a module \(B\) over a commutative ring \(\Lambda\), and let \(z \colon G \to B\) be a cocycle. Let \(s, \varphi \in G\) with \(\varphi s \varphi^{-1} = s^{q}\) or \(\varphi^{-1} s \varphi = s^{q}\), where \(h\) is a nonnegative integer and \(q\) a positive integer with \(q \equiv 1 \pmod{\ell^{h}}\). Let \(s\) act by a scalar \(\Gamma \in \Lambda\) with \(\sum_{0 \le j < \ell^{h}} \Gamma^j = 0\) on \(B\), and let \(\varphi\) act by \(\Phi\). (a) \(z(s^{q}) = z(s)\), and \((\Phi - 1)\,z(s) = (\Gamma - 1)\,z(\varphi)\). (b) If \(\Gamma = 1 + \sigma y\) and \(\Phi = (1 + y)A\) with \(\sigma, y \in \Lambda\) and a \(\Lambda\)-linear \(A\) such that \(\sigma(A - 1) = 0\) on \(B\), then \(\bigl((1 + y)A - 1\bigr)\bigl(z(s) - \sigma z(\varphi)\bigr) = 0\).

Now fix one of the Selmer data, \(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}= T_{\mathrm{Sel},i}\), with a torsion level \(c_{\mathrm{tor}}\), tied primes at level \(\ell^{h_1}\) for \(c = c_{\mathrm{tor}}\) with \(h_1 > c_{\mathrm{tor}}\), and a rational \(0 < \rho \le 1\) with \(h_1\rho \ge c_{\mathrm{tor}}\). Write \(\mathrm{sl}(w) := \mathrm{sl}_i(w)\). §87.5. Stickelberger systems

For a finite set \(Q\) of finite places, write \(\mathrm{Cl}_Q\) for the group of finite ideles \(u\) of \(E\) with \(u\bar u = 1\), modulo the subgroup generated by those that are principal and those that are units at every place and congruent to \(1\) at every place of \(Q\). Every class of \(\mathrm{Cl}_Q\) is represented by \(y/\bar y\) for a finite idele \(y\). The value of a character \(\chi\) for \(Q\) at the class of \(y/\bar y\) is \(\chi(\mathrm{Art}\,y)\), where \(\mathrm{Art}\) is the Artin map; it depends only on the class. Extend this evaluation linearly to \(\bar{\mathbb{Z}}_\ell[\mathrm{Cl}_Q]\).

Definition 87.5.1. A Stickelberger system \(\mathrm{St}\) for \((\Sigma_0, g_0)\) comprises a continuous character \(\alpha\colon G_E \to \bar{\mathbb{Q}}_\ell^\times\) unramified outside \(\Sigma_0\) with \(\alpha(g_0) \ne 1\), a finite list \(L\) of integer exponents, and an element \(\mathrm{St}_Q \in \bar{\mathbb{Z}}_\ell[\mathrm{Cl}_Q]\) for each finite set \(Q\) of finite places, such that \(1(\mathrm{St}_\varnothing) \ne 0\), where \(1\) denotes the trivial character. If \(v \notin Q\) and \(Q \cup \{v\}\) is admissible for \(\Sigma_0\), then \[\chi(\mathrm{St}_{Q \cup \{v\}}) = \chi(\mathrm{St}_Q) \prod_{e \in L} \prod_{\epsilon = \pm 1} \bigl(1 - (\alpha(\mathrm{Fr}_v)\chi(\mathrm{Fr}_v))^{\epsilon}\, (\# k_v)^{-e}\bigr)\] for every character \(\chi\) for \(Q\) and every arithmetic Frobenius element \(\mathrm{Fr}_v\) at any place of \(\bar{\mathbb{Q}}\) above \(v\); the product over \(L\) counts entries with multiplicity, as does \(\#L\). Put \[\mathrm{bd}(\mathrm{St}) := \operatorname{val}\bigl(1(\mathrm{St}_\varnothing)\bigr) + 2\,\# L\,\operatorname{val}\bigl(\alpha(g_0) - 1\bigr) .\]

§87.6. The polynomial input

Problem 87.6.1. Let \(c' \ge 1\) be an integer that bounds the ranks of finitely many Selmer data and the numbers \(\#L\) of finitely many Stickelberger systems, let \(n_Q\) be a nonnegative integer, and put \(s_0 := 2c'(1 + n_Q)\). If \(Q\) is a nonempty finite set with \(\#Q \le n_Q\) and \(\rho > 0\) with \(s_0\rho < 1\), then \(\rho \le 1\), \(2c'\#Q \le s_0\), \(2\,\#Q \le s_0\), \(2\operatorname{rk}T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\,(\#Q - 1)\rho \le s_0\rho\) and \(2\,\#L\,(\#Q - 1)\rho \le s_0\rho\) for each datum and system, and \(2\,\#L\,\#Q\,\rho < 1\).

Theorem 87.6.1 (the polynomial input). Let \(E \subseteq \bar{\mathbb{Q}}\) be a CM field, \(\Sigma_0\) a finite set of finite places of \(E\), and \(g_0\) an element of inertia at a place of \(\Sigma_0\) prime to \(\ell\). Let finitely many Selmer data for \((\Sigma_0, g_0)\) over one coefficient ring \(\mathcal{O}\) and finitely many Stickelberger systems for \((\Sigma_0, g_0)\) be given, and let \(c_{\mathrm{tor}}\) be a torsion level for the data. There is an integer \(s_0 \ge 1\) such that for every rational \(\rho > 0\) with \(s_0\rho < 1\) and every sufficiently large integer \(h_1\) there are:

  1. a set \(Q\), admissible for \(\Sigma_0\), of at most \(s_0/2\) places, and for each \(w \in Q\) a homomorphism \(\lg_w\colon G_E \to \mathbb{Z}/\ell^{h_1}\) that is a character for \(\{w\}\) and takes the value \(1\) at an element of an inertia group at \(w\);

  2. for each datum and each system a polynomial \(F\) over \(\bar{\mathbb{Z}}_\ell\) in the variables \(X_w\), \(w \in Q\), and a point with coordinates in \(\bar{\mathbb{Z}}_\ell\) of valuation at least \(\rho\) at which \(\operatorname{val}(F) \le \mathrm{bd}+ s_0\rho\), where \(\mathrm{bd}\) is \(\mathrm{bd}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}})\) or \(\mathrm{bd}(\mathrm{St})\);

  3. for \(\ell^{h_1}\)-th roots of unity \(\zeta_w\), with \(x := (\zeta_w - 1)_w\): for a system, \(F(x) = \chi_\zeta(\mathrm{St}_Q)\); for a datum with \(\operatorname{val}(F(x)) < \mathrm{bd}(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}) + 1\), \(\mathrm{len}\,\mathrm{Sel}_Q(T_{\mathrm{Sel}}\chi_\zeta) \le \operatorname{val}(F(x))\).
