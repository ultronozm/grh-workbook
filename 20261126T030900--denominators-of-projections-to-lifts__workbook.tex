From Chapter~\ref{ec-chap}, \emph{Eisenstein congruences and Selmer classes}: Problem~\ref{ec-pr:diag} and Ribet's lemma, Theorem~\ref{ec-thm:ribet-lemma}. To compare lifts at the trivial twist with lifts at a twist chosen for each \(\iota \mid \ell\), one needs to control the denominators of the orthogonal projections of integral test forms onto spaces of lifts.  How large can they be?  In Chapter~\ref{cg-chap} the exponent of a congruence module gave the scalar denominator of a projection onto an eigenspace, and in Chapter~\ref{ec-chap} a congruence produced a Galois cohomology class.  Here we measure a denominator by the determinant of an operator that clears it.  Sections~\ref{sec:clearing} and~\ref{sec:eleven} are models, the second the smallest example, a class set with two elements; Section~\ref{sec:classes} describes the Galois side; Section~\ref{sec:statement} gives the definitions and the statement.  Theorem~\ref{thm:denominators} is read from the proof \cite{GL}; of its bound, the local loss is developed in Chapter~\ref{n3-chap}.

\section{Clearing a projection}\label{sec:clearing}

Let \(\Oc\) be a discrete valuation ring with fraction field \(K\) and valuation \(\ord\), normalized by \(\ord(\varpi) = 1\) for a uniformizer \(\varpi\); we use \(\ord(0) = +\infty\).  Let \(V\) be a finite-dimensional \(K\)-vector space with a lattice \(L\), and let \(V = \mathcal F \oplus \mathcal F'\) with projection \(\pr \colon V \to \mathcal F\) along \(\mathcal F'\).  Write \(\Cg(L; \mathcal F) := \pr(L)/(L \cap \mathcal F)\), as in Chapter~\ref{cg-chap}.  An operator \(C_\ell \in \mathrm{End}_K(\mathcal F)\) (the subscript recalls the prime \(\ell\) of the later sections) \emph{clears} \(\pr\) on a subset \(Y \subseteq L\) if \(C_\ell(\pr(y)) \in L\) for every \(y \in Y\).  A scalar \(c\) clears \(\pr\) on \(L\) when \(c\,\pr(L) \subseteq L\); an operator may clear different directions of \(\mathcal F\) by different amounts, and we measure it by \(\ord(\det C_\ell)\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:clear-det}
\(C_\ell\) clears \(\pr\) on \(L\) if and only if \(C_\ell(\pr(L)) \subseteq L \cap \mathcal F\).  The least value of \(\ord(\det C_\ell)\) over such \(C_\ell\) is \(\len_\Oc \Cg(L; \mathcal F)\).
\end{problem}

The operators that clear \(\pr\) on a given \(Y\) form an \(\Oc\)-module; the definitions make sense for any valuation ring \(\Oc\) and any \(\Oc\)-submodule \(L\) of \(V\).  The next problem combines clearing operators that are diagonal in one basis.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:diagonal}
Let \(\Oc\) be a valuation ring with infinite residue field and valuation \(\ord\), such as the ring of integers of \(\bar\Q_\ell\) with \(\ord(\ell) = 1\), and let \(b_1, \dots, b_n\) be a basis of \(\mathcal F\).  Let \(C_\ell^{(1)}, \dots, C_\ell^{(n)}\) clear \(\pr\) on \(Y\), with \(C_\ell^{(j)} b_i = \lambda_{ji} b_i\).  Then some \(C_\ell = \sum_j u_j C_\ell^{(j)}\) with \(u_j \in \Oc\) has \(C_\ell b_i = \mu_i b_i\) with \(\ord(\mu_i) \le \ord(\lambda_{ii})\) for every \(i\); it clears \(\pr\) on \(Y\).
\end{problem}

Now let \(K\) be a subfield of \(\C\) stable under complex conjugation, and \(\Oc\) a discrete valuation ring with fraction field \(K\).  Let \(V\) carry a positive definite hermitian form \(\langle\,,\rangle\), linear in the first variable, let \(\mathcal F' = \mathcal F^\perp\), and let \(T\) be a self-adjoint operator on \(V\) with \(T(L) \subseteq L\), diagonalizable with eigenvalues in \(\Oc\), such that \(\mathcal F \subseteq \ker(T - \lambda)\).  The vectors of \(\mathcal F^\perp\) with the eigenvalue of \(\mathcal F\),
\[
  F_T := \mathcal F^\perp \cap \ker(T - \lambda) ,
\]
are the \emph{false lifts} for \(T\).  Let \(\Pi(X) := \prod_\mu (X - \mu)\), the product over the eigenvalues \(\mu \ne \lambda\) of \(T\), each taken once.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:hecke-clear}
For \(y \in L\) orthogonal to \(F_T\), \(\Pi(T)\,y = \Pi(\lambda)\,\pr(y)\).  Hence the scalar \(\Pi(\lambda)\) clears \(\pr\) on the \(y \in L\) orthogonal to \(F_T\), and the determinant of the scalar \(\Pi(\lambda)\) acting on \(\mathcal F\) has valuation \(\dim \mathcal F \cdot \ord(\Pi(\lambda))\).
\end{problem}

The cost \(\ord(\Pi(\lambda)) = \sum_\mu \ord(\lambda - \mu)\) counts congruences between the eigenvalue of \(\mathcal F\) and the other eigenvalues.  The restriction to vectors orthogonal to the false lifts cannot be dropped.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:false-needed}
Let \(V = K^2\) with \(\langle x, y \rangle = x_1\bar y_1 + x_2\bar y_2\), \(L = \Oc^2\), \(T = \lambda\cdot 1\), \(\mathcal F = K(1,1)\), and suppose \(\ord(2) > 0\).  Then \(F_T = K(1,-1)\), no unit \(c \in \Oc^\times\) clears \(\pr\) on \(L\), and \(c = 1\) clears it on the \(y \in L\) orthogonal to \(F_T\).
\end{problem}

\section{Two classes}\label{sec:eleven}

We take the class set of Section~\ref{hk-sec:brandt} of Chapter~\ref{hk-chap}: the two left ideal classes of a maximal order in the quaternion algebra over \(\Q\) ramified at \(11\) and \(\infty\), indexed by their right orders, with \(w_1 = 2\) and \(w_2 = 3\).  Functions on the two classes are vectors \(\varphi = (\varphi(1), \varphi(2))\), with
\[
  \langle \varphi, \varphi' \rangle := \frac{\varphi(1)\overline{\varphi'(1)}}{w_1} + \frac{\varphi(2)\overline{\varphi'(2)}}{w_2} ,
\]
and the Brandt matrix \(B(\ell)\), for a prime \(\ell \ne 11\), acts by \((B(\ell)\varphi)(i) = \sum_j B_{ij}(\ell)\varphi(j)\).  Recall from that section that \(B(\ell)\) has integer entries, that \(w_jB_{ij}(\ell) = w_iB_{ji}(\ell)\) and \(\sum_j B_{ij}(\ell) = \ell + 1\), and that \(B(\ell)\) is the action of \(T_\ell\) on \(M_2(\Gamma_0(11))\), which is spanned by an Eisenstein series of \(T_\ell\)-eigenvalue \(\ell + 1\) and by the form called \(f\) there; we write
\[
  f_{11} := q\prod_{n \ge 1}(1-q^n)^2(1-q^{11n})^2 = \sum_{n \ge 1} a_n q^n .
\]
By Problem~\ref{hk-pr:brandt}, \(B(2) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 0 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(B(3) = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\).  The constant function \(1\) plays the role of the lifts: \(\mathcal F\) is the line of constant functions, and \(\pr\) is the orthogonal projection onto \(\mathcal F\).  The integral functions are \(L := \Z^2\).  Apply Section~\ref{sec:clearing} over \(K = \Q\) with \(\Oc = \Z_{(\ell)}\); the two orthogonal lines and the projection extend by scalars to \(\Q_\ell\), where we use the lattice \(\Z_\ell^2\) and the same formula without the bar.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:eleven-adjoint}
\(B(\ell)\) is self-adjoint for \(\langle\,,\rangle\), \(B(\ell)1 = (\ell + 1)1\), and \(\varphi_{11} := (2, -3)\) spans \(\mathcal F^\perp\) and satisfies \(B(\ell)\varphi_{11} = a_\ell\varphi_{11}\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:eleven-pr}
\(\pr(\varphi) = \frac15\bigl(3\varphi(1) + 2\varphi(2)\bigr)\cdot 1\), and \(5\,\pr = B(2) + 2 = B(3) + 1\).  Hence \(\pr(L) = \frac15\Z\cdot 1\) and \(\Cg(L; \mathcal F) \cong \Z/5\Z\).  The least value of \(\val_5(\det C_\ell)\) for \(C_\ell\) clearing \(\pr\) on \(\Z_5^2\) is \(1\); for \(\ell \ne 5\) the identity clears \(\pr\) on \(\Z_\ell^2\).  The scalar \(\Pi(\lambda)\) of Problem~\ref{pr:hecke-clear} is \(5\) both for \(T = B(2)\) and for \(T = B(3)\).
\end{problem}

Read backwards, the denominator forces a congruence modulo \(5\) at every prime \(\ell \ne 11\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:eleven-cong}
\(B(\ell) - a_\ell = (\ell + 1 - a_\ell)\pr\).  Hence \(a_\ell \equiv 1 + \ell \pmod 5\) for every prime \(\ell \ne 11\); for example \(a_{13} = 4\) (Problem~\ref{gr-pr:coeffs}).
\end{problem}

The denominator \(5\) is the numerator of the mass \(1/w_1 + 1/w_2 = (11 - 1)/12 = 5/6\).  The next problem gives the denominator at primes dividing none of the weights.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:mass}
Let \(I\) be a nonempty finite set with positive integers \(w_i\), let \(\langle \varphi, \varphi' \rangle := \sum_i \varphi(i)\varphi'(i)/w_i\) on \(\Q_\ell^I\), let \(\mathcal F\) be the line of constant functions, and let \(\ell\) be a prime dividing no \(w_i\).  Then \(\mathfrak m := \sum_i 1/w_i\) has \(\val_\ell(\mathfrak m) \ge 0\), and \(\Cg(\Z_\ell^I; \mathcal F) \cong \Z/\ell^{\val_\ell(\mathfrak m)}\Z\).
\end{problem}

For the quaternion algebra ramified at a prime \(p\) and \(\infty\) the mass is \((p-1)/12\), and Mazur showed that the index of his Eisenstein ideal is the numerator of \((p-1)/12\) \cite{Ma}.  There is also an integrality question for the pairing of a cleared form with an integral form.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:pairing}
In the setting of Problem~\ref{pr:mass}, drop the condition on \(\ell\), and let \(n_0\) be a common multiple of the \(w_i\).  If \(C_\ell\) clears \(\pr\) on \(Y \subseteq \Z_\ell^I\), then so does \(n_0C_\ell\), and \(\langle n_0C_\ell\pr(y), \varphi' \rangle \in \Z_\ell\) for \(y \in Y\) and \(\varphi' \in \Z_\ell^I\).  In the class set with two elements, \(6\langle \varphi, \varphi' \rangle \in \Z\) for \(\varphi, \varphi' \in \Z^2\).
\end{problem}

\section{Lifts and the classes they produce}\label{sec:classes}

Where does the congruence of Problem~\ref{pr:eleven-cong} come from?  The constant function carries the Galois representation \(1 \oplus \cyc\), where \(\cyc\) is the \(5\)-adic cyclotomic character; its trace at an arithmetic Frobenius \(\Frob_\ell\) is \(1 + \ell\) for \(\ell \ne 5\).  The form \(f_{11}\) carries the representation \(\rho_{11}\) of \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/\Q)\) on the rational \(5\)-adic Tate module \(T_5(X_0(11)) \otimes \Q_5\), whose trace at \(\Frob_\ell\) is \(a_\ell\) for \(\ell \nmid 55\) (Section~\ref{gr-sec:es} of Chapter~\ref{gr-chap}).  Let \(\omega\) be the reduction of \(\cyc\) modulo \(5\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:residual}
The semisimplification of the reduction of \(\rho_{11}\) modulo \(5\) is \(1 \oplus \omega\), and \(\omega(c) = -1\) for every complex conjugation \(c\).
\end{problem}

The representation \(\rho_{11}\) is irreducible: it is semisimple and its commutant is \(\Q_5\), by Faltings' theorem \cite{Fa}, since \(X_0(11)\) has no complex multiplication (its \(j\)-invariant \(-122023936/161051\) is not an integer).  Ribet's lemma, Theorem~\ref{ec-thm:ribet-lemma}, then gives a nonzero class with values in \(\F_5(\omega)\) or \(\F_5(\omega^{-1})\), an odd character.

In the general setting a single theta lift takes values in forms on a standard definite unitary space for the CM extension \(E/E^+\).  For a main summand \(R\) (Definition~\ref{def:main-summand} below), the normalized representation \(S\) attached to a lift eigenform for a pair twisted by a tame character \(\xi\) satisfies \(\mathrm{tr}\,S(g)=\hat\xi(g)\,\mathrm{tr}\,R(g)+\sum_{0\le e<t'}\cyc(g)^{-e}\) for every \(g\in\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\), where \(\hat\xi\) is the inverse avatar of \(\xi\) (Definition~\ref{def:aux-twist}), \(t'\) is the rank difference and \(\cyc\) is the \(\ell\)-adic cyclotomic character.  The constant function above has this shape, with no \(R\) and two characters, after a twist by the inverse \(5\)-adic cyclotomic character.  Ribet's method deals with extensions between such summands: for an extension of \(\cyc^{-e'}\) by \(\cyc^{-e}\) modulo \(\ell^\nu\), the extension cocycle takes values in \((\Oc/\ell^\nu)(-a)\), where \(a = e - e'\).  On cocycles \(z\) of \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\) a complex conjugation \(c\) acts by \(z^c(g) := z(cgc)\); call \(z\) \emph{odd} if \(z^c = -z\) and \emph{even} if \(z^c = z\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:parity}
Let \(\ell\) be odd and \(\Oc\) the ring of integers of a finite extension of \(\Q_\ell\).  Every cocycle of \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E^+)\) with values in \((\Oc/\ell^\nu)(-a)\) differs by a coboundary from one with \(z(c) = 0\).  For such \(z\), the restriction to \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\) satisfies \(z^c = (-1)^a z\).
\end{problem}

For odd \(\ell\) and odd \(a\), restriction from \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E^+)\) to \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\) therefore gives classes with odd representatives.  For the CM field \(E\subseteq\Q(\mu_{q_1d})\) of Chapter~\ref{da-chap}, Theorem~\ref{tt-thm:oddsha}, proved using Gauss sums as in Chapter~\ref{gs-chap}, gives one positive integer \(X_{\mathrm{odd}}\) for every prime \(\ell\), every coefficient ring \(\Oc\) allowed there, every \(0<a<t\) and every precision \(\nu\ge0\).  The \(\Oc\)-length of the module of continuous odd cocycles with values in \((\Oc/\ell^\nu)(-a)\), unramified off \(\ell\) and zero on the kernel of \(\cyc\) in every decomposition group above \(\ell\), is at most \(\operatorname{ord}_{\Oc}(X_{\mathrm{odd}})\), with a uniformizer of order \(1\).  For \(a=0\) the same bound holds for the everywhere unramified homomorphisms with values in \(\Oc/\ell^\nu\).  No order of a group of even classes occurs in Definition~\ref{def:clearing-bound}: the even cocycles are detected by Frobenius elements at further places.  Here is such a detection, for a Kummer class.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:kummer}
Let \(\ell\) be an odd prime, \(u \in \Q^\times\) not an \(\ell\)-th power, and \(\mathrm{kum}_u\) the Kummer class of \(u\) in \(H^1(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/\Q), \mu_\ell)\).  For a prime \(q \equiv 1 \pmod \ell\) with \(\val_q(u) = 0\), \(\mathrm{kum}_u\) is unramified at \(q\), and \(\mathrm{kum}_u(\Frob_q) = 0\), a condition independent of the choice of \(\Frob_q\), if and only if \(u^{(q-1)/\ell} \equiv 1 \pmod q\).  There are infinitely many such \(q\) with \(\mathrm{kum}_u(\Frob_q) \ne 0\).  For \(\ell = 3\) and \(u = 2\), \(q = 7\) detects \(\mathrm{kum}_2\) and \(q = 31\) does not.
\end{problem}

Ribet's lemma needs an element on which the two characters differ (Problem~\ref{ec-pr:diag}).  For the characters \(\cyc^{-e}\), \(0 \le e < t\), this costs the valuations of the differences \(\cyc(g)^{a} - 1\), \(0 < a < t\): for large \(\ell\) an element \(g\) of inertia at \(\ell\) costs nothing, by Problem~\ref{pr:generator}, and otherwise a Frobenius element at a chosen place above a level prime costs at most \(\val_\ell(\mathcal N)\), by Problem~\ref{pr:gl-order} with \(n = rt\), for the integer \(\mathcal N\) of Section~\ref{ob-sec:chosen}.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:generator}
Let \(g \in \Z_\ell^\times\) reduce to a generator of \(\F_\ell^\times\).  Then \(g^a - 1 \in \Z_\ell^\times\) for \(0 < a < \ell - 1\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:gl-order}
Let \(q\) be a prime power and \(1 \le a \le n\).  Then \(q^a - 1\) divides \(\#\GL_n(\F_q) = q^{n(n-1)/2}\prod_{i=1}^n (q^i - 1)\).
\end{problem}

\section{The statement}\label{sec:statement}

We use the data of Chapter~\ref{da-chap}: the CM field \(E\) with \(f\) infinite places, the rank \(r\), \(t = 5r\), the numbers \(m\) and \(k\), the level primes \(p_0, p_1\) with \(M = p_0p_1\), the level \(N\), the moduli \(q_1\) and \(d\), and the budget \(B\) of Definition~\ref{da-def:budget}.  From Chapter~\ref{ob-chap} we use the chosen places \(\mathcal M\), labels, the good idempotent \(e_0\) and the integer \(\mathcal N\) (Section~\ref{ob-sec:chosen}), the gap \(\Delta = 2r^8\) and the weights \(\kappa_j\) of the lifts (Section~\ref{ob-sec:weights}), tame characters \(\xi\) and twists \(\vartheta\) with their sets \(Q_\xi\), \(Q_\vartheta\) (Section~\ref{ob-sec:twists}), and the single lifts \(u_{\vartheta,j}\) on good forms (Section~\ref{ob-sec:chain}).

Fix a prime \(\ell\) and a ring isomorphism \(\iota \colon \C \simeq \bar\Q_\ell\).  As in Chapter~\ref{ob-chap}, \(\val\) is the valuation of \(\bar\Q_\ell\) with \(\val(\ell) = 1\) and \(\val(0) = \infty\), read through \(\iota\) on complex numbers, and \(\val_+ := \max(\val, 0)\).  A form is \emph{integral at \(\iota\)} if its values, read through \(\iota\) in the \(\ell\)-adic gauge of its weight, have norm at most \(1\).  The field \(E\) is embedded in \(\bar\Q\), and \(G_E := \mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\); with \(c\) the complex conjugation of \(\bar\Q\) fixed in the proof, \(g^c := cgc\) for \(g \in G_E\); and \(\cyc\) is the \(\ell\)-adic cyclotomic character.  For a place of \(\bar\Q\) above a finite place \(w\) of \(E\) not dividing \(\ell\), \(s_w\) denotes its distinguished generator of tame inertia; it depends on the place of \(\bar\Q\).

A \emph{coefficient ring} \(\Oc\) is the ring of integers of a finite Galois extension of \(\Q_\ell\) in \(\bar\Q_\ell\), and a statement holds \emph{for all large \(\Oc\)} if it holds for all coefficient rings containing a fixed one.  The \emph{normalized length} of an \(\Oc\)-module of finite length is its length divided by the ramification index \(e_{\Oc}\) of \(\Oc\) over \(\Z_\ell\); so \(\Oc/\ell\) has normalized length \(1\), and the normalized length does not change when \(\Oc\) is enlarged and the module extended.

\begin{definition}\label{def:clearing-bound}
Let \(n_\ell := \val_\ell(\mathcal N)\) and \(o_\ell := \val_\ell(X_{\mathrm{odd}})\), where \(X_{\mathrm{odd}}\) is the integer that the proof fixes for the length bound of Section~\ref{sec:classes}, with \(0 < X_{\mathrm{odd}} \le 2^{4(q_1d + t + 3)^{27}} h_E\).  Let \(\loc_\ell = 4f\,\mathrm{defect}_\ell\) be the local loss of Definition~\ref{n3-def:loss}, and put
\[
  \mathrm{tw}_\ell := (4f + 3)\,r\,n_\ell + 2^{-\ell}, \qquad \varepsilon_\ell := t\,o_\ell + 8ft\,n_\ell + 2n_\ell + \bigl((4t + 1)(\Delta/2)^{22} + \Delta/2\bigr)n_\ell .
\]
The \emph{clearing bound} is \(\cl_\ell := \loc_\ell + (1 + 2t)\,\mathrm{tw}_\ell + \varepsilon_\ell + n_\ell\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:descent-pair}
A \emph{descent pair} \(\DP\) has two finite indexed families of places of \(E\), chosen places and twist places; all their entries are distinct, and none is the conjugate of any entry.  It has a hermitian space \(W\) of rank \(r'\) with signs on its basis indices, a natural number assigned to each index, an injection of those indices into the basis indices of the standard definite space \(V = I_{n'}\) with \(r' < n'\), column multiplicities \(k^\pm(\tau,i)\) at each infinite place \(\tau\) and height \(i \ge 0\), and a continuous unitary character \(\mu\) of \(U(1)(\mathbb A)\), trivial on rational points, with specified integral weight at each infinite place and specified tame characters at all finite places, trivial off the two families.  Its forms \(\mathcal A(\DP)\) are the algebraic forms on \(V\) of the weight, level and level character of \(\DP\).  We write \(t' := n' - r'\) for its rank difference.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:false-lift}
Let \(e_1\) be an element of the group algebra of \(\prod_w \GL_{r'}(k_w)\) over the chosen places of \(\DP\), and \(A \subseteq \mathcal A(\DP)\) a subspace.  For a split place \(w\) such that neither \(w\) nor \(\bar w\) is a place of \(\DP\), the \emph{false lifts} at \(w\) are the forms in \(\mathrm{im}(e_1) \cap A\) killed by the Hecke polynomial operator of \(\DP\) at \(w\) at the value \(\bigl(\nu_w q_w^{n'-r'-1}\bigr)^{-1}\) expected of a lift, where \(q_w = \#k_w\) and \(\nu_w\) is the rank-\(r'\) Frobenius value of the character of the source space \(W\).  A form is \emph{orthogonal to the false lifts} for \((\DP, e_1, A)\) if it is orthogonal to the false lifts at every such \(w\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:denominator}
Let \(e_1\) be as in Definition~\ref{def:false-lift}, \(\mathcal F \subseteq \mathcal A(\DP)\) a subspace, with orthogonal projection \(\pr\), and \(\mathrm{den} \in \mathbb R\).  The projection onto \(\mathcal F\) has \emph{denominator at most \(\mathrm{den}\)} at \(\iota\) if some operator \(C_\ell\) on \(\mathcal F\) has \(\lvert \iota(\det C_\ell) \rvert_\ell \ge \exp(-\dim \mathcal F \cdot r' \cdot \mathrm{den})\) and, for every form \(y\) integral at \(\iota\) and orthogonal to the false lifts for \((\DP, e_1, \mathcal F^\perp)\), \(C_\ell(\pr(y))\) is integral at \(\iota\) and \(\lvert \iota\langle C_\ell(\pr(y)), y' \rangle \rvert_\ell \le 1\) for every form \(y'\) integral at every ring map \(\C \to \C_\ell\).  Here \(\langle\,,\rangle\) is the class sum of the pointwise inner products, linear in the first variable.
\end{definition}

For a twist \(\vartheta\) and \(1 \le j \le m\), take the \(j\)-th descent pair of \(\vartheta\), \(e_1 = e_0\) and \(\mathcal F\) the image of \(u_{\vartheta,j}\).  Then the false lifts are those of Definition~\ref{l1-def:false}, and the condition of Definition~\ref{def:denominator}, without the bound on the pairings \(\langle C_\ell(\pr(y)), y'\rangle\), says that \(\mathrm{den}\) clears the projection at \((\iota, \vartheta, j)\) in the sense of Definition~\ref{l1-def:clears}.

\begin{definition}\label{def:aux-twist}
The \emph{inverse avatar} of a tame character \(\xi\) is the \(\ell\)-adic avatar \(\hat\xi \colon G_E \to \bar\Q_\ell^\times\), through \(\iota\), of the base change of \(\xi^{-1}\) to \(E\): the continuous character with \(\hat\xi(\mathrm{Art}\,x) = \iota\bigl(\xi(x/\bar x)\bigr)^{-1}\) for every finite idele \(x\) of \(E\), where \(\mathrm{Art}\) is the Artin map.  Let \(\mathrm{ram} \ge 0\) be real.  A twist \(\vartheta\) is \emph{auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{ram}\)} (at \(\iota\)) if:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] every place of \(Q_\vartheta\) has prime norm, and neither it nor its conjugate divides \(\ell MN\) (its conjugate is not in \(Q_\vartheta\), as for every tame character);
\item[(b)] \(\hat\vartheta\) is locally constant, and there is a character \(\eta\) of the finite ideles of \(E\), with values in \(\bar\Q_\ell^\times\), such that \(\hat\vartheta(\mathrm{Art}\,x) = \eta(x/\bar x)\) for every finite idele \(x\), and \(\eta\) is trivial on \(E^\times\) and on every idele that is a unit at the places not above \(\ell\) and is congruent to \(1\) at the places of \(Q_\vartheta\);
\item[(c)] for some choice of a place of \(\bar\Q\) above each \(w \in Q_\vartheta\), with tame generators \(s_w\), \(\sum_{w \in Q_\vartheta} \val_+\bigl(\hat\vartheta(s_w) - 1\bigr) \le \mathrm{ram}\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:jumps}
Let \(r'\) and \(t'\) be natural numbers.  A family \(\HT = (\HT_\sigma)\) of finite multisets of integers, one for each embedding \(\sigma \colon E \to \C\), is \emph{admissible for \((r', t')\)} if:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \(1 \le r' \le r\) and \(t' \le t\);
\item[(ii)] there is one \(0 \le j \le m\) such that, for every \(\sigma\), every \(u \in \HT_\sigma\) satisfies \(\Delta/4 \le \lvert u\rvert \le 2m\Delta\) or \(\lvert s_\sigma u + \kappa_j([\sigma])\rvert \le t\); here \([\sigma]\) is the infinite place of \(\sigma\), and \(s_\sigma = \pm 1\) is the sign of \(\sigma\) for the CM type, that is, \(\sigma(\sqrt{-d}) = s_\sigma\sqrt{-d}\);
\item[(iii)] \(k \cdot \#\{u \in \HT_\sigma : \lvert u\rvert \le 2(\Delta/2)^2\} \le r'm\) for every \(\sigma\), the members counted with multiplicity;
\item[(iv)] \(\HT_\sigma\) and \(\HT_{\bar\sigma}\) together have at most \(r'\) negative members, for every \(\sigma\).
\end{enumerate}
The members of \(\HT_\sigma\) are called \emph{jumps}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:main-summand-like}
Let \(t' \ge 0\) be an integer, \(\HT\) a family as in Definition~\ref{def:jumps}, and \(R \colon G_E \to \GL_{r'}(\bar\Q_\ell)\) a homomorphism.  A \emph{lattice of \(R\) over \(\Oc\)} is a homomorphism \(G_E \to \GL_{r'}(\Oc)\) conjugate to \(R\) by an element of \(\GL_{r'}(\bar\Q_\ell)\).  The representation \(R\) is \emph{main-summand-like for \((t', \HT)\)} if:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] \(R\) is continuous, the trace pairing on the span of its image is nondegenerate, and \(\mathrm{tr}\,R(g^c) = \cyc(g)^{-\max(t'-1,0)}\,\mathrm{tr}\,R(g^{-1})\) for every \(g \in G_E\);
\item[(b)] at every place of \(\bar\Q\) above every finite place \(w\) of \(E\): if \(w \nmid \ell\), \(R\) is trivial on the elements of the inertia group in a neighbourhood of \(1\); if \(w \mid \ell\), \(R\) is potentially crystalline, with jumps \(\HT\) at the embeddings corresponding to each \(\ell\)-adic local datum; and if \(w \nmid M\), \(R\) is unramified (\(w \nmid \ell\)), respectively crystalline (\(w \mid \ell\));
\item[(c)] \(\HT\) is admissible for \((r', t')\);
\item[(d)] at every place of \(\bar\Q\) above every \(w \mid M\) with \(w \nmid \ell\) there are an element \(\delta\) of the inertia group and a natural number \(n_w\) prime to the residue characteristic such that \(R(s^{n_w}) = 1\) for every inertia element \(s\) there, \(\delta\) is the tame generator \(s_w\) if \(w \in \mathcal M\), and
\(\val_+\det(R(\delta) - 1) \le r'n_\ell\), \(\val_+\det\bigl(R((\delta^c)^{-1}) - 1\bigr) \le r'n_\ell\);
\item[(e)] for all large \(\Oc\), the image of every lattice of \(R\) over \(\Oc\) spans \(M_{r'}(\Oc)\) over \(\Oc\); and for all large \(\Oc\) and every lattice of \(R\) over \(\Oc\), some \(g \in G_E\) with \(\cyc(g) \in \{p_0^{r'}, p_1^{r'}\}\) acts on it by a scalar;
\item[(f)] some element of an inertia group above a place dividing \(M\) and not dividing \(\ell\) acts through \(R\) by a scalar \(y\) with \(\lvert y^{\ell^a j} - 1\rvert_\ell = 1\) for all integers \(a \ge 0\) and \(1 \le j \le 4r' + 2\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:main-summand}
Let \(\mathfrak s = (\mathfrak s(w,i))\) be a family of complex numbers indexed by the finite places \(w\) of \(E\) and the integers \(i \ge 0\).  A form \(y \in \mathcal A(\DP)\) \emph{lifts} \(\mathfrak s\) if, at every split place \(w\) such that neither \(w\) nor \(\bar w\) is a place of \(\DP\), it is spherical and its Hecke polynomial at \(w\) is
\[
  \prod_{0 \le i < t'}\bigl(1 - q_w^i\,\nu_W T\bigr)\cdot H_{\mathfrak s(w)}\bigl(q_w^{t'}\nu_W T/\nu_V\bigr), \qquad H_{\mathfrak s(w)}(T) := \sum_{i=0}^{r'} (-T)^i q_w^{i(i-1)/2}\,\mathfrak s(w,i) ,
\]
where \(\nu_W\) and \(\nu_V\) are the Frobenius values at \(w\) of the characters of \(W\) and \(V\), in ranks \(r'\) and \(n'\).  A homomorphism \(R \colon G_E \to \GL_{r'}(\bar\Q_\ell)\) is a \emph{main summand} for a descent pair \(\DP_0\) of ranks \(r' < n'\) and \(\mathfrak s\) if it is main-summand-like for \((n' - r', \HT)\) for some \(\HT\), and
\[
  \mathrm{tr}\,S(g) = \hat\xi(g)\,\mathrm{tr}\,R(g) + \sum_{0 \le e < n'-r'} \cyc(g)^{-e} \qquad (g \in G_E)
\]
for every tame character \(\xi\), every pair \(\DP\) on the same index sets whose character is that of \(\DP_0\) times \(\xi\), every eigenform of \(\DP\) that lifts \(\mathfrak s\), and every representation attached to its Hecke polynomials, with \(S\) its normalization for \(\DP\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:selmer-bound}
Let \(R \colon G_E \to \GL_{r'}(\bar\Q_\ell)\) be a homomorphism, \(\xi\) a tame character, \(g_* \in G_E\) and \(\mathrm{sel} \ge 0\) real.  Let \(L\) be a lattice of \(R\) over a coefficient ring \(\Oc\) containing the values of \(\hat\xi\), and \(L_{\mathrm{tw}}\) the module \(\Oc^{r'}\) on which \(g \in G_E\) acts by \(\hat\xi(g)L(g)\).  Then \(\mathrm{Sel}(L_{\mathrm{tw}}, g_*)\) is the \(\Oc\)-module of locally constant maps \(z \colon G_E \to L_{\mathrm{tw}} \otimes_{\Oc} (\mathrm{Frac}(\Oc)/\Oc)\) such that: \(z(gh) = z(g) + g\,z(h)\) for all \(g, h\); \(z\) vanishes on the inertia groups above the finite places of \(E\) that neither divide \(\ell M\) nor lie in \(Q_\xi \cup \bar Q_\xi\); \(z\) satisfies the \emph{crystalline relations}, that is, every linear relation \(\sum_i a_i\,z(g_i) = 0\), with \(g_i\) in the decomposition group of the place of \(\bar\Q\) fixed in the proof above a place \(v \mid \ell\) of \(E\) and \(a_i \in M_{r'}(\Oc)\), that holds for all integral crystalline cocycles of this decomposition group with values in \(L_{\mathrm{tw}}\); and \(z(g_*) = 0\).  The twist \(\hat\xi R\) \emph{has Selmer bound \(\mathrm{sel}\)} if there are an element \(g_*\) of an inertia group above a place of \(E\) not dividing \(\ell\) and an integer \(\zeta\) of \(\bar\Q_\ell\) such that, for all large \(\Oc\), there is a lattice \(L\) of \(R\) over \(\Oc\) for which \(\zeta \in \Oc\), \(\zeta - 1 \in \Oc^\times\), \(g_*\) acts on \(L_{\mathrm{tw}}\) by the scalar \(\zeta\), and \(\mathrm{Sel}(L_{\mathrm{tw}}, g_*)\) has normalized length at most \(\mathrm{sel}\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:lift-setting}
A descent pair \(\DP\) with a tuple of polynomials over the residue fields of its chosen places is in \emph{lift setting} for \(\vartheta\) if its indexed chosen and twist places agree with those of the data and of \(\vartheta\); its tame part is the \(j\)-th power of the level character of the data times that of \(\vartheta\), for some \(1 \le j \le m\); \(r'\) and \(n' - r'\) are odd, with \(r' \le r\) and \(n' - r' \le t\); each polynomial is a label of degree \(r'\) (Section~\ref{ob-sec:chosen}), and none of its roots becomes \(1\) on multiplication by the element of \(k_w^\times\) naming the inverse tame character of \(\DP\) at its place \(w\); and above each \(p_i\) one polynomial is primitive with \(r' = r\), or equal to \(X - 1\) with \(r' = 1\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:lift-generator}
Let \(\vartheta\) be a twist, \(\DP\) a descent pair, \(e_1\) as in Definition~\ref{def:false-lift}, \(\mathcal F \subseteq \mathcal A(\DP)\) a subspace and \(\mathrm{sel} \ge 0\) real.  A \emph{lift generator} for \((\vartheta, \DP, e_1, \mathcal F)\) with Selmer bound \(\mathrm{sel}\) is a form \(y \in \mathcal F\) such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] \(y\) lies in the range of the idempotent of a tuple of polynomials with which \(\DP\) is in lift setting for \(\vartheta\) (Definition~\ref{def:lift-setting}), and the whole range of this idempotent is contained in \(\mathrm{im}(e_1)\);
\item[(b)] \(y\) lifts a family \(\mathfrak s\) for which there are a descent pair \(\DP_0\) on the same index sets, whose character times \(\vartheta\) is the character of \(\DP\), and a main summand \(R\) for \(\DP_0\) and \(\mathfrak s\) such that \(\hat\vartheta R\) has Selmer bound \(\mathrm{sel}\) (Definition~\ref{def:selmer-bound}).
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:denominators}
Let \(\ell\) and \(\iota\) be as above, and let \(\vartheta\) be a twist that is auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\).  Let \(\DP\) be a descent pair of ranks \(r' < n'\), \(e_1\) an element of the group algebra of \(\prod_w \GL_{r'}(k_w)\) over its chosen places, and \(\mathcal F \subseteq \mathcal A(\DP)\) a subspace contained in the span of the lift generators for \((\vartheta, \DP, e_1, \mathcal F)\) with Selmer bound \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\).  Then the projection onto \(\mathcal F\) has denominator at most \(\cl_\ell \log \ell\) at \(\iota\), in the sense of Definition~\ref{def:denominator}.
\end{theorem}

\emph{Status}: read from the proof \cite{GL}; not proved here.  \emph{Sketch.}  For \(\mathcal F = 0\) the identity serves.  Otherwise write \(t'=n'-r'\) and choose a basis of \(\mathcal F\) among the lift generators.  For each basis vector Ribet's method gives a clearing operator diagonal in this basis whose eigenvalue on that vector has valuation at most \((1+2t')\mathrm{tw}_\ell+r'\loc_\ell+\varepsilon_\ell\) through \(\iota\).  Problem~\ref{pr:diagonal} gives one integral combination with this bound on every eigenvalue.  Multiplication by \(\mathcal N\) clears the pairings as in Problem~\ref{pr:pairing}, and Problems~\ref{pr:budget} and~\ref{pr:det-bound} give the determinant bound.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:budget}
Let \(r', t'\) be integers with \(1 \le r' \le r\) and \(0 \le t' \le t\), and let \(0 \le \mathrm{sel}, \mathrm{ram} \le \mathrm{tw}_\ell\).  Then
\[
  \mathrm{sel} + 2t'\,\mathrm{ram} + r'\,\loc_\ell + \varepsilon_\ell + n_\ell \le r'\cl_\ell .
\]
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:det-bound}
Let \(\mathcal F\) be a finite-dimensional complex vector space, \(n_0 \ge 0\) an integer, \(u_0 \ge 0\), and \(C_\ell \in \mathrm{End}_\C(\mathcal F)\) with \(\val(n_0\iota(\mu)) \le u_0\) for every eigenvalue \(\mu\) of \(C_\ell\).  Then, with \(\lvert \ell \rvert_\ell = \ell^{-1}\), \(\lvert \iota(\det(n_0C_\ell)) \rvert_\ell \ge \exp(-\dim \mathcal F \cdot u_0 \log \ell)\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:negligible}
The sum of \(2^{-\ell}\log \ell\) over all primes \(\ell\) is \(0.3776\ldots\), and for every finite set \(P_0\) of primes, \(\sum_{\ell \in P_0} \mathrm{tw}_\ell \log \ell \le (4f + 3)\,r \log \mathcal N + 0.378\).
\end{problem}

The clearing bounds of all primes together fit the budget \(B\).  Let \(C_1\) be the number of \(U(1)\) double cosets at the chosen level; its valuation is added to \(\cl_\ell\) for the rank-one lifts of Chapter~\ref{tw-chap}.  With \(x\) the scale of Chapter~\ref{da-chap}, the proof shows for the data, by comparison of sizes:
\[
  \log\bigl(2^{4(q_1d + t + 3)^{27}} h_E\bigr) \le x^{2000}, \qquad (4f+3)\,r\log\mathcal N + 2 \le x^{999}, \qquad \log C_1 \le x^{30},
\]
\[
  t\,x^{2000} + \bigl(8ft + 2 + (4t + 1)(\Delta/2)^{22} + \Delta/2\bigr)\log\mathcal N \le x^{4000},
\]
\[
  4\cdot 10^6 e^{180} f\,\frac mk\, b\log b + (1 + 2t)\,x^{999} + x^{4000} + \log\mathcal N + x^{30} \le B .
\]

\begin{problem}\label{pr:budget-sum}
Granting the five displayed inequalities, for every finite set \(P_0\) of primes
\[
  \sum_{\ell \in P_0} \bigl(\cl_\ell + \val_\ell(C_1)\bigr)\log\ell \;\le\; 4\cdot 10^6 e^{180} f\,\frac mk\, b\log b + (1 + 2t)\,x^{999} + x^{4000} + \log\mathcal N + x^{30} \;\le\; B .
\]
\end{problem}

The clearing operator bounds the \(\ell\)-adic norm of the determinant of the comparison operator after projection onto the twisted lift space.  For \(1 \le j \le m\), \(e_1 = e_0\) and \(\mathcal F\) the image of \(u_{\vartheta,j}\), so that \(r' = r\), Theorem~\ref{thm:denominators} shows that every real \(\cl(\ell) \ge \cl_\ell\log\ell\) clears the projection at \((\iota, \vartheta, j)\) in the sense of Definition~\ref{l1-def:clears}, provided \(\vartheta\) is auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\) and every eigensystem occurring in a nonzero good form on \(W_m\) has a main summand, for the \(j\)-th descent pair of the trivial twist, whose twist by \(\hat\vartheta\) has Selmer bound \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\).  Chapter~\ref{tw-chap} chooses an \(m\)-fold twist \(\theta_\iota\) for each \(\iota \mid \ell\), and at index \(j\) one takes \(\vartheta = \theta_\iota^j\).  Of the terms of \(\cl_\ell\), Chapter~\ref{n3-chap} develops the local loss \(\loc_\ell\) and proves the bound for its sum (Theorem~\ref{n3-thm:local-loss}); Theorem~\ref{thm:denominators} itself is taken from the proof.
