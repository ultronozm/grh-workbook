The denominator of the orthogonal projection onto the lifts is a congruence module, bounded by local losses, the class number, the level, odd classes and congruences between characters.  The chapter carries Ribet's method of Chapters~\ref{c28-chap}--\ref{c30-chap} to lifts of eigensystems, removes even classes by Taylor--Wiles places, and takes the odd classes from Chapter~\ref{c27-chap}.
