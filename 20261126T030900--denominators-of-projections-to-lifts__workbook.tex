\paragraph{Prerequisites.}
Discrete valuation rings and lengths of modules; the elementary divisor theorem.  The Chebotarev density theorem and the Brauer--Nesbitt theorem \cite[(30.16)]{CR}.  From Chapter~\ref{cg-chap}, \emph{Congruence modules}: the congruence module \(\Cg(\,\cdot\,;\,\cdot\,)\) and Problems~\ref{cg-pr:symmetry} and~\ref{cg-pr:denominator}.  From Chapter~\ref{ec-chap}, \emph{Eisenstein congruences and Selmer classes}: Problem~\ref{ec-pr:diag} and Ribet's lemma, Theorem~\ref{ec-thm:ribet-lemma}.  From Chapter~\ref{hk-chap}, \emph{Hecke operators and theta lifts}: Section~\ref{hk-sec:brandt}, with Problems~\ref{hk-pr:brandt} and~\ref{hk-pr:basis}.  From Chapter~\ref{gr-chap}: Section~\ref{gr-sec:es} on \(X_0(11)\).  For Section~\ref{sec:classes}, Chapters~\ref{tt-chap} and~\ref{gs-chap} on Tate twists and odd classes; Chapter~\ref{pc-chap} is background.  For Section~\ref{sec:theorem}, the data of Chapter~\ref{da-chap}.

To compare lifts at the trivial twist with lifts at a twist chosen for each prime \(\ell\) and each isomorphism \(\iota \colon \C \simeq \bar\Q_\ell\), one needs to control the denominators of the orthogonal projections of integral test forms onto spaces of lifts.  How large can they be?  In Chapter~\ref{cg-chap} the exponent of a congruence module gave the scalar denominator of a projection onto an eigenspace, and in Chapter~\ref{ec-chap} a congruence produced a Galois cohomology class.  Here we measure a denominator by the determinant of an operator that clears it, and work out the smallest example, a class set with two elements.  Section~\ref{sec:theorem} states the denominator theorem in the form of the rewritten Lean proof of Alpöge's theorem \cite{GL}, a sum of local losses, Selmer lengths of twisted main summands, odd classes and congruences between the characters \(\chi^{-e}\), and gives exercises on its estimates.

\section{Clearing a projection}\label{sec:clearing}

Let \(\Oc\) be a discrete valuation ring with fraction field \(K\) and valuation \(v\), normalized by \(v(\varpi) = 1\) for a uniformizer \(\varpi\); we use \(v(0) = +\infty\).  Let \(V\) be a finite-dimensional \(K\)-vector space with a lattice \(L\), and let \(V = Z \oplus Z'\) with projection \(\pr \colon V \to Z\) along \(Z'\).  Write \(\Cg(L; Z) := \pr(L)/(L \cap Z)\), as in Chapter~\ref{cg-chap}.  An operator \(\cC \in \mathrm{End}_K(Z)\) \emph{clears} \(\pr\) on a subset \(Y \subseteq L\) if \(\cC(\pr(y)) \in L\) for every \(y \in Y\).  A scalar \(c\) clears \(\pr\) on \(L\) when \(c\,\pr(L) \subseteq L\); an operator may clear different directions of \(Z\) by different amounts, and we measure it by \(v(\det \cC)\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:clear-det}
\(\cC\) clears \(\pr\) on \(L\) if and only if \(\cC(\pr(L)) \subseteq L \cap Z\).  The least value of \(v(\det \cC)\) over such \(\cC\) is \(\len_\Oc \Cg(L; Z)\).
\end{problem}

The operators that clear \(\pr\) on a given \(Y\) form an \(\Oc\)-module.  In the application one constructs, for each member \(b_k\) of a basis \(b_1, \dots, b_m\) of \(Z\) made of lifts, a clearing operator diagonal in this basis with a bound at \(b_k\); the next problem models their combination.  The definitions make sense for any valuation ring \(\Oc\) and any \(\Oc\)-submodule \(L\) of \(V\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:diagonal}
Let \(\Oc\) be a valuation ring with infinite residue field, such as the ring of integers of \(\bar\Q_\ell\).  Let \(\cC_1, \dots, \cC_m\) clear \(\pr\) on \(Y\), each diagonal in the basis \(b_1, \dots, b_m\), with \(\cC_i b_k = \lambda_{ik} b_k\).  Then some \(\cC = \sum_i d_i \cC_i\) with \(d_i \in \Oc\) has \(\cC b_k = \mu_k b_k\) with \(v(\mu_k) \le v(\lambda_{kk})\) for every \(k\); it clears \(\pr\) on \(Y\).
\end{problem}

Now let \(K\) be a subfield of \(\C\) stable under complex conjugation, and \(\Oc\) a discrete valuation ring with fraction field \(K\) (a localization of the ring of integers of a number field, say).  Let \(V\) carry a positive definite hermitian form \(\langle\,,\rangle\), linear in the first variable, let \(Z' = Z^\perp\), and let \(T\) be a self-adjoint operator on \(V\) with \(T(L) \subseteq L\), diagonalizable with eigenvalues in \(\Oc\), such that \(Z \subseteq \ker(T - \lambda)\).  The vectors of \(Z^\perp\) with the eigenvalue of \(Z\),
\[
  F := Z^\perp \cap \ker(T - \lambda) ,
\]
are the \emph{false lifts} for \(T\).  Let \(\Pi(X) := \prod_\mu (X - \mu)\), the product over the eigenvalues \(\mu \ne \lambda\) of \(T\), each taken once.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:hecke-clear}
For \(y \in L\) orthogonal to \(F\), \(\Pi(T)\,y = \Pi(\lambda)\,\pr(y)\).  Hence the scalar \(\Pi(\lambda)\) clears \(\pr\) on the \(y \in L\) orthogonal to \(F\), and the determinant of the scalar \(\Pi(\lambda)\) acting on \(Z\) has valuation \(\dim Z \cdot v(\Pi(\lambda))\).
\end{problem}

The cost \(v(\Pi(\lambda)) = \sum_\mu v(\lambda - \mu)\) counts congruences between the eigenvalue of \(Z\) and the other eigenvalues.  The restriction to vectors orthogonal to the false lifts cannot be dropped.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:false-needed}
Let \(V = K^2\) with \(\langle x, y \rangle = x_1\bar y_1 + x_2\bar y_2\), \(L = \Oc^2\), \(T = \lambda\cdot 1\), \(Z = K(1,1)\), and suppose \(v(2) > 0\).  Then \(F = K(1,-1)\), no unit \(c \in \Oc^\times\) clears \(\pr\) on \(L\), and \(c = 1\) clears it on the \(y \in L\) orthogonal to \(F\).
\end{problem}

\section{Two classes}\label{sec:eleven}

We take the class set of Section~\ref{hk-sec:brandt} of Chapter~\ref{hk-chap}: the two left ideal classes of a maximal order in the quaternion algebra over \(\Q\) ramified at \(11\) and \(\infty\), indexed by their right orders, with \(w_1 = 2\) and \(w_2 = 3\).  Functions on the two classes are vectors \(\varphi = (\varphi(1), \varphi(2))\), with
\[
  \langle \varphi, \varphi' \rangle := \frac{\varphi(1)\overline{\varphi'(1)}}{w_1} + \frac{\varphi(2)\overline{\varphi'(2)}}{w_2} ,
\]
and the Brandt matrix \(B(\ell)\), for a prime \(\ell \ne 11\), acts by \((B(\ell)\varphi)(i) = \sum_j B_{ij}(\ell)\varphi(j)\).  Recall from that section that \(B(\ell)\) has integer entries, that \(w_jB_{ij}(\ell) = w_iB_{ji}(\ell)\) and \(\sum_j B_{ij}(\ell) = \ell + 1\), and that \(B(\ell)\) is the action of \(T_\ell\) on \(M_2(\Gamma_0(11))\), which is spanned by an Eisenstein series of \(T_\ell\)-eigenvalue \(\ell + 1\) and by the form called \(f\) there; we write
\[
  f_{11} := q\prod_{n \ge 1}(1-q^n)^2(1-q^{11n})^2 = \sum_{n \ge 1} a_n q^n .
\]
By Problem~\ref{hk-pr:brandt}, \(B(2) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 0 \end{pmatrix}\) and \(B(3) = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 2 \\ 3 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\).  The constant function \(1\) plays the role of the lifts: \(Z\) is the line of constant functions, and \(\pr\) is the orthogonal projection onto \(Z\).  The integral functions are \(L := \Z^2\), and at a prime \(\ell\) we use \(L \otimes \Z_\ell\); for functions with values in \(\Q_\ell\) the inner product is given by the same formula without the bar.  Apply Section~\ref{sec:clearing} over \(K = \Q\) with \(\Oc = \Z_{(\ell)}\); the two orthogonal lines and the projection extend by scalars to \(\Q_\ell\), where we use the lattice \(\Z_\ell^2\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:eleven-adjoint}
\(B(\ell)\) is self-adjoint for \(\langle\,,\rangle\), \(B(\ell)1 = (\ell + 1)1\), and \(\varphi_{11} := (2, -3)\) spans \(Z^\perp\) and satisfies \(B(\ell)\varphi_{11} = a_\ell\varphi_{11}\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:eleven-pr}
\(\pr(\varphi) = \frac15\bigl(3\varphi(1) + 2\varphi(2)\bigr)\cdot 1\), and \(5\,\pr = B(2) + 2 = B(3) + 1\).  Hence \(\pr(L) = \frac15\Z\cdot 1\) and \(\Cg(L; Z) \cong \Z/5\Z\).  The least value of \(v_5(\det\cC)\) for \(\cC\) clearing \(\pr\) on \(L \otimes \Z_5\) is \(1\); for \(\ell \ne 5\) the identity clears \(\pr\) on \(L \otimes \Z_\ell\).  The scalar \(\Pi(\lambda)\) of Problem~\ref{pr:hecke-clear} is \(5\) both for \(T = B(2)\) and for \(T = B(3)\).
\end{problem}

Read backwards, the denominator forces a congruence modulo \(5\) at every prime \(\ell \ne 11\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:eleven-cong}
\(B(\ell) - a_\ell = (\ell + 1 - a_\ell)\pr\).  Hence \(a_\ell \equiv 1 + \ell \pmod 5\) for every prime \(\ell \ne 11\); for example \(a_{13} = 4\) (Problem~\ref{gr-pr:coeffs}).
\end{problem}

The denominator \(5\) is the numerator of the mass \(1/w_1 + 1/w_2 = (11 - 1)/12 = 5/6\).  The next problem gives the denominator at primes dividing none of the weights.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:mass}
Let \(I\) be a nonempty finite set with positive integers \(w_i\), let \(\langle \varphi, \varphi' \rangle := \sum_i \varphi(i)\varphi'(i)/w_i\) on \(\Q_\ell^I\), let \(Z\) be the line of constant functions, and let \(\ell\) be a prime dividing no \(w_i\).  Then \(\mathfrak m := \sum_i 1/w_i\) has \(v_\ell(\mathfrak m) \ge 0\), and \(\Cg(\Z_\ell^I; Z) \cong \Z/\ell^{v_\ell(\mathfrak m)}\Z\).
\end{problem}

For the quaternion algebra ramified at a prime \(p\) and \(\infty\) the mass is \((p-1)/12\), and Mazur showed that the index of his Eisenstein ideal is the numerator of \((p-1)/12\) \cite{Ma}.  There is also an integrality question for the pairing of a cleared form with an integral form.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:pairing}
In the setting of Problem~\ref{pr:mass}, drop the condition on \(\ell\), and let \(\mathcal N\) be a common multiple of the \(w_i\).  If \(\cC\) clears \(\pr\) on \(Y \subseteq \Z_\ell^I\), then so does \(\mathcal N\cC\), and \(\langle \mathcal N\cC\pr(y), \varphi' \rangle \in \Z_\ell\) for \(y \in Y\) and \(\varphi' \in \Z_\ell^I\).  In the class set with two elements, \(6\langle \varphi, \varphi' \rangle \in \Z\) for \(\varphi, \varphi' \in \Z^2\).
\end{problem}

\section{Lifts and the classes they produce}\label{sec:classes}

Where does the congruence of Problem~\ref{pr:eleven-cong} come from?  The constant function carries the Galois representation \(1 \oplus \chi\), where \(\chi\) is the \(5\)-adic cyclotomic character; its trace at an arithmetic Frobenius \(\Frob_\ell\) is \(1 + \ell\) for \(\ell \ne 5\).  The form \(f_{11}\) carries the representation \(\rho_{11}\) of \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/\Q)\) on the rational \(5\)-adic Tate module \(T_5(X_0(11)) \otimes \Q_5\), with its lattice \(T_5(X_0(11))\), whose trace at \(\Frob_\ell\) is \(a_\ell\) for \(\ell \nmid 55\) (Section~\ref{gr-sec:es} of Chapter~\ref{gr-chap}).  Let \(\omega\) be the reduction of \(\chi\) modulo \(5\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:residual}
The semisimplification of the reduction of \(\rho_{11}\) modulo \(5\) is \(1 \oplus \omega\), and \(\omega(c) = -1\) for every complex conjugation \(c\).
\end{problem}

The representation \(\rho_{11}\) is irreducible: it is semisimple and its commutant is \(\Q_5\), by Faltings' theorem \cite{Fa}, since \(X_0(11)\) has no complex multiplication (its \(j\)-invariant \(-122023936/161051\) is not an integer).  Ribet's lemma, Theorem~\ref{ec-thm:ribet-lemma}, then gives a nonzero class with values in \(\F_5(\omega)\) or \(\F_5(\omega^{-1})\), an odd character.  In general the lifts are theta lifts to forms on a standard definite unitary space for a CM extension \(E/E^+\).  In the rewritten Lean proof \cite{GL}, let \(R\) be a main summand for a descent pair \(\mathcal P_0\) and a family of complex numbers \(z(w,i)\) indexed by finite places \(w\) of \(E\) and integers \(i \ge 0\).  For every descent pair \(\mathcal P\) with the same index sets whose character is that of \(\mathcal P_0\) times a weight-zero tame character \(\vartheta\) of the data, every eigenform for \(\mathcal P\) satisfying the lift Hecke polynomial identity for \(z\), and every Galois representation attached to its Hecke polynomials, the trace at \(g \in \mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\), after twisting by the normalizing character of \(\mathcal P\), is
\[
  \psi(g)\,\mathrm{tr}\,R(g) + \sum_{e=0}^{t'-1} \chi(g)^{-e} ,
\]
where \(\psi\) is the \(\ell\)-adic avatar, through \(\iota\), of the base change of \(\vartheta^{-1}\), \(\chi\) is the \(\ell\)-adic cyclotomic character, and \(t'\) is the difference of the ranks of the two hermitian spaces.  The constant function above has the same shape, with no \(R\) and \(t' = 2\), after a twist by \(\chi^{-1}\).  Ribet's method, as in Chapter~\ref{ec-chap}, deals with extensions between these summands.  For two character summands \(\chi^{-e}\), \(\chi^{-e'}\), the coefficient character for extensions of the second by the first is \(\chi^{-(e - e')}\), so modulo \(\ell^\nu\) the coefficient module for the extension cocycles is \((\Oc/\ell^\nu)(-a)\), where \(a = e - e'\).  For the main summand, let \((u_x)_{x \in Y}\) be the finite family of test representations over \(\Oc\), the ring of integers of a finite Galois extension of \(\Q_\ell\) in \(\bar\Q_\ell\).  The rewriting bounds the module of cocycles in the twisted main lattice with divisible coefficients that obey every \(\Oc\)-linear relation among the families \((u_x(g) - 1)_{x \in Y}\) as \(g\) varies and vanish at a distinguished element, the chosen tame generators and the inverses of their conjugates.  Write \(T\) for the twisted main lattice, \(Q\) for the twist modulus, \(W_{\mathrm{TW}}\) for the set of Taylor--Wiles places, and \(S\) for the places over \(\ell p_0p_1\) together with \(Q \cup \bar Q\).  Assume \(T\) is unramified away from \(S\) and the test representations are continuous and unramified away from \(S \cup W_{\mathrm{TW}} \cup \bar W_{\mathrm{TW}}\); at each \(v \mid \ell\) assume that the test lattices and all the summands lie in the prescribed local class, and that the sum of the normalized dual defects of the summands over these places is at most \(r'\,\mathrm{loc}_\ell\), where \(r'\) is the rank of \(T\) and \(\mathrm{loc}_\ell\) is defined in Section~\ref{sec:theorem}.  If \(T\) has finite crystalline Selmer length, the ordinary length of this cocycle module is at most the sum of the local loss of \(T\) above \(\ell\) and its ordinary crystalline Selmer length.  On cocycles \(z\) of \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\) with values in \((\Oc/\ell^\nu)(-a)\), a complex conjugation \(c\) acts by \(z \mapsto z^c\), \(z^c(g) := z(cgc)\).  Call a cocycle \(z\) \emph{odd} if \(z^c = -z\) and \emph{even} if \(z^c = z\).  This involution also acts on cohomology.  For positive exponents the odd-cocycle length bound below counts cocycles satisfying \(z^c = -z\) before passage to cohomology.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:parity}
Let \(\ell\) be odd.  Every cocycle of \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E^+)\) with values in \((\Oc/\ell^\nu)(-a)\) differs by a coboundary from one with \(z(c) = 0\).  For such \(z\), the restriction to \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\) satisfies \(z^c = (-1)^a z\).
\end{problem}

For odd \(\ell\) and odd \(a\), a class with these Tate coefficients restricted from \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E^+)\) to \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\) therefore has an odd cocycle representative; this applies in particular to classes restricted from \(\Q\), as in Chapter~\ref{tt-chap}.  Let \(N_0 = q_1d\) be the cyclotomic modulus of the data, so that \(E \subseteq \Q(\mu_{N_0})\), and let \(h_E\) be the class number of \(E\).  The rewriting chooses one integer \(X\) with \(0 < X \le 2^{4(N_0 + t + 3)^{27}} h_E\) such that for every prime \(\ell\), every ring of integers \(\Oc\) of a finite extension of \(\Q_\ell\) containing a primitive root of unity of order the exponent of \((\Z/N_0)^\times\), every \(0 < a < t\) and every \(\nu \ge 0\), the \(\Oc\)-length of the module of odd continuous cocycles in \((\Oc/\ell^\nu)(-a)\), unramified away from \(\ell\) and zero on the kernel of \(\chi\) in the decomposition groups above \(\ell\), is at most the order of \(X\) in \(\Oc\) (a uniformizer having order one); for \(a = 0\) the same holds for the everywhere unramified homomorphisms.  Such bounds are the subject of Chapter~\ref{gs-chap}.  The clearing bound uses no order of a module of even cocycles.  The proof chooses a finite ordered index set with nondecreasing exponents \(0 < \mathrm{ty}(j) < t'\), and for every \(\nu > 0\) Taylor--Wiles places with tame generators \(\tau_j\) and geometric Frobenius elements \(\phi_j\) fixing \(\mu_{\ell^\nu}\).  Here \(\Oc\) ranges over the rings of integers of finite Galois extensions of \(\Q_\ell\) in \(\bar\Q_\ell\), and the cocycles at precision \(\nu\) have coefficients modulo \(\ell^\nu\).  For an even cocycle \(z\) of exponent \(\mathrm{ty}(j)\), unramified away from \(\ell\), the chosen places and their conjugates, and zero on the kernel of \(\chi\) in the decomposition groups above \(\ell\), one has \(\ell^{Hh}z(\tau_j) = 0\) provided \(z(\tau_i) = 0\) for all \(i < j\) and \(z(\phi_i) = 0\) for all \(i < j\) of the same exponent; here \(H = r^8\), and \(h\) is the separation exponent below.  There is a finite set \(S_0 \subseteq \ker\chi\), chosen before \(\nu\), such that a continuous cocycle in \((\Oc/\ell^\nu)(-e)\) unramified away from \(\ell\) vanishes if it vanishes on \(S_0\) and at \(\gamma^{-1}\), where \(\gamma\) is the generator below; and for each \(0 < e < t'\) and \(g \in S_0\), some \(j\) of exponent \(e\) satisfies \(z(\phi_j) = 2z(g)\) for every even cocycle \(z\) of that exponent unramified away from \(\ell\) with the stated local condition.  The next problem illustrates detection by a Frobenius element for a Kummer class.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:kummer}
Let \(\ell\) be an odd prime, \(\alpha \in \Q^\times\) not an \(\ell\)-th power, and \(\kappa_\alpha\) the Kummer class of \(\alpha\) in \(H^1(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/\Q), \mu_\ell)\).  For a prime \(q \equiv 1 \pmod \ell\) with \(v_q(\alpha) = 0\), \(\kappa_\alpha\) is unramified at \(q\), and \(\kappa_\alpha(\Frob_q) = 0\), a condition independent of the choice of \(\Frob_q\), if and only if \(\alpha^{(q-1)/\ell} \equiv 1 \pmod q\).  There are infinitely many such \(q\) with \(\kappa_\alpha(\Frob_q) \ne 0\).  For \(\ell = 3\) and \(\alpha = 2\), \(q = 7\) detects \(\kappa_2\) and \(q = 31\) does not.
\end{problem}

Ribet's lemma needs an element on which the two characters differ (Problem~\ref{ec-pr:diag}).  For the characters \(\chi^{-e}\), \(0 \le e < t\), this costs the valuations of the differences \(\chi(\gamma)^{a} - 1\), \(0 < a < t\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:generator}
Let \(g \in \Z_\ell^\times\) reduce to a generator of \(\F_\ell^\times\).  Then \(g^a - 1 \in \Z_\ell^\times\) for \(0 < a < \ell - 1\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:gl-order}
Let \(q\) be a prime power and \(1 \le a \le n\).  Then \(q^a - 1\) divides \(\#\GL_n(\F_q) = q^{n(n-1)/2}\prod_{i=1}^n (q^i - 1)\).
\end{problem}

In the rewriting there are an integer \(0 \le h \le v_\ell(\mathcal N)\), with \(h = 0\) for \(\ell > H^2\), and an element \(\gamma \in \mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\) generating \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\) modulo the kernel of \(\chi\) topologically, such that \(\chi(\gamma)^a - 1\) divides \(\ell^h\) in \(\Z_\ell\) for every \(0 < a < t\); here \(H\) and \(\mathcal N\) are those of Section~\ref{sec:theorem}.  For \(\ell > H^2\) the proof first uses an element of inertia at \(\ell\), as in Problem~\ref{pr:generator}, and for \(\ell \le H^2\) a Frobenius element at a chosen place above a level prime different from \(\ell\), as in Problem~\ref{pr:gl-order} with \(n = rt\); the divisibility passes to a topological generator in both cases.

\section{The clearing bound}\label{sec:theorem}

We now state the bound of the rewritten Lean proof \cite{GL}.  Let \(E\), the rank \(r\) and the parameter \(t = 5r\), the number \(f\) of infinite places of \(E\) and the level primes \(p_0, p_1\) be those of the data of Chapter~\ref{da-chap}; above each \(p_i\) there are \(f\) chosen places of \(E\), the places above \(p_i\) that divide \(\bar\alpha\) for the element \(\alpha \in \Oc_K\) of the data, each of norm \(p_i\).  Put \(H := r^8\) and
\[
  \mathcal N := \prod_{w} \#\GL_{rt}(k_w) ,
\]
the product over the chosen places \(w\) above \(p_0\) and \(p_1\), with residue fields \(k_w\).  Let \(X\) be the integer of Section~\ref{sec:classes} bounding the odd classes.  For a prime \(\ell\) let
\begin{itemize}
\item \(n_\ell := v_\ell(\mathcal N)\) and \(o_\ell := v_\ell(X)\);
\item \(\mathrm{loc}_\ell := 4f\,\mathrm{defect}_\ell\), where \(\mathrm{defect}_\ell\) is an explicit number depending only on the data and \(\ell\), the quantity \(\mathrm{loss}(\ell)\) of Section~\ref{tw-sec:regular} (\texttt{defectBound}); \(\mathrm{loc}_\ell\) bounds the local defect above \(\ell\) per unit of rank;
\item \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell := (4f + 3)\,r\,n_\ell + 2^{-\ell}\), the allowance for the Selmer lengths and for the ramification of a twist;
\item \(\mathrm{err}_\ell := t\,o_\ell + 8ft\,n_\ell + 2n_\ell + \bigl((4t + 1)H^{22} + H\bigr)n_\ell\);
\end{itemize}
and define the \emph{clearing bound}
\[
  \beta_\ell := \mathrm{loc}_\ell + (1 + 2t)\,\mathrm{tw}_\ell + \mathrm{err}_\ell + n_\ell .
\]
The class number enters only through \(o_\ell\), and no term involves the order of a group of even classes.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:budget}
Let \(r', t'\) be integers with \(1 \le r' \le r\) and \(0 \le t' \le t\), and let \(0 \le \mathrm{sel}, \mathrm{ram} \le \mathrm{tw}_\ell\).  Then
\[
  \mathrm{sel} + 2t'\,\mathrm{ram} + r'\,\mathrm{loc}_\ell + \mathrm{err}_\ell + n_\ell \le r'\beta_\ell .
\]
\end{problem}

Here \(\mathrm{sel}\) is a common upper bound for the normalized Selmer lengths in the witnesses for a spanning set of lift generators and \(\mathrm{ram}\) an upper bound for the ramification of the twist.  The local allowance \(r'\,\mathrm{loc}_\ell\) covers the loss of the main summand and of the character summands; the terms of \(\mathrm{err}_\ell\) cover the odd cocycles and the class group, the places above \(p_0p_1\), the idempotent and the automorphisms, and the separation of the characters.  For \(Z \ne 0\) the rewriting proves Theorem~\ref{thm:denominators} below by combining operators for a basis of lift generators, as in Problem~\ref{pr:diagonal}, into a diagonal operator whose eigenvalues all have valuation at most \(\mathrm{sel} + 2t'\,\mathrm{ram} + r'\,\mathrm{loc}_\ell + \mathrm{err}_\ell\); it then multiplies it by \(\mathcal N\) to clear the pairings, since every stabilizer order in the inner product divides \(\mathcal N\) (the level lies in the stabilizer of the standard lattice, and the lift setting bounds the rank), as in Problem~\ref{pr:pairing}.  For \(Z = 0\) the identity serves.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:det-bound}
Let \(Z\) be a complex vector space of dimension \(d\), \(\iota \colon \C \simeq \bar\Q_\ell\), \(\mathcal N\) a nonnegative integer, \(B \ge 0\), and \(\cC \in \mathrm{End}_\C(Z)\).  Write \(v\) for the valuation of \(\bar\Q_\ell\) with \(v(\ell) = 1\) and \(v(0) = +\infty\), and suppose \(v(\mathcal N\iota(\mu)) \le B\) for every eigenvalue \(\mu\) of \(\cC\).  Then \(\lvert \iota(\det(\mathcal N\cC)) \rvert_\ell \ge \exp(-d B \log \ell)\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:negligible}
The sum of \(2^{-\ell}\log \ell\) over all primes \(\ell\) is \(0.3776\ldots\), and for every finite set \(T\) of primes, \(\sum_{\ell \in T} \mathrm{tw}_\ell \log \ell \le (4f + 3)\,r \log \mathcal N + 0.378\).
\end{problem}

Problem~\ref{pr:negligible} is a sharpening for exercise; the rewriting uses a cruder constant.  Fix an isomorphism \(\iota \colon \C \simeq \bar\Q_\ell\), and write \(v\) for the valuation of \(\bar\Q_\ell\) with \(v(\ell) = 1\) and \(v(0) = +\infty\).  A form is \emph{integral at \(\iota\)} if its values, read through \(\iota\) in the \(\ell\)-adic gauge of its weight, have norm at most \(1\).

\emph{Twists.}  Let \(\vartheta\) be a weight-zero tame automorphic character of the norm-one torus for the data, with nontrivial tame part exactly at the finite set \(Q\).  As part of the twist data, \(Q \cap \bar Q = \varnothing\) and every place in \(Q\) is prime to \(p_0p_1\) and to the level of the data.  Let \(\psi\) be the \(\ell\)-adic avatar of the base change of \(\vartheta^{-1}\) to \(E\), taken through \(\iota\).  The twist is \emph{auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\)} if each \(w \in Q\) has prime norm different from \(\ell\); if \(\psi\) is locally constant and \(\psi(\mathrm{Art}\,x) = \eta(x/\bar x)\) for a finite idele class character \(\eta\) of modulus dividing \(Q\) and trivial on all local components above \(\ell\); and if \(\sum_{w \in Q} v(\psi(\sigma_w) - 1) \le \mathrm{tw}_\ell\) for some choice, for each \(w \in Q\), of a place \(A_w\) above \(w\), with \(\sigma_w\) the chosen tame generator at \(A_w\).

\emph{Selmer length.}  For a representation \(R\), say that \(\psi R\) has \emph{Selmer length at most \(s\) at \(Q\)} if there are an inertia element \(\gamma\) above a place away from \(\ell\) and a scalar \(\zeta \in \bar\Z_\ell\), with \(\zeta - 1\) a unit, such that over every sufficiently large coefficient ring \(\Oc\) (the integers of a finite Galois extension of \(\Q_\ell\) in \(\bar\Q_\ell\)) there are models of \(R\), \(\psi\), \(\zeta\) for which \(\gamma\) acts by \(\zeta\) on the twisted lattice \(T\), and for which the module of continuous cocycles of \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar\Q/E)\) with values in \(T \otimes_\Oc (\mathrm{Frac}(\Oc)/\Oc)\), unramified away from the places above \(\ell p_0p_1\) and \(Q \cup \bar Q\), satisfying the crystalline relations above \(\ell\) and vanishing at \(\gamma\), has length at most \(s\), normalized so that \(\Oc/\ell\Oc\) has length one.

\emph{Forms.}  Let \(\mathcal P\) be a descent pair over \(E\), with source \(W\) of rank \(r'\), target the standard definite space \(V\) of rank \(n > r'\), and the specified injection of basis index sets, and write \(\mathcal A(\mathcal P)\) for its complex space of algebraic forms on \(V\) of the weight, level and level character of \(\mathcal P\).  Let \(\mathcal E\) be an element of the group algebra of \(\prod_w \GL_{r'}(k_w)\), where \(w\) runs over the chosen places of \(\mathcal P\), and let \(Z \subseteq \mathcal A(\mathcal P)\) be a subspace.  Write \(\nu_w\) for the Frobenius value of the source character of \(\mathcal P\) in rank \(r'\), \(q_w = \#k_w\), and \(\mathcal H_w\) for the Hecke polynomial operator of \(\mathcal P\) at \(w\).  A form is \emph{orthogonal to the false lifts} if for every split \(w\) with \(w\) and \(\bar w\) outside the places of \(\mathcal P\), it is orthogonal to \(\mathrm{im}(\mathcal E) \cap Z^\perp \cap \ker \mathcal H_w\bigl((\nu_w q_w^{n - r' - 1})^{-1}\bigr)\).  A \emph{lift generator} is a form \(y \in Z\) with two properties.  First, \(y\) lies in the range of the idempotent of a type, a tuple of polynomials over the residue fields of the chosen places, for which \(\mathcal P\) is in \emph{lift setting} for \(\vartheta\), and this whole range lies in \(\mathrm{im}(\mathcal E)\); lift setting requires that the indexed chosen places and the indexed twist places of \(\mathcal P\) agree with those of the data and of \(\vartheta\), respectively, that its tame part be the \(j\)-th power of the level character of the data times that of \(\vartheta\) for some \(1 \le j \le m\), that \(r'\) and \(n - r'\) be odd with \(r' \le r\) and \(n - r' \le t\), that each polynomial be monic and squarefree of degree \(r'\) with nonzero constant term and that none of its roots becomes \(1\) on multiplication by the element of \(k_w^\times\) corresponding, under the chosen faithful character, to the inverse of the tame character of \(\mathcal P\) at its place \(w\), and that above each \(p_i\) one polynomial be primitive with \(r' = r\) or equal to \(X - 1\) with \(r' = 1\).  Second, there is a family \(z(w,i)\) as in Section~\ref{sec:classes} such that, at every split place away from the level places and their conjugates, \(y\) is spherical and satisfies the lift Hecke polynomial identity for \(z\), and there are a representation \(R\) of rank \(r'\) and a descent pair \(\mathcal P_0\) with the same index sets such that \(R\) is a main summand for \(\mathcal P_0\) and \(z\) (for some family \(J\) of multisets of integers indexed by the embeddings of \(E\), \(R\) satisfies the local and global conditions of a main-summand-like representation for the data, \(n - r'\) and \(J\), and it satisfies the trace condition of Section~\ref{sec:classes} for every tame character and descent pair specified there), the character of \(\mathcal P\) is that of \(\mathcal P_0\) times \(\vartheta\), and \(\psi R\) has Selmer length at most \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\) at \(Q\).  The inner product \(\langle\,,\rangle\) of forms is the class sum of the pointwise inner products, linear in the first variable.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:denominators}
Let \(\ell\) be a prime, \(\iota \colon \C \simeq \bar\Q_\ell\), and \(\vartheta\) a twist that is auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\).  Let \(\mathcal P\), \(\mathcal E\) and \(Z\) be as above, and suppose that \(Z\) is spanned by lift generators.  Then there is an operator \(\cC\) on \(Z\) with
\[
  \lvert \iota(\det \cC) \rvert_\ell \ge \exp\bigl(-\dim Z \cdot r' \cdot \beta_\ell \log \ell\bigr)
\]
such that, for every form \(y\) integral at \(\iota\) and orthogonal to the false lifts, \(\cC(\pr(y))\) is integral at \(\iota\), and \(\lvert \iota\langle \cC(\pr(y)), y' \rangle \rvert_\ell \le 1\) for every form \(y'\) integral at every embedding \(\C \to \C_\ell\).  Here \(\pr\) is the orthogonal projection onto \(Z\).
\end{theorem}

Now let \(1 \le j \le m\), let \(\vartheta\) be auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\), take \(\mathcal E = e_0\), the idempotent of the good forms, and let \(Z\) be the image of the lift of the good forms on \(W_j\) for \(\vartheta\).  Suppose that for each system of Hecke eigenvalues occurring in a nonzero good form on \(W_m\) there is a main summand \(R\) for the \(j\)-th descent pair of the trivial twist such that \(\psi R\) has Selmer length at most \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\) at \(Q\).  Then the rewriting shows that \(Z\) is spanned by lift generators.  An operator on \(Z\) \emph{clears the projection} with exponent \(\beta\) if its determinant has norm at least \(\exp(-\dim Z \cdot r \cdot \beta)\) at \(\iota\) and it takes the projection of every \(\iota\)-integral form orthogonal to the false lifts for \(e_0\) and \(Z^\perp\) to an \(\iota\)-integral form.  Since \(r' = r\) here, Theorem~\ref{thm:denominators} gives such an operator for every real \(\beta \ge \beta_\ell \log \ell\).  Chapter~\ref{tw-chap} chooses, for each \((\ell, \iota)\), one twist whose first \(m\) powers have the required short Selmer groups for the given finite family of main summands.

\section*{Hints}

\subsection*{\S\ref{sec:clearing}}
\begin{description}
\item[\ref{pr:clear-det}] \(\cC\) maps \(Z\) to \(Z\).  For the minimum, \(\pr(L)\) and \(L \cap Z\) are lattices in \(Z\); use elementary divisors.
\item[\ref{pr:diagonal}] Choose the \(d_i\) generically modulo the maximal ideal.
\item[\ref{pr:hecke-clear}] Decompose \(y\) into eigenvectors of \(T\); \(\ker(T - \lambda) = Z \oplus F\) is orthogonal to the other eigenspaces.
\end{description}

\subsection*{\S\ref{sec:eleven}}
\begin{description}
\item[\ref{pr:eleven-adjoint}] Apply the balance and row-sum identities to the two orthogonal lines.
\item[\ref{pr:eleven-pr}] Apply the projection onto constants to the coordinate functions; then Problem~\ref{pr:clear-det}.
\item[\ref{pr:eleven-cong}] Compare both sides on a basis.
\item[\ref{pr:mass}] Compute \(\pr\) on the indicator functions.
\item[\ref{pr:pairing}] Inspect each weight in the class sum.
\end{description}

\subsection*{\S\ref{sec:classes}}
\begin{description}
\item[\ref{pr:residual}] The characteristic polynomial of \(\rho_{11}(\Frob_\ell)\) is \(X^2 - a_\ell X + \ell\) for \(\ell \nmid 55\) (Section~\ref{gr-sec:es} of Chapter~\ref{gr-chap}).  Use Problem~\ref{pr:eleven-cong}, the Chebotarev density theorem and the Brauer--Nesbitt theorem.
\item[\ref{pr:parity}] Evaluate the cocycle relation on \(c \cdot c = 1\) and on \(c \cdot g \cdot c\), using \(\chi(c) = -1\).
\item[\ref{pr:kummer}] \(\kappa_\alpha(\Frob_q)\) is read off from the action of \(\Frob_q\) on \(\alpha^{1/\ell}\) modulo a prime above \(q\).  The extension \(\Q(\mu_\ell, \alpha^{1/\ell})/\Q(\mu_\ell)\) has degree \(\ell\), since \(\gcd(\ell, \ell - 1) = 1\); apply the Chebotarev density theorem to its nontrivial elements.
\end{description}

\subsection*{\S\ref{sec:theorem}}
\begin{description}
\item[\ref{pr:budget}] Use \(r' \ge 1\) and \(t' \le t\).
\item[\ref{pr:negligible}] Use the factorization of \(\mathcal N\).  For the prime sum, sum through \(31\) and bound the rest by \(\sum_{k > 31} k\,2^{-k}\).
\end{description}
