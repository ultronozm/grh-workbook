\paragraph{Prerequisites.}
The data of Chapter~\ref{da-chap}; the chosen places, labels, weights, twists and single lifts of Chapter~\ref{ob-chap}; Definitions~\ref{l1-def:false} and~\ref{l1-def:clears} and Theorem~\ref{l1-thm:compression}; the gauge of Definition~\ref{dl-def:gauge}; the Hecke operators \(T_{w,i}\), the eigenforms and the attached representations of Section~\ref{gr-sec:spherical}; Theorem~\ref{tt-thm:oddsha}.  \(\ell\)-adic representations of Galois groups, crystalline representations and their Hodge--Tate weights, at the level of definitions.  The companion sheet \emph{Clearing a projection: two classes} computes a clearing operator on a class set with two elements.  Its preceding two-dimensional example shows why the orthogonality condition on test forms is needed.  Chapter~\ref{n3-chap} bounds the sum of the local losses over the primes, and Chapter~\ref{n1-chap}, \emph{Congruences bounded by lengths}, deduces Theorem~\ref{thm:denominators} from a bound for the eigenvalues of one clearing operator.  Chapter~\ref{n2-chap}, \emph{Auxiliary primes: even classes at no cost}, shows why the bound has no term for even classes.

To compare lifts at the trivial twist with lifts at a twist chosen for each \(\iota \mid \ell\), one needs to control the denominators of the orthogonal projections of integral test forms onto spaces of lifts.  We measure a denominator by the determinant of an operator that clears it, as in \emph{Clearing a projection: two classes}.  This chapter gives the definitions, a running instance of rank one, the statement, and the arithmetic of its bound.  Theorem~\ref{thm:denominators} is deduced in Chapter~\ref{n1-chap} from Theorem~\ref{n1-thm:eigenvalues}, a bound for the eigenvalues of one clearing operator, which is read from the proof \cite{GL} and not proved here; of the bound, the sum of the local losses over the primes is bounded in Chapter~\ref{n3-chap}.

\section{The statement}\label{sec:statement}

We use the data of Chapter~\ref{da-chap}: the CM field \(E\) with \(f\) infinite places, the rank \(r\), \(t = 5r\), the numbers \(m\) and \(k\), the level primes \(p_0, p_1\) with \(M = p_0p_1\), the level \(N\), the moduli \(q_1\) and \(d\), and the budget \(B\) of Definition~\ref{da-def:budget}.  From Chapter~\ref{ob-chap} we use the chosen places \(\mathcal M\), labels, the good idempotent \(e_0\) and the integer \(\mathcal N\) (Section~\ref{ob-sec:chosen}), the gap \(\Delta = 2r^8\) and the weights \(\kappa_j\) of the lifts (Section~\ref{ob-sec:weights}), tame characters \(\xi\) and twists \(\vartheta\) with their sets \(Q_\xi\), \(Q_\vartheta\) (Section~\ref{ob-sec:twists}), and the single lifts \(u_{\vartheta,j}\) on good forms (Section~\ref{ob-sec:chain}).

Fix a prime \(\ell\) and a ring isomorphism \(\iota \colon \C \simeq \bar{\Q}_\ell\).  As in Chapter~\ref{ob-chap}, \(\val\) is the valuation of \(\bar{\Q}_\ell\) with \(\val(\ell) = 1\) and \(\val(0) = \infty\), read through \(\iota\) on complex numbers, and \(\val_+ := \max(\val, 0)\).  A form on a definite space is \emph{integral at \(\iota\)} if its gauge (Definition~\ref{dl-def:gauge}), for \(\iota\) followed by \(\bar{\Q}_\ell \subset \C_\ell\), is at most \(1\) at every finite adelic point.  We use the choices of complex conjugation, tame generators and local embeddings fixed in the proof; they are called \emph{of the proof} below.  The field \(E\) is embedded in \(\bar{\Q}\), and \(G_E := \mathrm{Gal}(\bar{\Q}/E)\); with \(c\) the complex conjugation of \(\bar{\Q}\) fixed in the proof, \(g^c := cgc\) for \(g \in G_E\); and \(\cyc\) is the \(\ell\)-adic cyclotomic character.  For a place of \(\bar{\Q}\) above a finite place \(w\) of \(E\) not dividing \(\ell\), \(s_w\) denotes its distinguished generator of tame inertia; it depends on the place of \(\bar{\Q}\).

A \emph{coefficient ring} \(\Oc\) is the ring of integers of a finite Galois extension of \(\Q_\ell\) in \(\bar{\Q}_\ell\), and a statement holds \emph{for all large \(\Oc\)} if it holds for all coefficient rings containing a fixed one.  The \emph{normalized length} of an \(\Oc\)-module of finite length is its length divided by the ramification index \(e_{\Oc}\) of \(\Oc\) over \(\Z_\ell\); so \(\Oc/\ell\) has normalized length \(1\), and the normalized length does not change when \(\Oc\) is enlarged and the module extended.  An \(\Oc\)-module of infinite length has normalized length \(+\infty\).

\begin{definition}\label{def:clearing-bound}
Let \(X_{\mathrm{odd}}\) be the positive integer of Theorem~\ref{tt-thm:oddsha} for \(N = q_1d\), with \(X_{\mathrm{odd}} \le 2^{4(q_1d + t + 3)^{27}} h_E\) and \(h_E\) the class number of \(E\), and put \(n_\ell := \val_\ell(\mathcal N)\).  Let \(\mathrm{Int}_b\) be the product of the integers \(i\) with \(\max(0, b - 30(\Delta/2)^3) < i \le b + 30(\Delta/2)^3\), for the weight scale \(b\), and put
\[
  \mathrm{crys}_\ell := \val_\ell\bigl((2b)!\,\mathrm{Int}_b^{(\Delta/2)^3}\bigr), \qquad \mathrm{defect}_\ell := 10^6\,\frac mk\,\bigl(b\,\val_\ell(M) + \mathrm{crys}_\ell\bigr), \qquad \loc_\ell := 4f\,\mathrm{defect}_\ell ,
\]
\[
  \mathrm{tw}_\ell := (4f + 3)\,r\,n_\ell + 2^{-\ell}, \qquad \varepsilon_\ell := t\,\val_\ell(X_{\mathrm{odd}}) + 8ft\,n_\ell + 2n_\ell + \bigl((4t + 1)(\Delta/2)^{22} + \Delta/2\bigr)n_\ell .
\]
The \emph{clearing bound} is \(\cl_\ell := \loc_\ell + (1 + 2t)\,\mathrm{tw}_\ell + \varepsilon_\ell + n_\ell\).
\end{definition}

The terms of \(\cl_\ell\) pay for the following.  \(\loc_\ell\): the crystalline condition above \(\ell\) (Chapter~\ref{n3-chap}).  \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\): the Selmer length of the twisted main summand and, with the factor \(2t\), the ramification of the twist (Problem~\ref{pr:budget}).  The four terms of \(\varepsilon_\ell\), in order: the odd classes and the class group (the section on lifts and the classes they produce in \emph{Clearing a projection: two classes}); the places above \(M\) (Problem~\ref{n1-pr:tame}); the idempotent and the stabilizers; and the congruences between the characters \(\cyc^{-e}\), \(0 \le e < t\) (the exercises on generators and general linear group orders in \emph{Clearing a projection: two classes}; Definition~\ref{n1-def:sep}).  Problem~\ref{n1-pr:arith} adds the four terms up.  The last \(n_\ell\) is the cost of multiplying the clearing operator by \(\mathcal N\) to make its pairings integral.  No term pays for even classes (Chapter~\ref{n2-chap}).

\begin{definition}\label{def:descent-pair}
A \emph{descent pair} \(\DP\) has two finitely indexed families of finite places of \(E\), chosen places and twist places; all their entries are distinct, and none is the conjugate of any entry.  It has a hermitian space \(W\) of rank \(r'\) with signs on its basis indices and a specified adelic frame in which its Gram matrix is the diagonal matrix of the signs at every infinite place and the identity at every finite place, a function from the basis indices of \(W\) to the natural numbers, an injection of those indices into the basis indices of the standard definite space \(V = I_{n'}\) with \(r' < n'\) (its image is the set of \emph{selected} indices), column multiplicities \(\beta^\pm(\tau,i)\) at each infinite place \(\tau\) and height \(i \ge 0\) (the diagrams of the weight, as in Definition~\ref{ob-def:diagram}), and a continuous unitary character \(\mu\) of \(U(1)(\mathbb A)\), trivial on rational points, with specified integral weight at each infinite place and specified tame part, a character \(\psi_w\) of \(k_w^\times\) at each finite place \(w\), trivial off the two families.  Write \(K_0\) for the stabilizer of the standard integral lattice of \(V\) in the finite adelic group.  The \emph{level} of \(\DP\) consists of the elements of \(K_0\) whose reductions fix the selected basis vectors at the chosen places and preserve the span of the other basis vectors at the twist places.  Its \emph{level character} is the product over the twist places \(w\) of \(\psi_w^{-1}\) applied to the determinant of the block on the selected indices.  Its forms \(\mathcal A(\DP)\) are the algebraic forms on \(V\) on which the level acts through the level character, of the weight with column multiplicities \(\beta^+(\tau,i)+\beta^-(\tau,n'-i)\) at \(\tau\) and each height \(0\le i<n'\), and with determinant exponent \(-\sum_{1\le i<n'} \beta^-(\tau,i)\) at \(\tau\).  We write \(t' := n' - r'\) for its rank difference.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:false-lift}
Let \(e_1\) be an element of the complex group algebra of \(\prod_w \GL_{r'}(k_w)\) over the chosen places of \(\DP\), and \(A \subseteq \mathcal A(\DP)\) a subspace.  A tuple \(g = (g_w)_w\) of this group acts on \(\mathcal A(\DP)\) by right translation by an element of \(K_0\) that reduces to \(\operatorname{diag}(g_w,1)\), in the order selected indices then complementary indices, at each chosen place \(w\), and to the identity at the twist places, multiplied by \(\prod_w \psi_w(\det g_w)\); write \(\mathrm{im}(e_1)\) for the image of the operator by which \(e_1\) then acts.  In this sheet \(\upsilon_E\) is the splitting character of the Weil data on the ideles of \(E\) (Section~\ref{gr-sec:spherical}), and \(\upsilon\) is its restriction to the norm-one ideles, as in Section~\ref{ob-sec:characters}.  For a character \(a\) of the norm-one ideles and a natural number \(N_0\ge 0\), the \emph{Frobenius value} of \(a\) in rank \(N_0\) at a finite place \(w\) is \(q_w^{N_0/2}\) times the value, at the finite idele with the chosen uniformizer at \(w\) and \(1\) elsewhere, of the Hecke character \(x \mapsto a(x/\bar x)\,\upsilon_E(x)^{-N_0}\); here \(q_w = \#k_w\).  For a split place \(w\) such that neither \(w\) nor \(\bar w\) is a place of \(\DP\), with the Hecke operators \(T_{w,i}\), \(0 \le i \le n'\), of Section~\ref{gr-sec:spherical} acting on \(\mathcal A(\DP)\), the \emph{Hecke polynomial operator at \(w\) at the value \(T \in \C\)} is \(\sum_{i=0}^{n'} (-T)^i q_w^{i(i-1)/2}\, T_{w,i}\).  The \emph{false lifts} at \(w\) are the forms in \(\mathrm{im}(e_1) \cap A\) killed by the Hecke polynomial operator of \(\DP\) at \(w\) at the value \(\bigl(\nu_w q_w^{n'-r'-1}\bigr)^{-1}\) expected of a lift, where \(\nu_w\) is the Frobenius value at \(w\) of \(\upsilon^{r_-}\) in rank \(r'\), with \(r_-\) the number of negative signs of \(W\).  A form is \emph{orthogonal to the false lifts} for \((\DP, e_1, A)\) if it is orthogonal to the false lifts at every such \(w\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:denominator}
Let \(e_1\) be as in Definition~\ref{def:false-lift}, \(\mathcal F \subseteq \mathcal A(\DP)\) a subspace, with orthogonal projection \(\pr\), and \(\mathrm{den} \in \mathbb R\).  The projection onto \(\mathcal F\) has \emph{denominator at most \(\mathrm{den}\)} at \(\iota\) if some operator \(C_\ell\) on \(\mathcal F\) has \(\lvert \iota(\det C_\ell) \rvert_\ell \ge \exp(-\dim \mathcal F \cdot r' \cdot \mathrm{den})\) and, for every form \(y\) integral at \(\iota\) and orthogonal to the false lifts for \((\DP, e_1, \mathcal F^\perp)\), \(C_\ell(\pr(y))\) is integral at \(\iota\) and \(\lvert \iota\langle C_\ell(\pr(y)), y' \rangle \rvert_\ell \le 1\) for every form \(y'\) integral at every ring map \(\C \to \C_\ell\).  Here \(\langle\,,\rangle\) is the class sum of the pointwise Gram inner products, linear in the first variable; each term is weighted by the reciprocal of the order of the stabilizer of its double-coset representative.
\end{definition}

For a twist \(\vartheta\) and \(1 \le j \le m\), take the \(j\)-th descent pair of \(\vartheta\), \(e_1 = e_0\) and \(\mathcal F\) the image of \(u_{\vartheta,j}\).  Then the false lifts are those of Definition~\ref{l1-def:false}, and the condition of Definition~\ref{def:denominator}, without the bound on the pairings \(\langle C_\ell(\pr(y)), y'\rangle\), says that \(\mathrm{den}\) clears the projection at \((\iota, \vartheta, j)\) in the sense of Definition~\ref{l1-def:clears}.

\begin{definition}\label{def:aux-twist}
The \emph{inverse avatar} of a tame character \(\xi\) is the \(\ell\)-adic avatar \(\hat\xi \colon G_E \to \bar{\Q}_\ell^\times\), through \(\iota\), of the base change of \(\xi^{-1}\) to \(E\): the continuous character with \(\hat\xi(\mathrm{Art}\,x) = \iota\bigl(\xi(x/\bar x)\bigr)^{-1}\) for every finite idele \(x\) of \(E\), where \(\mathrm{Art}\) is the Artin map.  We use the Artin map of the proof, with values in the topological abelianization \(G_E^{\mathrm{ab}}\) and with a local uniformizer mapping to geometric Frobenius modulo inertia.  A character of \(G_E\) is evaluated there through the induced character.  Let \(\mathrm{ram} \in [0, \infty]\).  A twist \(\vartheta\) is \emph{auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{ram}\)} (at \(\iota\)) if:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] every place of \(Q_\vartheta\) has prime norm, and neither it nor its conjugate divides \(\ell MN\) (its conjugate is not in \(Q_\vartheta\), as for every tame character);
\item[(b)] \(\hat\vartheta\) is locally constant, and there is a character \(\chi_0\) of the finite ideles of \(E\), with values in \(\bar{\Q}_\ell^\times\), such that \(\hat\vartheta(\mathrm{Art}\,x) = \chi_0(x/\bar x)\) for every finite idele \(x\), and \(\chi_0\) is trivial on \(E^\times\) and on every idele that is a unit at the places not above \(\ell\) and is congruent to \(1\) at the places of \(Q_\vartheta\);
\item[(c)] for some choice of a place of \(\bar{\Q}\) above each \(w \in Q_\vartheta\), with tame generators \(s_w\), \(\sum_{w \in Q_\vartheta} \val_+\bigl(\hat\vartheta(s_w) - 1\bigr) \le \mathrm{ram}\).
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

For the rank-one case \(r'=1\), the main summand \(R\) is a character.  This case occurs for the lines of the lifts from \(U(1)\) attached to main pairs in Chapter~\ref{tw-chap}.  The line of constant functions in \emph{Clearing a projection: two classes} models the projection onto such a line.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:trivial-aux}
The trivial twist, with \(Q_\vartheta = \emptyset\), is auxiliary with ramification at most \(0\).
\end{problem}

\begin{definition}\label{def:jumps}
Let \(r'\) and \(t'\) be natural numbers.  A family \(\HT = (\HT_\sigma)\) of finite multisets of integers, one for each embedding \(\sigma \colon E \to \C\), is \emph{admissible for \((r', t')\)} if:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(i)] \(1 \le r' \le r\) and \(t' \le t\);
\item[(ii)] there is one \(0 \le j \le m\) such that, for every \(\sigma\), every \(u \in \HT_\sigma\) satisfies \(\Delta/4 \le \lvert u\rvert \le 2m\Delta\) or \(\lvert s_\sigma u + \kappa_j([\sigma])\rvert \le t\); here \([\sigma]\) is the infinite place of \(\sigma\), and \(s_\sigma = \pm 1\) is the sign of \(\sigma\) for the CM type, that is, \(\sigma(\sqrt{-d}) = s_\sigma\sqrt{-d}\);
\item[(iii)] \(k \cdot \#\{u \in \HT_\sigma : \lvert u\rvert \le 2(\Delta/2)^2\} \le r'm\) for every \(\sigma\), the members counted with multiplicity;
\item[(iv)] \(\HT_\sigma\) and \(\HT_{\bar\sigma}\) together have at most \(r'\) negative members, for every \(\sigma\).
\end{enumerate}
The members of \(\HT_\sigma\) are called \emph{jumps}.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:main-summand-like}
Let \(t' \ge 0\) be an integer, \(\HT\) a family as in Definition~\ref{def:jumps}, and \(R \colon G_E \to \GL_{r'}(\bar{\Q}_\ell)\) a homomorphism.  A \emph{lattice of \(R\) over \(\Oc\)} is a homomorphism \(G_E \to \GL_{r'}(\Oc)\) conjugate to \(R\) by an element of \(\GL_{r'}(\bar{\Q}_\ell)\).  The representation \(R\) is \emph{main-summand-like for \((t', \HT)\)} if:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] \(R\) is continuous, the trace pairing on the span of its image is nondegenerate, and \(\mathrm{tr}\,R(g^c) = \cyc(g)^{-\max(t'-1,0)}\,\mathrm{tr}\,R(g^{-1})\) for every \(g \in G_E\);
\item[(b)] at every place \(A\) of \(\bar{\Q}\) above every finite place \(w\) of \(E\): if \(w \nmid \ell\), \(R\) is trivial on the elements of the inertia group in a neighbourhood of \(1\); if \(w \mid \ell\), the restriction of \(R\) to the decomposition group is potentially crystalline for every local datum at \(A\), that is, every embedding \(e\) of \(\bar{\Q}\) in \(\bar{\Q}_\ell\) inducing \(A\), with the induced embedding of its decomposition group into \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar{\Q}_\ell/\Q_\ell)\), and for every such datum and every \(g \in \mathrm{Gal}(\bar{\Q}_\ell/\Q_\ell)\) its Hodge--Tate jumps at the coefficient embedding induced by \(g\) are, with multiplicity, the multiset \(\HT_\sigma\) for \(\sigma = \iota^{-1}\circ g^{-1}\circ e|_E\); and if \(w \nmid M\), \(R\) is unramified (\(w \nmid \ell\)), respectively crystalline for every local datum (\(w \mid \ell\));
\item[(c)] \(\HT\) is admissible for \((r', t')\);
\item[(d)] at every place of \(\bar{\Q}\) above every \(w \mid M\) with \(w \nmid \ell\) there are an element \(\delta\) of the inertia group and a natural number \(n_w\) prime to the residue characteristic such that \(R(s^{n_w}) = 1\) for every inertia element \(s\) there, \(\delta\) is the tame generator \(s_w\) if \(w \in \mathcal M\), and
\(\val_+\det(R(\delta) - 1) \le r'n_\ell\), \(\val_+\det\bigl(R((\delta^c)^{-1}) - 1\bigr) \le r'n_\ell\);
\item[(e)] for all large \(\Oc\), the image of every lattice of \(R\) over \(\Oc\) spans \(M_{r'}(\Oc)\) over \(\Oc\); and for all large \(\Oc\) and every lattice of \(R\) over \(\Oc\), some \(g \in G_E\) with \(\cyc(g) \in \{p_0^{r'}, p_1^{r'}\}\) acts on it by a scalar;
\item[(f)] some element of an inertia group above a place dividing \(M\) and not dividing \(\ell\) acts through \(R\) by a scalar \(y\) with \(\lvert y^{\ell^a j} - 1\rvert_\ell = 1\) for all integers \(a \ge 0\) and \(1 \le j \le 4r' + 2\).
\end{enumerate}
The Hodge--Tate jumps are taken in the convention of the proof, in which the cyclotomic character has jump \(-1\).
\end{definition}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:rank-one}
Let \(r' = 1\) and let \(R = \rho\) be a continuous character \(G_E \to \bar{\Q}_\ell^\times\).  (i) The trace pairing in (a) is nondegenerate, and the identity in (a) says \(\rho(g^c)\,\rho(g) = \cyc(g)^{-\max(t'-1,0)}\) for every \(g\).  (ii) The first half of (e) holds for every lattice of \(\rho\), and its second half holds if and only if some \(g \in G_E\) has \(\cyc(g) \in \{p_0, p_1\}\).  (iii) In (d), the two determinant inequalities become \(\val_+(\rho(\delta) - 1) \le n_\ell\) and \(\val_+(\rho((\delta^c)^{-1}) - 1) \le n_\ell\).
\end{problem}

\begin{definition}\label{def:main-summand}
Let \(\mathfrak s = (\mathfrak s(w,i))\) be a family of complex numbers indexed by the finite places \(w\) of \(E\) and the integers \(i \ge 0\).  A form \(y \in \mathcal A(\DP)\) \emph{lifts} \(\mathfrak s\) if, at every split place \(w\) such that neither \(w\) nor \(\bar w\) is a place of \(\DP\), it is spherical and for every \(T \in \C\) the Hecke polynomial operator at \(w\) at the value \(T\) sends \(y\) to \(y\) multiplied by
\[
  \prod_{0 \le i < t'}\bigl(1 - q_w^i\,\nu_W T\bigr)\cdot H_{\mathfrak s(w)}\bigl(q_w^{t'}\nu_W T/\nu_V\bigr), \qquad H_{\mathfrak s(w)}(T) := \sum_{i=0}^{r'} (-T)^i q_w^{i(i-1)/2}\,\mathfrak s(w,i) ,
\]
where \(\nu_W\) and \(\nu_V\) are the Frobenius values at \(w\) of \(\upsilon^{r_-}\) in rank \(r'\) and of \(\mu\,\upsilon^{\lfloor n'/2\rfloor}\) in rank \(n'\), respectively, as in Definition~\ref{def:false-lift}.  A homomorphism \(R \colon G_E \to \GL_{r'}(\bar{\Q}_\ell)\) is a \emph{main summand} for a descent pair \(\DP_0\) of ranks \(r' < n'\) and \(\mathfrak s\) if it is main-summand-like for \((n' - r', \HT)\) for some \(\HT\), and
\[
  \mathrm{tr}\,R^{\mathrm{nor}}(g) = \hat\xi(g)\,\mathrm{tr}\,R(g) + \sum_{0 \le e < n'-r'} \cyc(g)^{-e} \qquad (g \in G_E)
\]
for every tame character \(\xi\), every descent pair \(\DP\) with the same basis index sets for \(W\) and for \(V\) as \(\DP_0\) and with character \(\mu\) that of \(\DP_0\) times \(\xi\), every form in \(\mathcal A(\DP)\) lifting \(\mathfrak s\), every family \(P\) of polynomials for which that form is an eigenform, and every representation attached to \(P\) (Section~\ref{gr-sec:spherical}).  Here \(R^{\mathrm{nor}}\), the \emph{normalization} of that representation for \(\DP\), is its twist by the \(\ell\)-adic avatar, through \(\iota\), of the Hecke character \(x\mapsto\upsilon^{-r_-}(x/\bar x)\,\upsilon_E(x)^{r'}\,|x|^{r'/2}\), with \(r_-\) taken for \(\DP\) and \(|x|\) the idelic norm.
\end{definition}

The constant function in the two-class model illustrates the cyclotomic string in the trace formula.  Its representation \(1\oplus\cyc\) of \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar{\Q}/\Q)\), twisted by the inverse \(5\)-adic cyclotomic character, is \(1\oplus\cyc^{-1}\).

\begin{definition}\label{def:selmer-bound}
Let \(R \colon G_E \to \GL_{r'}(\bar{\Q}_\ell)\) be a homomorphism, \(\xi\) a tame character, \(g_* \in G_E\) and \(\mathrm{sel} \in [0, \infty]\).  Let \(L\) be a lattice of \(R\) over a coefficient ring \(\Oc\) containing the values of \(\hat\xi\), and \(L_{\mathrm{tw}}\) the module \(\Oc^{r'}\) on which \(g \in G_E\) acts by \(\hat\xi(g)L(g)\).  For each \(v\mid\ell\), let \(G_v\) be the decomposition group of the chosen place above \(v\) and use the local embedding of \(G_v\) into \(\mathrm{Gal}(\bar{\Q}_\ell/\Q_\ell)\) fixed in the proof.  In the crystalline relations below, an integral crystalline cocycle means a function \(z_0\colon G_v\to L_{\mathrm{tw}}\) for which some \(b_0\in B_{\max}\otimes_{\Z_\ell} L_{\mathrm{tw}}\) satisfies \(1\otimes z_0(g)=g b_0-b_0\) for every \(g\in G_v\).  Here \(B_{\max}\) is the crystalline period ring of the proof, and \(G_v\) acts on the tensor product through its local embedding on \(B_{\max}\) and through \(L_{\mathrm{tw}}\) on the second factor.  Then \(\mathrm{Sel}(L_{\mathrm{tw}}, g_*)\) is the \(\Oc\)-module of locally constant maps \(z \colon G_E \to L_{\mathrm{tw}} \otimes_{\Oc} (\mathrm{Frac}(\Oc)/\Oc)\) such that: \(z(gh) = z(g) + g\,z(h)\) for all \(g, h\); \(z\) vanishes on the inertia groups above the finite places of \(E\) that neither divide \(\ell M\) nor lie in \(Q_\xi \cup \bar Q_\xi\); \(z\) satisfies the \emph{crystalline relations}, that is, for each \(v\mid\ell\) and every finite family of \(g_i \in G_v\) and \(a_i \in M_{r'}(\Oc)\) such that \(\sum_i a_i\,z_0(g_i) = 0\) for every integral crystalline cocycle \(z_0 \colon G_v \to L_{\mathrm{tw}}\), also \(\sum_i a_i\,z(g_i) = 0\); and \(z(g_*) = 0\).  The twist \(\hat\xi R\) \emph{has Selmer bound \(\mathrm{sel}\)} if there are an element \(g_*\) of an inertia group above a place of \(E\) not dividing \(\ell\) and an integer \(\zeta\) of \(\bar{\Q}_\ell\) such that, for all large \(\Oc\), there is a lattice \(L\) of \(R\) over \(\Oc\) for which \(\zeta \in \Oc\), \(\zeta - 1 \in \Oc^\times\), \(g_*\) acts on \(L_{\mathrm{tw}}\) by the scalar \(\zeta\), and \(\mathrm{Sel}(L_{\mathrm{tw}}, g_*)\) has normalized length at most \(\mathrm{sel}\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:lift-setting}
A descent pair \(\DP\) with a tuple of polynomials over the residue fields of its chosen places is in \emph{lift setting} for \(\vartheta\) if its indexed chosen and twist places agree with those of the data and of \(\vartheta\); its tame part is the \(j\)-th power of the tame part of \(\chi_M\) from Section~\ref{ob-sec:characters}, times the tame part of \(\vartheta\), for some \(1 \le j \le m\); \(r'\) and \(n' - r'\) are odd, with \(r' \le r\) and \(n' - r' \le t\); each polynomial is a label of degree \(r'\) (Section~\ref{ob-sec:chosen}), and none of its roots becomes \(1\) on multiplication by the element \(a_w\in k_w^\times\) defined as follows.  Write \(T_w\) for the fixed injective character of \(\overline{k_w}^{\,\times}\) from Section~\ref{ob-sec:chosen} and \(\psi_w\) for the tame part at \(w\).  Choose \(z_w\in k_w^\times\) with \(T_w(z_w)=\exp(2\pi i/(q_w-1))\).  Then \(T_w(a_w)=\psi_w(z_w)^{-1}\).  Finally, above each \(p_i\) one polynomial is irreducible with a root of order \((\#k_w)^{r} - 1\) (and then \(r' = r\)), or equal to \(X - 1\) with \(r' = 1\).
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}\label{def:lift-generator}
Let \(\vartheta\) be a twist, \(\DP\) a descent pair, \(e_1\) as in Definition~\ref{def:false-lift}, \(\mathcal F \subseteq \mathcal A(\DP)\) a subspace and \(\mathrm{sel} \in [0, \infty]\).  A \emph{lift generator} for \((\vartheta, \DP, e_1, \mathcal F)\) with Selmer bound \(\mathrm{sel}\) is a form \(y \in \mathcal F\) such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] \(y\) lies in the range of the idempotent of a tuple of polynomials with which \(\DP\) is in lift setting for \(\vartheta\) (Definition~\ref{def:lift-setting}), and the whole range of this idempotent is contained in \(\mathrm{im}(e_1)\);
\item[(b)] \(y\) lifts a family \(\mathfrak s\) for which there are a descent pair \(\DP_0\) with the same basis index sets for \(W\) and for \(V\) as \(\DP\), whose character \(\mu\) times \(\vartheta\) is that of \(\DP\), and a main summand \(R\) for \(\DP_0\) and \(\mathfrak s\) such that \(\hat\vartheta R\) has Selmer bound \(\mathrm{sel}\) (Definition~\ref{def:selmer-bound}).
\end{enumerate}
\end{definition}

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:denominators}
Let \(\ell\) and \(\iota\) be as above, and let \(\vartheta\) be a twist that is auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\).  Let \(\DP\) be a descent pair of ranks \(r' < n'\), \(e_1\) an element of the complex group algebra of \(\prod_w \GL_{r'}(k_w)\) over its chosen places, and \(\mathcal F \subseteq \mathcal A(\DP)\) a subspace contained in the span of the lift generators for \((\vartheta, \DP, e_1, \mathcal F)\) with Selmer bound \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\).  Then the projection onto \(\mathcal F\) has denominator at most \(\cl_\ell \log \ell\) at \(\iota\), in the sense of Definition~\ref{def:denominator}.
\end{theorem}

\emph{Status}: deduced in Problem~\ref{n1-pr:deduce} from Theorem~\ref{n1-thm:eigenvalues}, which is read from the proof \cite{GL} and not proved here.  The next four problems are arithmetic of the clearing bound and of determinants; Problems~\ref{pr:budget} and~\ref{pr:det-bound} are used in that deduction.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:budget}
Let \(r', t'\) be integers with \(1 \le r' \le r\) and \(0 \le t' \le t\), and let \(0 \le \mathrm{sel}, \mathrm{ram} \le \mathrm{tw}_\ell\).  Then
\[
  \mathrm{sel} + 2t'\,\mathrm{ram} + r'\,\loc_\ell + \varepsilon_\ell + n_\ell \le r'\cl_\ell .
\]
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:rank-one-budget}
For \(r' = 1\), \(1 \le t' \le t\) and \(\mathrm{sel} = \mathrm{ram} = \mathrm{tw}_\ell\), the two sides of Problem~\ref{pr:budget} differ by exactly \(2(t - t')\,\mathrm{tw}_\ell\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:det-bound}
Let \(\mathcal F\) be a finite-dimensional complex vector space, \(n_0 \ge 0\) an integer, \(u_0\) a nonnegative real number, and \(C_\ell \in \mathrm{End}_{\C}(\mathcal F)\) with \(\val_+(n_0\iota(\mu)) \le u_0\) for every eigenvalue \(\mu\) of \(C_\ell\).  Then, with \(\lvert \ell \rvert_\ell = \ell^{-1}\), \(\lvert \iota(\det(n_0C_\ell)) \rvert_\ell \ge \exp(-\dim \mathcal F \cdot u_0 \log \ell)\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:negligible}
The sum of \(2^{-\ell}\log \ell\) over all primes \(\ell\) is \(0.3776\ldots\), and for every finite set \(P_0\) of primes, \(\sum_{\ell \in P_0} \mathrm{tw}_\ell \log \ell \le (4f + 3)\,r \log \mathcal N + 0.378\).
\end{problem}

The clearing bounds of all primes together fit the budget \(B\): the proof bounds by \(B\) the sum in Problem~\ref{pr:budget-sum}.  Let \(C_1\), the class number of \(U(1)\), be the number of double cosets of the finite adelic points of \(U(1)\) by the rational points and by the integral ideles congruent to \(1\) at every chosen place.  For a main pair at \(j\) and \(\vartheta=\theta_\iota^j\), the pairing of the projections of two test forms onto the line of the lift from \(U(1)\) has \(\ell\)-adic norm at most \(\exp((\cl_\ell+\val_\ell(C_1))\log\ell)\) (Section~\ref{tw-sec:regular}).  With \(x\) the length of the prime sum and \(b\) the weight scale of Chapter~\ref{da-chap}, consider the following five comparisons.
\[
  \log\bigl(2^{4(q_1d + t + 3)^{27}} h_E\bigr) \le x^{2000}, \qquad (4f+3)\,r\log\mathcal N + 2 \le x^{999}, \qquad \log C_1 \le x^{30},
\]
\[
  t\,x^{2000} + \bigl(8ft + 2 + (4t + 1)(\Delta/2)^{22} + \Delta/2\bigr)\log\mathcal N \le x^{4000},
\]
\[
  4\cdot 10^6 e^{180} f\,\frac mk\, b\log b + (1 + 2t)\,x^{999} + x^{4000} + \log\mathcal N + x^{30} \le B .
\]

\begin{problem}\label{pr:budget-sum}
Granting the five displayed inequalities, for every finite set \(P_0\) of primes
\[
  \sum_{\ell \in P_0} \bigl(\cl_\ell + \val_\ell(C_1)\bigr)\log\ell \;\le\; 4\cdot 10^6 e^{180} f\,\frac mk\, b\log b + (1 + 2t)\,x^{999} + x^{4000} + \log\mathcal N + x^{30} \;\le\; B .
\]
\end{problem}

Fix \(1\le j\le m\).  By Theorem~\ref{l1-thm:compression}(b), for a twist \(\vartheta\) of modulus prime to \(\ell\), a real \(\cl(\ell)\) that clears the projection at \((\iota,\vartheta,j)\) bounds the \(\ell\)-adic norm, through \(\iota\), of the determinant of the compression of a comparison operator to the image of \(u_{\vartheta,j}\) by \(\exp(\dim_{\C} H_g(W_j)\cdot r\cdot\cl(\ell))\).  For \(1 \le j \le m\), \(e_1 = e_0\) and \(\mathcal F\) the image of \(u_{\vartheta,j}\), so that \(r' = r\), the proof deduces from Theorem~\ref{thm:denominators} that every real \(\cl(\ell) \ge \cl_\ell\log\ell\) clears the projection at \((\iota, \vartheta, j)\) in the sense of Definition~\ref{l1-def:clears}, provided \(\vartheta\) is auxiliary with ramification at most \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\) and every eigensystem occurring in a nonzero good form on \(W_m\) has a main summand, for the \(j\)-th descent pair of the trivial twist, whose twist by \(\hat\vartheta\) has Selmer bound \(\mathrm{tw}_\ell\).  Chapter~\ref{tw-chap} chooses an \(m\)-fold twist \(\theta_\iota\) for each \(\iota \mid \ell\), and at index \(j\) one takes \(\vartheta = \theta_\iota^j\).  Chapter~\ref{n3-chap} explains the local loss \(\loc_\ell\) and proves the bound for its sum over the primes (Theorem~\ref{n3-thm:local-loss}); Chapter~\ref{n1-chap} deduces Theorem~\ref{thm:denominators} from Theorem~\ref{n1-thm:eigenvalues}, which is taken from the proof.
