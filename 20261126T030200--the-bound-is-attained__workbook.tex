\paragraph{Prerequisites.}
Chapters~\ref{c4-chap}, \ref{c14-chap} and~\ref{c15-chap}.

\medskip
On one curve with complex multiplication the divisibility of derivatives proved in Chapter~\ref{c14-chap} cannot be improved: for every order \(n\) some power series has an \(n\)-th derivative at the origin whose valuation is close to the guaranteed one.  This chapter proves that for \(y^2 = x^3 - x\) at a prime \(p \equiv 3 \pmod 4\), in one variable, states the case of two variables used in the proof, and explains why the lower bound for the determinant needs it, and where the sparsity of the places of Chapter~\ref{c4-chap} enters.

This chapter is being drafted.
