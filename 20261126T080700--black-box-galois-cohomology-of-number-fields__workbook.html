Chapter 32 states an attachment theorem for eigenforms on definite unitary groups. Chapter 88, Tied primes, uses a bound for a group of cocycles in terms of relations among their values at auxiliary primes. How does a formula for the orders of two cohomology groups give such a bound? We check the formula for cubic characters over \(\mathbb Q\), then relate dual cohomology classes to values of cocycles at auxiliary primes.

Prerequisites.

Continuous Galois cohomology through degree two, cup products, Kummer theory and local and global class field theory [NSW; CFT]. Section 32.4 supplies the definition of most Frobenius data. For the final section use the setting of Definition 88.3.1 in Chapter 88.

§33.1. Local conditions Tag 238D

Local duality gives the complementary conditions needed to apply the counting formula. Let \(F\) be a number field, \(G_F\) its absolute Galois group and \(F_v\) the completion at a place \(v\). In this chapter \(M\) denotes a finite discrete \(G_F\)-module. Write \(M^*=\operatorname{Hom}(M,\mu)\), with \(\mu\) the roots of unity in an algebraic closure and with the action on both source and target. Cohomology is continuous. Restriction uses a choice of place above \(v\), so that \(G_{F_v}\) is the decomposition group. At finite \(v\), write \(I_v\) for inertia, the kernel of the action on the residue field. The unramified subgroup \(H^1_{\mathrm{ur}}(F_v,M)\) is the kernel of restriction to \(I_v\).

Tag 238E

Theorem 33.1.1 (Local Tate duality and Euler characteristic). At a finite place \(v\), the cup product followed by evaluation \(M\otimes M^*\to\mu\) and the local invariant \(\operatorname{inv}_v\) gives a perfect pairing \[H^1(F_v,M)\times H^1(F_v,M^*)\longrightarrow \mathbb Q/\mathbb Z.\] The orders of the local groups satisfy \[\begin{align} \# H^1(F_v,M)&=\# H^0(F_v,M)\,\# H^2(F_v,M)\,|\#M|_v^{-1},\\ \# H^2(F_v,M)&=\# H^0(F_v,M^*). \end{align}\] The absolute value \(|\cdot|_v\) gives a uniformizer absolute value \((\#k_v)^{-1}\), where \(k_v\) is the residue field.

Used in: Chapter 34

Status. Classical [NSW]. At a real place use the same degree-one pairing with the real invariant taking values in \(\tfrac12\mathbb Z/\mathbb Z\). At a complex place both degree-one groups are zero [NSW].

Tag 238F

Theorem 33.1.2 (Unramified classes). At a finite place, \[\#H^1_{\mathrm{ur}}(F_v,M)=\#H^0(F_v,M).\] If inertia acts trivially on \(M\) and the residue characteristic is prime to \(\#M\), then \(H^1_{\mathrm{ur}}(F_v,M)\) and \(H^1_{\mathrm{ur}}(F_v,M^*)\) are exact annihilators.

Status. Classical [NSW].

Tag 238G

Definition 33.1.1. A family of local conditions \(\mathcal L\) consists of subgroups \(\mathcal L_v\subseteq H^1(F_v,M)\), equal to \(H^1_{\mathrm{ur}}(F_v,M)\) at all but finitely many finite places. Write \(\mathcal L_v^\perp\) for the annihilator under the local pairing. The Selmer group \(H^1_{\mathcal L}(F,M)\) is the group of classes whose restriction at each \(v\) lies in \(\mathcal L_v\). Use the same definition for \(H^1_{\mathcal L^\perp}(F,M^*)\).

Tag 238H

Theorem 33.1.3 (Greenberg–Wiles). For local conditions \(\mathcal L\) as above, the two Selmer groups are finite and \[\frac{\#H^1_{\mathcal L}(F,M)}{\#H^1_{\mathcal L^\perp}(F,M^*)} = \frac{\#H^0(F,M)}{\#H^0(F,M^*)} \prod_v \frac{\#\mathcal L_v}{\#H^0(F_v,M)} .\] Here \(H^0\) means ordinary invariants, including at infinite places.

Used in: Problem 33.2.7; Chapter 84; Chapter 88

Status. Classical [DDT, Thm. 2.19], [NSW].

Tag 238J

Theorem 33.1.4 (Reciprocity). For a class in \(H^2(F,\mu)\), the sum of the local invariants is zero. Let \(n\ge 1\) and write \(\mu_n\) for the \(n\)-th roots of unity. At a finite place \(v\), let \(\chi\) be a continuous character of \(G_{F_v}\) into \(\tfrac1n\mathbb Z/\mathbb Z\) and \([a]\) the Kummer class of \(a\in F_v^\times\) in \(H^1(F_v,\mu_n)\). Then \[\operatorname{inv}_v(\chi\cup[a])=\chi(\operatorname{rec}_v(a)).\] The coefficient pairing sends \((j/n,\xi)\) to \(\xi^j\) for \(\xi\in\mu_n\). The local Artin map \(\operatorname{rec}_v\) sends a uniformizer to arithmetic Frobenius modulo inertia. In particular, if \(\chi\) is unramified, the invariant is \(\operatorname{ord}_v(a)\chi(\mathrm{Fr}_v)\), where \(\operatorname{ord}_v\) gives a uniformizer value \(1\).

Status. Classical [CFT, Ch. VIII, Thm. 4.2], [NSW].

§33.2. Cubic characters over \(\mathbb Q\) Tag 238K

The following problems compare the two sides of the counting formula independently. Take \(F=\mathbb Q\), \(M=\mathbb Z/3\) with trivial action, and \(q\in\{5,7,13,19\}\). Use the full local group at \(q\), the unramified subgroup at every other finite place, and zero at the real place. Thus \(M^*=\mu_3\).

Tag 238L

Problem 33.2.1. The group of cube roots of unity in \(\mathbb Q_q\) has order \(1\) at \(q=5\) and \(3\) at \(q=7,13,19\).

Tag 238M

Problem 33.2.2. The order of \(H^1(\mathbb Q_q,\mathbb Z/3)\) is \(3\) at \(q=5\) and \(9\) at \(q=7,13,19\).

Tag 238N

Problem 33.2.3. For any finite place \(v\) of \(\mathbb Q\), the annihilator of the unramified subgroup of \(H^1(\mathbb Q_v,\mathbb Z/3)\) consists of Kummer classes represented by elements of valuation divisible by \(3\).

Tag 238P

Problem 33.2.4. The dual Selmer group is trivial.

Tag 238Q

Problem 33.2.5. Inflation identifies \(\operatorname{Hom}(\operatorname{Gal}(\mathbb Q(\mu_q)/\mathbb Q),\mathbb Z/3)\) with the Selmer group.

Tag 238R

Problem 33.2.6. The Selmer group has order \(1\) at \(q=5\) and \(3\) at \(q=7,13,19\). For each of the latter primes the nonzero characters cut out the unique cubic subfield of \(\mathbb Q(\mu_q)\).

Tag 238S

Problem 33.2.7. Both sides of Theorem 33.1.3 are \(1,3,3,3\) in the order \(q=5,7,13,19\). The real place contributes a factor of \(1/3\) to the product.

§33.3. Choosing primes and bounding ramification Tag 238T

Chebotarev supplies primes with prescribed Frobenius behavior. A cocycle has bounded ramification here if it vanishes on inertia outside a fixed finite set of places.

Tag 238U

Theorem 33.3.1 (Chebotarev, existence form). Let a nonempty subset of \(G_F\) be a neighborhood of each of its points, and prescribe a finite set of finite places of \(F\) to avoid. There is a finite place \(v\) outside the prescribed set with prime residue-field order, and a place above \(v\) of the algebraic closure. There is an arithmetic Frobenius element at the chosen place in the given subset, and every arithmetic Frobenius element at the chosen place lies in the subset. An arithmetic Frobenius element is a lift to the decomposition group of the map \(x\mapsto x^{\#k_v}\) on the residue field of the chosen place.

Used in: Chapter 88

Status. The existence statement follows from the classical density theorem [CFT, Ch. V, Thm. 3.23].

The density problem uses the Frobenius data of Section 32.4.

Tag 238V

Problem 33.3.1. Let \(F\) be a CM field. If a property of \(s\in G_F\) holds for most Frobenius data \((v,A,s)\) in the sense of Section 32.4, then the set of elements satisfying the property is dense in \(G_F\).

Tag 238W

Theorem 33.3.2 (Finiteness with bounded ramification). For a finite set of finite places of \(F\), there are only finitely many continuous \(1\)-cocycles with values in \(M\) that vanish on every inertia group above places outside the set.

Status. Classical. For the finiteness of classes with restricted ramification, see [ADT, Ch. I, Cor. 4.15].

§33.4. The relation count Tag 238X

The count uses the totally complex setting of Section 88.3 and Definition 88.3.1. Use \(E\) for the field of the data as in that chapter. Retain \(V\), \(A_v\), \(Z(R)\), \(G'\), \(\Sigma'\) and \(f_E\) from the setting of the definition. Assume \(\sigma\in G_E\) fixes the \(\ell\)-power roots of unity, is central modulo \(G'\) and fixes only \(0\) in \(V\). Put \(\Sigma''=\Sigma'\cup\{v:v\mid\ell\}\). Use \(M=V\) in Definition 33.1.1, with \(\mathcal L_v\) the image of \(A_v\) at places in \(\Sigma'\), and with the unramified condition at the other finite places. At complex places take zero. Write \(D=H^1_{\mathcal L^\perp}(E,M^*)\) in this section. Let \(\zeta\) be the fixed primitive root of order \(\ell^{h_1}\) in Definition 88.3.1. Sending \(\zeta^k\) to \(k/\ell^{h_1}+\mathbb Z\) identifies \(M^*\) as an abelian group with the character group of that definition. The Galois action on a character is precomposition by the inverse action on \(V\), followed by multiplication by the cyclotomic character modulo \(\ell^{h_1}\).

The first two problems change a count of cohomology classes to a count of cocycles.

Tag 238Y

Problem 33.4.1. The natural map induces an isomorphism \(Z(\varnothing)/B^1(E,V)\cong H^1_{\mathcal L}(E,V)\), where \(B^1(E,V)\) is the group of global coboundaries. For \(v\in\Sigma'\), the order of the local image \(\mathcal L_v\) is \(\#A_v\cdot \#H^0(E_v,V)/\#V\).

Tag 238Z

Problem 33.4.2. The orders satisfy \[\#Z(\varnothing)\,(\#V)^{\#\Sigma'+f_E} = \frac{\#D}{\#H^0(E,M^*)}\,\#V\prod_{v\in\Sigma'}\#A_v .\]

The next two problems relate dual classes to homomorphisms on \(G'\).

Tag 2392

Problem 33.4.3 (Sah’s lemma). Let \(w\) be a \(1\)-cocycle on \(G_E/G'\) with values in \(M^*\), and use \(\sigma\) also for its image in the quotient. Then \((\sigma-1)w(g)=(g-1)w(\sigma)\) for each \(g\in G_E/G'\). The map \(\sigma-1\) is invertible on \(M^*\), the cocycle \(w\) is a coboundary, and \(H^0(E,M^*)=0\).

Tag 2393

Problem 33.4.4. Restriction gives an injection from \(D\) to the group of continuous homomorphisms from \(G'\) to \(M^*\). For \(b\in D\), let \(y\) be the restricted homomorphism extended by zero on \(G_E\setminus G'\) and viewed in the character group of Definition 88.3.1. Then \(y\) is locally constant, satisfies the conjugation rule in that definition and vanishes on inertia outside \(\Sigma''\).

Used in: Problem 33.4.5, Problem 33.4.6

It remains to check the reciprocity condition for the relation.

Tag 2394

Problem 33.4.5. Fix \(b\in D\) with associated \(y\) from Problem 33.4.4. Take any family \((v_j,\mathrm{Fr}_j,s_j)_j\) with the choices of Definition 88.3.1 outside \(\Sigma''\), and let \(z\in Z(\{v_j\})\) with class \([z]\). The local invariant of \(b\cup[z]\), using evaluation on \(M^*\otimes M\), is \(y(\mathrm{Fr}_j)(z(s_j))\) at \(v_j\) and zero at every other place.

Tag 2395

Problem 33.4.6. The group \(\mathrm{Rel}(\Sigma'')\) is finite. For every \(b\in D\), let \(y\) be the function from Problem 33.4.4. For every test family of Definition 88.3.1 outside \(\Sigma''\) and every \(z\in Z(\{v_j\})\), the sum of \(y(\mathrm{Fr}_j)(z(s_j))\) is zero. The map \(b\mapsto y\) is an injection into \(\mathrm{Rel}(\Sigma'')\) and gives \[\#Z(\varnothing)\,(\#V)^{\#\Sigma'+f_E} \le \#\mathrm{Rel}(\Sigma'')\,\#V\prod_{v\in\Sigma'}\#A_v .\] If a positive integer \(p\) is prime to \(\ell\) and no place of \(\Sigma'\) lies above a prime dividing \(p\), then no place of \(\Sigma''\) lies above a prime dividing \(p\).

Used in: Chapter 88

In the Lean formalization.

In the Lean proof [GL], the existence statement is New.chebotarev. The finiteness statement is New.selmerZ1.finite_unramified_cocycles, the absolute Galois group case of New.finite_cocycles_unramified_outside. Problem 33.3.1 is the CM case of New.dense_of_mostFrobenii.

The Lean form of the count uses the avoidance hypotheses of Section 88.3. Let \(p\) be a positive integer prime to \(\ell\), with no place of \(\Sigma'\) above a prime dividing \(p\) and with every inertia group above primes dividing \(p\) contained in \(G'\). New.RelationSetting.exists_card_cocycles_le gives a finite set \(\widetilde\Sigma\supseteq\Sigma'\) with no place above a prime dividing \(p\) such that the inequality of Problem 33.4.6 holds with \(\mathrm{Rel}(\widetilde\Sigma)\) on the right.

Under the avoidance hypotheses of Section 88.3, Problem 33.4.6 gives the count used there with \(\Sigma''=\Sigma'\cup\{v:v\mid\ell\}\). The deductions take as given local Tate duality and the local Euler characteristic, the unramified annihilator and cardinality statements, the Greenberg–Wiles formula, reciprocity, Chebotarev and finiteness with bounded ramification.
