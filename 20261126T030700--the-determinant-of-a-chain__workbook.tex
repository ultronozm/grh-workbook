\medskip
For the lower chain and the single lifts, fix the data of Chapter~\ref{da-chap}.  Let \(m\) be the number of lifts, \(r\) the rank of \(Y_0\), \(t := 5r\), and \(x_1\) and \(b\) the scales of \(D\).

How does the determinant \(Z(\vartheta)\) of the chain change with the \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\)?  By Theorem~\ref{thm:main}, its quotient by \(Z(1)\) is the product of the \(m\) quotients \(Z_j(\vartheta)/Z_j(1)\) for single lifts, times a correction factor: a product of pairwise ratios \(\rho_{ij}(\vartheta)\) of Hecke \(L\)-values, multiplied by \((\Nm Q_\vartheta)^{e_Z/2}\) with an integer \(e_Z\) independent of \(\vartheta\), and raised to the power \(d_T\).  The problems derive Theorem~\ref{thm:main} using the Gram identity of one lift (Theorem~\ref{c1-thm:one-lift}), the comparison on good forms (Corollary~\ref{c2-cor:equal}), the Gram identity of the chain (Theorem~\ref{c4-thm:chain-gram}), and the injectivity of the single lifts and the chain.  Chapter~\ref{c4-chap} derives the Gram identity of the chain from three statements read from the proof: the realization of a triangle of lifts, the inner product formula on its first edges, and the comparison at the final level.  The square laws in the realization use the exchange identity (Theorem~\ref{c3-thm:exchange}, stated and not proved in Chapter~\ref{c3-chap}).  Section~\ref{sec:volumes} treats the linear algebra in its simplest setting; Sections~\ref{sec:one-lift}--\ref{sec:factor} assemble the theorem.  Section~\ref{sec:aux} recalls the auxiliary forms of Chapter~\ref{c5-chap} (Theorem~\ref{c5-thm:aux}) and proves a height bound for the ratios (Theorem~\ref{thm:small-ratios}); both are used in the proof of Theorem~\ref{tc-thm:C}.

\section{Volumes of maps}\label{sec:volumes}

For a linear map \(u \colon \mathcal E \to \mathcal E'\) of finite-dimensional complex inner product spaces let
\[
  Z(u) := \det\nolimits_{\mathcal E} (u^* u) ,
\]
the determinant on the domain \(\mathcal E\); we call it the \emph{volume} of \(u\).  Dimensions are complex dimensions, the determinant on the zero space is \(1\), and inner products are conjugate-linear in the first argument and linear in the second.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:gram}
For every orthonormal basis \(e_1, \dots, e_d\) of \(\mathcal E\), \(Z(u) = \det\bigl(\langle u e_a, u e_b \rangle\bigr)_{a,b}\).  The number \(Z(u)\) is real and \(\ge 0\), and \(Z(u) > 0\) if and only if \(u\) is injective.
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:scalar-gram}
Let \(\mathcal R\) be an endomorphism of \(\mathcal E\) and \(a \in \C\) with \(\langle ux, uy\rangle = a \langle x, \mathcal R y\rangle\) for all \(x, y \in \mathcal E\).  Then \(Z(u) = a^{\dim \mathcal E} \det \mathcal R\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:square-chain}
If \(u_j \colon \mathcal E_{j-1} \to \mathcal E_j\), for \(1 \le j \le m\), are linear maps between spaces of one and the same dimension, then \(Z(u_m \circ \dots \circ u_1) = \prod_{j=1}^m Z(u_j)\).
\end{problem}

When the dimension grows along the chain, equality can fail.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:position}
Let \(u_2 \colon \C^2 \to \C^2\) be \(\operatorname{diag}(1, 2)\), and let \(u_1, u_1' \colon \C \to \C^2\) be \(x \mapsto (x, 0)\) and \(x \mapsto (0, x)\).  Then \(Z(u_1) = Z(u_1') = 1\) and \(Z(u_2) = 4\), while \(Z(u_2u_1) = 1\) and \(Z(u_2u_1') = 4\).
\end{problem}

For the lower chain restricted to \(T\), Theorem~\ref{c4-thm:chain-gram} gives an identity for its Gram form on \(T\).  Problem~\ref{c4-pr:push-down} is a model of how such an identity can be propagated along a chain.

For \(a \ge 0\) divisible by \(4\), the two lifts from \(\mathrm U(1)\) of Chapter~\ref{tn-chap} used here have a one-dimensional source.  The lift \(\Theta_{(a)}\) from \(\mathrm U(1)\) to \(\mathrm U(1)\) is multiplication by \(c_a/4\) (Problem~\ref{tn-pr:U1U1}), and by Problem~\ref{tn-pr:rallis} the lift \(\Theta_{(a)}\) from \(\mathrm U(1)\) to \(\mathrm U(2)\) satisfies
\[
  \lVert \Theta_{(a)}\varphi \rVert^2 = \lambda_a \lVert \varphi \rVert^2 , \qquad
  \lambda_a := \frac{1}{128} \sum_j \binom{a}{j} c_j^2 c_{a-j}^2 .
\]
This finite calculation illustrates the inner product of a lift with itself; the strict rank inequality of the inner product formula (Theorem~\ref{c1-thm:rallis}) first allows a source of rank \(1\) when the target has rank \(3\), the case with which Chapter~\ref{c1-chap} begins.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:smallest}
For \(4 \mid a\), the composite of the lift \(\mathrm U(1) \to \mathrm U(1)\) with the lift \(\mathrm U(1) \to \mathrm U(2)\) of Chapter~\ref{tn-chap}, in degree \(a\), has volume \(\lambda_a c_a^2/16\), the product of the two volumes.
\end{problem}

\section{One lift}\label{sec:one-lift}

For \(1 \le j \le m\) and a tame character \(\xi\), the single lift \(\hat u_{\xi,j}\) on \(H(W_j)\) and its restriction \(u_{\xi,j}\) to \(H_g(W_j)\) are those of Section~\ref{ob-sec:chain}; \(H(V)\) carries the Petersson product (Definition~\ref{ob-def:petersson}).  We recall two statements.  By Theorem~\ref{c1-thm:one-lift}, for every \(1 \le j \le m\) there is \(a_j \in \C\), \(a_j \ne 0\), such that for every tame character \(\xi\),
\[
  \hat u_{\xi,j}^*\, \hat u_{\xi,j} = a_j\, c_{Q_\xi}\, \Rc_j(\xi; W_j)
\]
on \(H(W_j)\), with \(c_Q\) the twist factor of Definition~\ref{c1-def:twist-factor} (a power of \(\Nm Q_\xi\) times a product of factors \(1 - q_w^{-v}\) over \(w \in Q_\xi\), with \(c_\varnothing = 1\)) and \(\Rc_j(\xi; V)\) the Rallis operator of Definition~\ref{c1-def:rallis-operator} (a convergent sum of right translations by finite adelic representatives supported outside the places of \(\mathcal M \cup Q_\xi\) and their conjugates, with coefficients that do not depend on \(V\)).  For the same \(j\) and \(\xi\), \(\hat u_{\xi,j}\) is injective (Chapter~\ref{c1-chap}, Section~\ref{c1-sec:theorem}).  By Corollary~\ref{c2-cor:equal}, for \(1 \le j \le m\): \(\dim H_g(W_j) = d_T\), and for every tame character \(\xi\), \(\Rc_j(\xi; Y_0)\) maps \(T\) to itself, \(\Rc_j(\xi; W_j)\) maps \(H_g(W_j)\) to itself, and
\[
  \det\bigl(\Rc_j(\xi; W_j) \mid H_g(W_j)\bigr) = \det\bigl(\Rc_j(\xi; Y_0) \mid T\bigr) .
\]

\begin{problem}\label{pr:one-lift}
For \(1 \le j \le m\) and every tame character \(\xi\), \(Z(u_{\xi,j}) = (a_j c_{Q_\xi})^{d_T} \det\bigl(\Rc_j(\xi; Y_0) \mid T\bigr)\).  Moreover \(Z(u_{\xi,j}) > 0\).
\end{problem}

\section{Twists and the ratios}\label{sec:twists}

Tame characters \(\xi\), their sets \(Q_\xi\) and powers \(\xi^n\), twists and \(m\)-fold twists \(\vartheta\) are as in Chapter~\ref{ob-chap}; the power \(\xi^n\) has the same set \(Q_\xi\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:powers}
If \(\vartheta\) is an \(m\)-fold twist and \(1 \le n \le m\), then \(\vartheta^n\) is a twist with the same set \(Q_\vartheta\), so \(c_{Q_{\vartheta^n}} = c_{Q_\vartheta}\).
\end{problem}

For \(1 \le i < j \le m\) and an \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\), \(\rho_{ij}(\vartheta)\) is the ratio of Definition~\ref{c4-def:rho}: with \(\nu := \mu_j^0/\mu_i^0\),
\[
  \rho_{ij}(\vartheta) = \prod_{z=1}^{t} \frac{\lvert L^{\mathcal M \cup Q_\vartheta}\bigl(z, (\nu\vartheta^{j-i})^{-1}\bigr)\rvert}{\lvert L^{\mathcal M}(z, \nu^{-1})\rvert} ,
\]
a nonnegative real number (a quotient by \(0\) being \(0\), as in Definition~\ref{c4-def:rho}).  These ratios enter the Gram identity of the chain through the exchange identity (Theorem~\ref{c3-thm:exchange}).  In the direct square for \(i < j\), the squared modulus of the exchange scalar is a constant independent of \(\vartheta\) times \(\mathrm{ex}_t\bigl(\mathcal M \cup Q_\vartheta, (\nu\vartheta^{j-i})^{-1}\bigr)\) (Definition~\ref{c3-def:exchange-factor} and Problem~\ref{c4-pr:square-char}).  There is one such square for each pair \(1 \le i < j \le m\).

By Problem~\ref{c4-pr:rho-pair}, \(\rho_{ij}(1) = 1\) for \(1 \le i < j \le m\).

\section{The factorization}\label{sec:factor}

For an \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\), the lower chain \(\Theta_\vartheta\) of Section~\ref{ob-sec:chain}, restricted to \(T\), takes values in a space of algebraic level forms on \(Y_m\) with the class-sum inner product; it is injective (Problem~\ref{c4-pr:theta-injective}).  In the notation of Section~\ref{sec:volumes}, \(Z(\vartheta) = Z(\Theta_\vartheta|_T)\) and \(Z_j(\vartheta) = Z(u_{\vartheta^j, j})\).  Let \(\Rc_\vartheta = \Rc_1(\vartheta; Y_0)\,\Rc_2(\vartheta^2; Y_0) \cdots \Rc_m(\vartheta^m; Y_0)\) be the endomorphism of \(T\) of Definition~\ref{c4-def:rallis-product}.  By Theorem~\ref{c4-thm:chain-gram} there is a function \(c\) from \(m\)-fold twists to \(\C\) with \(c \doteq \prod_{1 \le i < j \le m}\rho_{ij}\), that is (Definition~\ref{c4-def:doteq}), with a real number \(C'\) and an integer \(e'\) such that \(\lvert c(\vartheta)\rvert^2 = C'(\Nm Q_\vartheta)^{e'}\bigl(\prod_{1 \le i < j \le m}\rho_{ij}(\vartheta)\bigr)^2\) for every \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\), and such that for every \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\) and all \(x, y \in T\),
\[
  \langle \Theta_\vartheta x, \Theta_\vartheta y\rangle = c(\vartheta)\,c_{Q_\vartheta}^m\,\langle x, \Rc_\vartheta y\rangle .
\]

\begin{problem}\label{pr:abs-ratio}
Let \(\Sigma\) be a set with a point \(1\), let \(\nu \colon \Sigma \to \R_{>0}\) and \(f \colon \Sigma \to \R_{\ge 0}\) with \(\nu(1) = f(1) = 1\), and let \(c \colon \Sigma \to \C\), \(C' \in \R\) and \(e' \in \Z\) satisfy \(\lvert c(x) \rvert^2 = C' \nu(x)^{e'} f(x)^2\) for all \(x\).  If \(c(1) \ne 0\), then \(C' = \lvert c(1)\rvert^2\) and \(\lvert c(x)/c(1) \rvert = \nu(x)^{e'/2} f(x)\) for all \(x\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:chain-det}
In Theorem~\ref{c4-thm:chain-gram}, if \(c(1) = 0\) then \(T = 0\).  For every \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\),
\[
  Z(\vartheta) = \Bigl( c(\vartheta) \Big/ \prod_{j=1}^m a_j \Bigr)^{d_T} \prod_{j=1}^m Z_j(\vartheta) ,
\]
and \(Z(\vartheta) > 0\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:quotient}
If \(T \ne 0\), then for every \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\),
\[
  \frac{Z(\vartheta)}{Z(1)} = \Bigl( (\Nm Q_\vartheta)^{e'/2} \prod_{1 \le i < j \le m} \rho_{ij}(\vartheta) \Bigr)^{d_T} \prod_{j=1}^m \frac{Z_j(\vartheta)}{Z_j(1)} .
\]
If \(T = 0\), both sides equal \(1\) (a real number to the power \(0\) is \(1\)).
\end{problem}

The normalized determinant identity is the following.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
For the data of Chapter~\ref{da-chap} there is an integer \(e_Z\) such that for every \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\):
\begin{enumerate}
\item[(a)] \(0 < Z(\vartheta)\);
\item[(b)] \(0 < Z_j(\vartheta)\) for every integer \(j\) with \(1 \le j \le m\);
\item[(c)] in \(\C\),
\[
  \frac{Z(\vartheta)}{Z(1)} = \biggl( (\Nm Q_\vartheta)^{e_Z/2} \prod_{i=1}^{m} \prod_{j=i+1}^{m} \rho_{ij}(\vartheta) \biggr)^{d_T} \prod_{j=1}^m \frac{Z_j(\vartheta)}{Z_j(1)} .
\]
\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

Here \(Z(\vartheta)\) and \(Z_j(\vartheta)\) are complex numbers by definition, and (a), (b) say that they are real and positive; the base of the power \(d_T\) in (c) is a real number, with \(e_Z/2\) a real exponent, and \(e_Z\) does not depend on \(\vartheta\).  Problems~\ref{pr:one-lift} and~\ref{pr:abs-ratio}--\ref{pr:quotient} prove Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.  One may take \(e_Z = e'\) when \(T \ne 0\).  Thus the volume of the chain at a twist, relative to the trivial twist, is expressed through \(m\) volumes of single lifts, a power of \(\Nm Q_\vartheta\), and \(m(m-1)/2\) ratios \(\rho_{ij}\) of \(L\)-values.

\section{Auxiliary forms}\label{sec:aux}

The proof of Theorem~\ref{tc-thm:C} extends a linearly independent family of target forms to a basis by auxiliary forms (Definition~\ref{c5-def:aux}).  By Theorem~\ref{c5-thm:aux} there are an integer \(e_\gamma\) and a function \(\gamma\) from the \(m\)-fold twists to \(\C\), with \(\gamma(\vartheta) \ne 0\) for every \(\vartheta\), \(\gamma(1) = 1\) and
\[
  \lvert\gamma(\vartheta)\rvert = (\Nm Q_\vartheta)^{e_\gamma/2} \prod_{0 \le i, j < m} \rho'_{ij}(\vartheta)
\]
for every \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\), such that \(T\) is spanned over \(\C\) by the forms that are auxiliary for \(\gamma\).  Here \(\rho'_{ij}\), \(0 \le i, j < m\), are the turn ratios of Definition~\ref{c5-def:turn-ratio}.  Theorem~\ref{thm:small-ratios} below is proved by Problems~\ref{pr:count-ratios} and~\ref{pr:norm-height}, with Problems~\ref{c4-pr:rho-pair} and~\ref{c5-pr:turn-pair}.

\begin{theorem}[small ratios]\label{thm:small-ratios}
Let \(\theta\) be a family of \(m\)-fold twists with small ratios (Definition~\ref{l5-def:small-ratios}).  For every integer \(e\), the families
\[
  \iota \mapsto \Bigl((\Nm Q_{\theta_\iota})^{e/2}\prod_{1 \le i < j \le m}\rho_{ij}(\theta_\iota)\Bigr)^{-1}
  \quad\text{and}\quad
  \iota \mapsto \Bigl((\Nm Q_{\theta_\iota})^{e/2}\prod_{0 \le i, j < m}\rho'_{ij}(\theta_\iota)\Bigr)^{-1}
\]
have height at most \(m^2tx_1\).
\end{theorem}

By Problem~\ref{c4-pr:rho-pair} each \(\rho_{ij}\) is a product of \(t\) ratios \(R_P(\vartheta^{j-i}, 1)\) with \(P\) a ramified critical pair.  By Problem~\ref{c5-pr:turn-pair}, for \(0 \le i, j < m\) each \(\rho'_{ij}(\vartheta)\) is the product of the \(t\) ratios \(R_{P_z}(\vartheta^{j+1}, 1)\), \(1 \le z \le t\), with \(P_z := (\mu^0_{i+1}\mu^0_{j+1}, z)\) a ramified critical pair and \(1 \le j + 1 \le m\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:count-ratios}
Let \(\theta\) be a family of \(m\)-fold twists such that, for every \(1 \le n \le m\) and every ramified critical pair \(P\), the ratios \(R_P(\theta_\iota^n, 1)\) are nonzero and the family \(\iota \mapsto R_P(\theta_\iota^n, 1)^{-1}\) has height at most \(x_1\).  Then the families \(\iota \mapsto \bigl(\prod_{1 \le i < j \le m}\rho_{ij}(\theta_\iota)\bigr)^{-1}\) and \(\iota \mapsto \bigl(\prod_{0 \le i, j < m}\rho'_{ij}(\theta_\iota)\bigr)^{-1}\) have height at most \(m^2tx_1\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:norm-height}
Let \(\theta = (\theta_\iota)\) be a family of \(m\)-fold twists.  Suppose that for every prime \(\ell\) and every \(\iota \mid \ell\) one has \(\ell \nmid \Nm Q_{\theta_\iota}\).  Then for every integer \(e\) the family \(\iota \mapsto (\Nm Q_{\theta_\iota})^{-e/2}\) has height at most \(0\).  Deduce Theorem~\ref{thm:small-ratios}.
\end{problem}
