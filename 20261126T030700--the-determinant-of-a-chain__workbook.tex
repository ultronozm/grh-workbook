\title{The determinant of a chain}

\paragraph{Prerequisites.}
Chapters~\ref{c7-chap}, \ref{c21-chap}, \ref{c22-chap} and~\ref{c32-chap}.

\medskip
The Gram determinant of the target forms under a chain of theta lifts factors, after division by its value at the trivial twist, into the determinants of single lifts and ratios of Hecke values.  The chapter states the Rallis inner product formula and the exchange identity that give the factorization, and explains why \(m\)-fold twists are needed.  The simplest settings are the chain of Chapter~\ref{c7-chap} and the norm of one definite lift in Chapter~\ref{c22-chap}.
