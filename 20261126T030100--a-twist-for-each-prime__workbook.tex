\paragraph{Prerequisites.}
Chapter~\ref{c5-chap}.

\medskip
The method of Chapter~\ref{c5-chap} compares the size of one determinant with its divisibility.  In the proof the two are read at different points: sizes at the trivial twist, valuations at a twist that may depend on the prime.  This chapter treats a family of maps \(\Theta_\xi\) indexed by twists, the vectors that are integral at a prime \(\ell\) for every twist allowed at \(\ell\), and the inequality between the covolume and the index of such a family that replaces the product formula for one determinant.  The simplest setting is \(\C^2\) with two twists, the identity and a diagonal map involving a power of a prime \(p\).
