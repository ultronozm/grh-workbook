Prerequisites.

Chapter 52, Sections 52.1–52.3 and Definitions 52.4.1, 52.4.2: the lattice model, the Bessel function \(\mathcal{W}_{\lambda,\varphi}\), the lower lifts \(\Theta_{\xi,j}\) with actions \(\omega_j\), data \(\phi_j(\xi)\) and finite factors \(\mathbf1_j(\xi)\), their levels \(K^Y_j(\xi)\subseteq K^{Y\circ}_j\), and the definite lifts \(\Theta^X_{\xi,j}(v)\) and pair tensors. Chapter 53, Definitions 53.2.1–53.4.2: strings, \(R=\mathcal{U}\rtimes U(X)\) and transfer. Use the adelic frames of Section 37.3. For the moments \(c_a\) see Problems 23.2.1, 23.2.3 and 23.2.4. In this sheet \(A,s,b,w,u,l\) are local letters.

The proof of the seesaw identity (Chapter 52) transfers the Bessel period along \(Y_0\to\dots\to Y_m\) one lift at a time (Theorem 53.4.1). How does the resulting function become a lift between definite spaces? Take lifts \(2i+1,2i+2\) together, \(0\le i\le\varrho\). In the last pair the three string lengths are \(1,0,0\). We model this pair first.

§54.1. The last pair in the lattice model

In the model of Chapter 52 take \(a=8\), \(j=4\), \(X=\mathcal{O}e_1\), \(Y=X\perp\mathcal{O}e_2=I_2\). Let \(\varphi_4(X)=e_1^4\), with Poincaré image \(P\varphi_4\in\mathcal{A}_{(8)}(2)\). The model for \(Y_{m-2},Y_{m-1},Y_m\) is \(Y,X'=I_1,Y''=I_2\), with \(X''=Y''\perp\mathcal{O}e''_3=I_3\) for \(X_m\). Use the bases \(e'\) of \(X'\) and \(e''_1,e''_2\) of \(Y''\). Problems 52.2.2 and 52.3.1 give the composite along \(Y\to X'\to Y''\), \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:model-lower}\tag{1} \Theta^{(2)}P\varphi_4 = \Theta_{(8)}\bigl((\Theta_{(4)}\varphi_4)\cdot c_4 e'^4\bigr) = \tfrac1{16}c_4^2\bigl(c_0c_8\,{e''_1}^8 + 70\,c_4^2\,{e''_1}^4{e''_2}^4 + c_0c_8\,{e''_2}^8\bigr) . \end{equation}\] The line \(\mathcal{O}e_2\) contributes \(c_4e'^4=\sum_{v\in\mathcal{O}}(ve')^4e^{-\pi|v|^2}\). Write \(h(x,y)=\operatorname{tr}(y^*x)\) as in Chapter 23.

Problem 54.1.1. For \(x=(x_1,x_2)\in\operatorname{Hom}_\mathcal{O}(Y,X')\), let \(x_2^\sharp(e')=\bar v e''_3\) when \(x_2(e_2)=ve'\). Then \((x,w)\mapsto(x_1,w\oplus x_2^\sharp)\) is a bijection from \(\operatorname{Hom}_\mathcal{O}(Y,X')\times\operatorname{Hom}_\mathcal{O}(X',Y'')\) to \(\operatorname{Hom}_\mathcal{O}(X,X')\times\operatorname{Hom}_\mathcal{O}(X',X'')\) preserving \(h(x,x)+h(w,w)\).

Problem 54.1.2. Let \(l:\operatorname{Sym}^{12}(X''_\mathbb{C})\to\operatorname{Sym}^8(Y''_\mathbb{C})\) extract the coefficient of \({e''_3}^4\) and divide by \(495\). Then \(l\) commutes with \(\Gamma_{Y''}\) acting by \(\operatorname{diag}(h,1)\) on \(X''\), and \(l((we'+ue''_3)^{12})=(we')^8u^4\) for \(w\in\operatorname{Hom}(X',Y'')\), \(u\in\mathbb{C}\).

Problem 54.1.3. Let \(\vartheta_{12} := \sum_{x \in \operatorname{Hom}_\mathcal{O}(X', X'')} (xe')^{12} e^{-\pi h(x,x)} \in \operatorname{Sym}^{12}(X''_\mathbb{C})\). By machine, its coefficients of \({e''_1}^8{e''_3}^4\) and \({e''_1}^4{e''_2}^4{e''_3}^4\) are \(495\,c_0c_4c_8 = 24.7061089962\ldots\) and \(34650\,c_4^3 = 101.731037043\ldots\); and \(l(\vartheta_{12}) = c_4\bigl(c_0c_8\,{e''_1}^8 + 70\,c_4^2\,{e''_1}^4{e''_2}^4 + c_0c_8\,{e''_2}^8\bigr)\).

Problem 54.1.4. With \(\psi := (\Theta_{(4)}\varphi_4)(X') = \tfrac14c_4e'^4\), \[\Theta^{(2)}P\varphi_4 = \tfrac14\, l\Bigl(\sum_{x \in \operatorname{Hom}_\mathcal{O}(X', X'')} \operatorname{Sym}^{12}(x)\bigl(\psi\,e'^8\bigr)\, e^{-\pi h(x,x)}\Bigr) .\]

The definite chain lifts from \(X\) in weight four, multiplies by \(e'^8\), lifts to \(X''\) in weight twelve and projects by \(l\). The adjoint in Problem 54.1.1 leaves the real moment \(c_4\) unchanged. In the last pair of the chain the string vector has norm \(-N\).

§54.2. Stage functions, and the last pair in the proof

For \(j<m\), \(Y_j\) has definite part \(X_j\), string length \(m-1-j\) and Bessel subgroup \(R_j\). The terminal \(Y_m\) is definite of rank \(n_m-1\), with \(R_m=U(Y_m)\). Fix \(\varphi\in S\), a linear form \(\lambda\) on its values, a tame \(\xi\), and points \((g_j,y_j)\in U(Y_j)(\mathbb{A}_\infty)\times U(Y_j)(\mathbb{A}_f)\) for \(j\ge0\).

Definition 54.2.1. Let \(\mathcal{W}^{\mathrm{tr}}_0 := \mathcal{W}_{\lambda,\varphi}\) on \(R_0(\mathbb{A})\). For \(0 \le j \le m - 1\) let \[\mathcal{W}_{j+1}(h) := \int_{[R_j]} \mathcal{W}^{\mathrm{tr}}_j(r)\,\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{st}_{j+1}}\bigl(\omega_{j+1}(r\,(g_j, y_j), h)\,\phi_{j+1}(\xi)\bigr)\,dr\] on \(U(Y_{j+1})(\mathbb{A})\). For \(j + 1 < m\), \(\mathcal{W}^{\mathrm{tr}}_{j+1}(r) := \mathcal{W}_{j+1}(r\,(g_{j+1}, y_{j+1}))\) on \(R_{j+1}(\mathbb{A})\). We call the functions \(\mathcal{W}^{\mathrm{tr}}_j\) the stage functions.

Used in: Chapter 55

As in Definition 53.4.2, an equivariant function on \(R_j(\mathbb{A})\) is continuous, left \(R_j(F)\)-invariant and transforms by \(\psi_\mathcal{U}(n)\) under left translation by \(n\in\mathcal{U}(\mathbb{A})\) with \(n-1\) of degree \(\ge2\).

Definition 54.2.2 (conjugate type, transpose). Replace the type kernel for lower-chain forms by its complex conjugate to obtain the conjugate type. For \(0\le j<m\) define the transpose of a form \(z\) of the conjugate type on \(Y_{j+1}\) to be the form on \(Y_j\) given by \[(\Theta_{\xi,j+1}^{\top} z)(g) := [K^{Y\circ}_j : K^Y_j(\xi)] \int_{[U(Y_{j+1})]} \theta\bigl(\omega_{j+1}(g, h)\,\phi_{j+1}(\xi)\bigr)\, z(h)\,dh .\]

Read from the proof (A) [GL, UnfoldingStages, line 657, and SeesawIdentity, lines 168–201, 260]. For \(0\le j<m\), \(\mathcal{W}^{\mathrm{tr}}_j\) is equivariant. Every form \(z\) of the conjugate type on \(Y_{j+1}\) is automorphic and rapidly decreasing, and \(\Theta^\top_{\xi,j+1}z\) has the conjugate type on \(Y_j\). For every form \(f\) of the lower chain on \(Y_j\), \(\int_{[U(Y_{j+1})]}z\,\Theta_{\xi,j+1}f=\int_{[U(Y_j)]}(\Theta^\top_{\xi,j+1}z)\,f\).

Problem 54.2.3. For \(0 \le j \le m-1\) and every form \(z\) of the conjugate type on \(Y_{j+1}\), the integral on the right converges absolutely and \[\int_{[R_j]} \mathcal{W}^{\mathrm{tr}}_j(r)\,(\Theta^{\top}_{\xi,j+1} z)\bigl(r\,(g_j, y_j)\bigr)\,dr = [K^{Y\circ}_j : K^Y_j(\xi)] \int_{R_{j+1}(F)\backslash U(Y_{j+1})(\mathbb{A})} \mathcal{W}_{j+1}\, z .\]

For pair \(i\) write \((V,V',V'')=(Y_{2i},Y_{2i+1},Y_{2i+2})\), with definite parts \(X=X_{2i},X'=X_{2i+1},X''_0\) and actions \(\omega,\omega'\) for the two lifts. Write \(V^-\) for \(V\) with its form negated. Problem 54.2.4. In a hermitian line over \(\mathbb Q(\mathrm{i})\) with basis vector \(e\) of norm \(5\), the vector \(e/(2+\mathrm{i})\) has norm \(1\). In the last pair of the chain, \(f_{\mathrm{mid}}/\pi_N\) has norm \(1\) in \(Y_{m-2}^-\). The part of grade zero of \(Y_{m-2}^- \oplus Y_m\), which is the whole space, is \(X_{m-2}^- \oplus X_m\) with \(X_m = Y_m \perp E\,f_{\mathrm{mid}}/\pi_N\), and \(h \in U(Y_m)\) acts on \(X_m\) as \(\operatorname{diag}(h, 1)\).

Problem 54.2.5. In the last pair the only map of positive degree between any two of \(V, V', V''\) is zero; \(R_{m-2} = U(X_{m-2})\) and \(R_{m-1} = U(Y_{m-1})\); the string maps \(\mathrm{st}_{m-1}\), \(\mathrm{st}_m\) vanish; and \(\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{st}_{m-1}}\phi = \theta(\phi)\), \(\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{st}_m}\phi' = \theta(\phi')\) for all data.

So for the last pair Definition 54.2.1 gives \[\begin{equation} \label{eq:last-stage}\tag{2} \mathcal{W}_m\bigl(h\,(g_m, y_m)\bigr) = \int\!\!\int \mathcal{W}^{\mathrm{tr}}_{m-2}(\gamma)\,\theta\bigl(\omega(\gamma, \gamma')\phi\bigr)\,\theta\bigl(\omega'(\gamma', h)\phi'\bigr)\,d\gamma\,d\gamma' , \end{equation}\] with the Bessel quotient measures on \([R_{m-2}],[R_{m-1}]\) (Problem 54.2.5). Here \(\phi=\phi_{m-1}(\xi)^{g,y}\), \(\phi'=\phi_m(\xi)^{g,y}\), where \(\phi_j(\xi)^{g,y}:=\omega_j((g_{j-1},y_{j-1}),(g_j,y_j))\phi_j(\xi)\).

Read from the proof (B) [GL, TwoEdges, lines 71–78, 128, 147, 195]. The join carries \(\phi\) on \(\operatorname{Hom}(V,V')\) to \(\operatorname{Hom}(V',V^-)\) by a scaled adjoint and takes its product with \(\phi'\) on \(\operatorname{Hom}(V',V'')\). For some \(z_\theta\ne0\) depending only on the framed triple, \[\begin{gather*} \theta(\phi \boxtimes \phi') = z_\theta\,\theta(\phi)\,\theta(\phi') , \qquad \mathrm{Heis}\tbinom{w^*}{w'}(\phi \boxtimes \phi') = \mathrm{Heis}(w)\phi \boxtimes \mathrm{Heis}(w')\phi' , \\ \omega(g, g')\phi \boxtimes \omega'(g', g'')\phi' = \omega^\triangle(g, g', g'')(\phi \boxtimes \phi') \end{gather*}\] for all data, adelic maps \(w:V\to V'\), \(w':V'\to V''\) in rational bases and adelic group elements \(g,g',g''\). Here \(\omega^\triangle\) is the Weil action on \(\operatorname{Hom}(V',V^-\oplus V'')\) times characters. The pair’s zero term \(\mathrm{zt}\) maps these data continuously and linearly to data on \(\operatorname{Hom}(X',X^-\oplus X_{2i+2})\), with \[\mathrm{zt}\bigl(\omega^\triangle(\gamma, \gamma', h)\,\phi^\triangle\bigr) = \omega^\triangle(\gamma, \gamma', \bar h)\,\mathrm{zt}(\phi^\triangle)\] for \(\gamma,\gamma',h\) in the adelic unitary groups of \(X,X',X''_0\), acting through the definite parts. On the right the triple is \(X,X',X_{2i+2}\), with \(\bar h=h\) in a middle pair and \(\bar h=\operatorname{diag}(h,1)\) in the last. The value \(\theta(\mathrm{zt}\,\phi^\triangle)\) is the mean of \(\theta(\mathrm{Heis}(b)\phi^\triangle)\) over adelic maps modulo rational maps as in Section 54.3. In the last pair only \(b=0\) occurs [GL, EdgePairEvaluation, lines 844, 929, and ZeroTerm, line 141].

Problem 54.2.6. In the last pair, for all data \(\phi, \phi'\), \(\gamma \in U(X_{m-2})(\mathbb{A})\), \(\gamma' \in U(Y_{m-1})(\mathbb{A})\) and \(h \in U(Y_m)(\mathbb{A})\), \[\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{st}_{m-1}}\bigl(\omega(\gamma, \gamma')\phi\bigr)\,\mathrm{CT}_{\mathrm{st}_m}\bigl(\omega'(\gamma', h)\phi'\bigr) = z_\theta^{-1}\,\theta\Bigl(\omega^\triangle\bigl(\gamma, \gamma', \operatorname{diag}(h, 1)\bigr)\,\mathrm{zt}(\phi \boxtimes \phi')\Bigr) .\]

In (2) the two kernels have thus become one on the definite triple, with factor \(z_\theta^{-1}\).

§54.3. Middle pairs: three strings

For \(i<\varrho\) the three string lengths are \(s+1,s,s-1\), where \(s=2(\varrho-i)\). Now the string maps are nonzero and the constant terms average over maps of positive degree.

Definition 54.3.1. Let \(V, V', V''\) be spaces with strings for the same \(N\), of lengths \(s+1, s, \max(s-1, 0)\) \((s \ge 0)\), with string maps \(\mathrm{st}_1 \colon V \to V'\) and \(\mathrm{st}_2 \colon V' \to V''\). Define the stacked string map by \(A := \binom{\mathrm{st}_1^*}{\mathrm{st}_2} \colon V' \to V^- \oplus V''\).

For the adelic identities use the frames and Weil data of Chapter 53. With these data the theta product formula of (B) gives \(z_\theta\ne0\) for any framed triple. In the last pair \(A=0\). For the two matrix calculations in Problems 54.3.2 and 54.3.3, work with hermitian spaces over \(E=\mathbb Q(\mathrm{i})\). Take \(N=5\), \(c_V=c_{V'}=c_{V''}=1\), no definite parts, \(J_V=\mathrm{antidiag}(1,-5,1)\), \(J_{V'}=\mathrm{antidiag}(1,1)\) and \(J_{V''}=(-5)\).

Problem 54.3.2. \(\mathrm{st}_1 = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 1&0&0\\ 0&-5&0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\), \(\mathrm{st}_2 = (1, 0)\), and \(\mathrm{st}_1\mathrm{st}_1^* = \mathrm{st}_2^*\mathrm{st}_2 = \bigl(\begin{smallmatrix} 0&0\\ -5&0 \end{smallmatrix}\bigr)\).

Problem 54.3.3. \(A\) has rows \((0,0)\), \((1,0)\), \((0,1)\), \((1,0)\), and \(A^*A = 0\) for the form \(-J_V \oplus J_{V''}\).

Problem 54.3.4. Let \(s \ge 1\) and \(c_V = c_{V'}\). Then \(\mathrm{st}_1\mathrm{st}_1^* = \mathrm{st}_2^*\mathrm{st}_2\); this map kills the definite part and \(f'_{s-1}\) and sends \(f'_q\) to a multiple of \(f'_{q+1}\) for \(q < s - 1\). The constants of \(Y_j\) agree for \(j<m\), and \(c_{Y_m}=1\) [GL, EdgePairEvaluation, lines 1144–1162]. For adelic \(w:V\to V'\), \(w':V'\to V''\) and all data, \[\mathrm{CT}_w \phi \cdot \mathrm{CT}_{w'} \phi' = z_\theta^{-1}\,\mathrm{zt}_{>0}\bigl(\mathrm{Heis}(w)\phi \boxtimes \mathrm{Heis}(w')\phi'\bigr) .\]

§54.4. After each pair

Theorem 54.4.1 (two lifts at a time). Fix \(\lambda\), archimedean points \(g_k\) of \(U(Y_k)\) \((k \ge 0)\) and \(0 \le i \le \varrho\).
