We present the arguments of Alpöge's [[https://alpo.ge/remark.pdf][proof]] of the generalized Riemann hypothesis for Dirichlet $L$-functions as a series of exercise sheets.  The sheets first introduce each ingredient in its simplest setting, then develop the proof following the [[https://github.com/leanprover-community/riemann-hypothesis][Lean code]].  The presentation is inspired by the problem sequences of Onishchik and Vinberg, /Lie Groups and Algebraic Groups/ (Springer, 1990).  The sheets are drafts written and refereed by AI agents (Claude, Codex and Gemini).

Updated continuously; last updated {{{updated}}} ([[https://github.com/ultronozm/grh-workbook/blob/main/STATUS.md][changelog]]).

The sheets prove the deductions and, for most inputs, a simplest instance or a model; the general inputs are stated from the Lean proof, not proved, and are listed in the [[file:20261127T100000--overview__workbook.html#sec:trust][overview]].

* Part I. The target
- [[file:20261125T001000--zeros-to-ramp-sums__workbook.html][1. From zeros to a one-sided bound]] \\
  An upper bound for a sum over primes excludes zeros: for $\zeta$, and for Dirichlet $L$-functions through one-sided bounds for a sum over primes in a progression with weight $1 - \chi_d$, for every prime $d$ of a class.
- [[file:20261125T001100--inert-primes-and-estimate-b__workbook.html][2. Inert primes and the choice of $d$]] \\
  What the weight $1 - \chi_d$ counts, and a prime $d$ for which the twists by $\chi_d$ of all characters of a given modulus have no zero in a given box.
- [[file:20261125T005000--galois-fourier-and-averaging__workbook.html][3. The places, the weights and the remainder]] \\
  Prime sums over the places of $E$ with two weights; the total remainder is an exact combination of $R_{q_0}(x)$, $R_{q_0}(x_1)$ and $R_1(x_1)$.
- [[file:20261125T005500--columns-from-a-sphere__workbook.html][4. Places from a sphere]] \\
  The places of weight $b$: dense, and sparse along multiplication by small primes, after Behrend.

- [[file:20261126T030100--a-twist-for-each-prime__workbook.html][6. A twist for each prime]] \\

- [[file:20261125T007000--stepanov-without-multiplicity__workbook.html][7. Stepanov without multiplicity]] \\

- [[file:20261125T003000--the-chain-and-the-square-root__workbook.html][8. The chain and the square root]] \\
  Stepanov's method, a chain of $m$ maps in place of an order of vanishing, and why $m \approx \sqrt{x}$.
- [[file:20261125T010000--rank-lemma-and-zero-estimate__workbook.html][9. Few translates span]] \\
  A second moment over a finite group bounds how many translates of one map are needed to span almost all of the target.
- [[file:20261125T010500--poincare-series-and-near-orthogonality__workbook.html][10. Poincaré series and near orthogonality]] \\

- [[file:20261124T214600--the-isotropic-plane__workbook.html][16. The isotropic plane]] \\

* Part IV. Sizes, periods and counts
- [[file:20261125T009000--kronecker-limit-and-chowla-selberg__workbook.html][20. The Kronecker limit formula and Chowla–Selberg]] \\
  Kronecker's limit formula and Lerch's formula compute $L'(1,\chi)/L(1,\chi)$ in two ways, and $e^{\gamma - L'(1,\chi)/L(1,\chi)}$ is, up to an algebraic factor, the square of a CM period divided by $\pi^2$.
- [[file:20261125T002000--definite-theta-lifts__workbook.html][21. Masses and the count of forms]] \\
  The number of forms on a definite unitary group as an average over stabilizers, whose main term is a mass times a Weyl dimension.
- [[file:20261125T015500--siegel-weil-in-the-smallest-case__workbook.html][22. The Siegel–Weil formula in the smallest case]] \\
  Siegel's theorem: the mass-weighted average of theta series over a genus is an Eisenstein series, checked for even unimodular lattices and the rank-five class set of [[file:20261125T002000--definite-theta-lifts__workbook.html][/Masses and the count of forms/]]; the norm of a rank-one lift through averaged representation numbers.
- [[file:20261125T002100--definite-theta-lifts-and-norms__workbook.html][23. Definite theta lifts and their norms]] \\
  Theta lifts between definite unitary groups in the smallest cases: their entries and norms, the seesaw, and chains.
- [[file:20261125T016500--hecke-operators-and-theta-lifts__workbook.html][24. Hecke operators and theta lifts]] \\
  Eichler's commutation relation, $p$-neighbours and Brandt matrices.
- [[file:20261125T016700--divisibility-along-the-chain__workbook.html][25. Divisibility along the chain]] \\
  Carrying a gain along a chain of lifts by moving a Hecke operator through it, and the step of the proof that does this.

* Part V. Denominators and congruences
- [[file:20261125T004000--denominators-and-sha__workbook.html][26. Denominators and the Stickelberger element]] \\

- [[file:20261125T004100--tate-twists-and-sha__workbook.html][27. Odd Tate twists]] \\

- [[file:20261126T020430--the-minus-part-by-gauss-sums__workbook.html][28. Odd classes by Gauss sums]] \\
  One integer, from Stickelberger elements and Gauss sums, bounds the length of the module of odd cocycles at every $\ell$ and every twist in the range used.
- [[file:20261125T014100--congruence-modules__workbook.html][29. Congruence modules]] \\
  The cost of projecting to an eigenspace: in the lattice of all level-one forms of weight 12, the congruence module between $\Delta$ and the Eisenstein line is $\mathbb{Z}/691$.
- [[file:20261125T014500--eisenstein-congruences-and-selmer-classes__workbook.html][30. Eisenstein congruences and Selmer classes]] \\
  Ribet's argument: a congruence with an Eisenstein series produces a Selmer class over $\mathbb{Q}$ and an unramified extension of a cyclotomic field.
- [[file:20261125T014700--pseudocharacters-and-the-length-bound__workbook.html][31. Pseudocharacters and the length bound]] \\
  Traces as pseudocharacters: a congruence gives a Selmer class, so Selmer lengths bound congruences; cocycles that obey the relations of a representation.
- [[file:20261125T011000--galois-representations-black-box__workbook.html][32. Black box: Galois representations of eigenforms]] \\
  The Galois representations attached to eigenforms on definite unitary groups, and the Hecke polynomial annihilating Frobenius at split spherical places away from $\ell$.
- [[file:20261126T080700--black-box-galois-cohomology-of-number-fields__workbook.html][33. Black box: Galois cohomology of number fields]] \\
  Local duality, Chebotarev and the Greenberg–Wiles formula, with cubic characters over $\mathbf Q$ and the relation count for tied primes.
- [[file:20261126T080800--black-box-crystalline-lattices-and-the-condition-above-l__workbook.html][34. Black box: crystalline lattices and the condition above $\ell$]] \\
  Ranks of crystalline cocycles, their reductions and local duality, with Tate twists as examples.
- [[file:20261126T080900--black-box-galois-representations-of-eigenforms-ii__workbook.html][35. Black box: Galois representations of eigenforms, II]] \\
  The comparison at spherical places above $\ell$, tame inertia and the integral operator at Taylor–Wiles places.

* Part VI. Data, spaces and the main inequality
Each chapter of Parts VI–IX states its results in the form of the Lean proof, at the commit named under [[*Sources][Sources]], and says which statements were read from the code and which are derived here.
- [[file:20261126T030300--the-data__workbook.html][36. The data]] \\
  The field, the places, the scales, the ranks and the level primes; their sizes, the amounts that the proof compares, and that such data exist for every sufficiently large integer $x$.
- [[file:20261126T040100--spaces-forms-and-twists__workbook.html][37. Spaces, forms and twists]] \\
  The spaces, forms, twists, characters and lifts that the proof attaches to the data, each defined once: chosen places and good forms, the target and source forms, tame characters and twists, the chain and the single lifts, critical pairs and the target lattice.
- [[file:20261126T031100--theorem-c-and-the-main-inequality__workbook.html][38. The main inequality]] \\
  Theorems A, B and C give the main inequality $m\,\mathrm{rem} \le fa\log^5 b$, and from it the one-sided bound and the theorem.

* Part VII. Theorem B: enough forms
- [[file:20261126T020210--bessel-poincare-series-and-their-gram-operators__workbook.html][39. Theorem B: enough forms]] \\
  Bessel–Poincaré series and their translates; a second moment shows that a few translates span almost all of the good target forms.
- [[file:20261126T051900--unfolding-twice-gram-operators-as-sums-over-rational-points__workbook.html][40. Unfolding twice: Gram operators as sums over rational points]] \\
  Petersson's formula unfolded twice: each Gram operator of the scaled Poincaré map is a sum over rational points, and the Bessel subgroup acts through a representation; on a finite group the lower bound of the second moment is attained.
- [[file:20261126T081400--black-box-discrete-series-and-the-comparison-of-traces__workbook.html][41. Black box: discrete series and the comparison of traces]] \\
  Formal degrees, matrix coefficients and character identities for real unitary groups, with the comparison of traces for groups that differ only at the real places.
- [[file:20261126T081500--a-bessel-function-at-a-real-place__workbook.html][42. A Bessel function at a real place]] \\
  A lower bound for a period and Schur orthogonality give the normalization and correlation.  The half-plane and two unitary groups illustrate how the position is chosen.
- [[file:20261126T052000--which-terms-survive-characters-minors-and-the-gain-at-the-level__workbook.html][43. Which terms survive: characters, minors and the congruence saving]] \\
  Characters and minors at the level primes remove all but a few rational points from the Gram operators near the Bessel subgroup, and the scaled Poincaré map is a near isometry.
- [[file:20261126T080300--size-gain-and-count-the-remaining-terms__workbook.html][44. Size, congruence saving and count: the remaining terms]] \\
  A bound on the discriminant of a common lattice gives the congruence saving for two terms with the same link. Counting pairs and bounding the tail give the two moment bounds for the data.
- [[file:20261126T052200--the-open-cell-siegels-formula-and-poisson-summation__workbook.html][45. The open cell: Siegel's formula and Poisson summation]] \\
  At an open reduction, a head and a residual isometry specify a double coset.  Siegel’s formula bounds the weighted sum over classes and vectors, and Poisson summation sums the heads.
- [[file:20261126T080400--the-sieve-over-the-entries__workbook.html][46. The sieve over the entries]] \\
  One step of Poisson summation on the integers modulo 25 models the recurrence over head entries, the ratios of successive leading minors of the pairing matrix. A sum over denominator ideals controls the nonzero frequencies.
- [[file:20261126T052300--positive-type-and-the-bootstrap-the-second-moment__workbook.html][47. Positive type and the bootstrap: the second moment]] \\
  Because the squared norms of the Gram operators form a function of positive type, the reductions with a singular minor carry little of the second moment; with the open cell this proves the second moment of Theorem B.
- [[file:20261126T052100--good-forms-and-the-count-of-the-target__workbook.html][48. Good forms and the count of the target]] \\
  Projected to the good forms, the scaled Poincaré map stays a near isometry; the target has dimension at most the order of the finite group times the degree and the mass.

* Part VIII. Theorem A: short forms
- [[file:20261126T030600--theorem-a-short-forms__workbook.html][49. Theorem A: short forms]] \\
  Integral forms whose translated images have short lifts at the trivial twist span the ramified source, and families of their translates have bounded index.
- [[file:20261126T080100--free-levels-and-short-bases__workbook.html][50. Free levels and short bases]] \\
  At a free level a form is determined by one vector per class. Short bases bound the number of classes and give short forms spanning the source.
- [[file:20261126T080200--the-good-part-and-the-ramified-forms__workbook.html][51. The good part and the ramified forms]] \\
  The scaled projection to good forms preserves integrality with a bounded cost in norm, and counting fixed forms bounds the dimension lost to ramification.
- [[file:20261126T030400--the-seesaw-identity__workbook.html][52. The seesaw identity]] \\
  On ramified source forms, the lower chain applied to a Poincaré image equals one nonzero constant, the same for all twists, times the restricted lift along a definite chain.
- [[file:20261126T051200--transferring-a-bessel-period-through-a-theta-lift__workbook.html][53. Transferring a Bessel period through a theta lift]] \\
  How a Bessel period passes through one theta lift: constant terms indexed by classes of rational maps, strings and the string map, and the transferred Bessel function.
- [[file:20261126T051300--two-lifts-at-a-time__workbook.html][54. Two lifts at a time]] \\
  The transfers grouped in pairs: the string maps of two consecutive lifts fit into one isotropic map, and after each pair the function left is a lift between the definite spaces; first worked out for the last pair in a lattice model.
- [[file:20261126T051400--lattices-cuts-and-the-ramified-forms__workbook.html][55. Lattices, cuts and the ramified forms]] \\
  The integrals over the remaining outer variables as finite sums over lattices and their cuts; on ramified source forms the seesaw holds with one tensor in place of a chain datum.
- [[file:20261126T051500--the-seesaw-tensor-translated-vacua-gluing-and-the-unit__workbook.html][56. The seesaw tensor: translated vacua, gluing and the unit]] \\
  Fourier inversion on a residue module, gluing of pair data and a common denominator express the tensor in glued vacuum tensors, which gives the seesaw identity with one nonzero constant.
- [[file:20261126T030500--divisibility-of-the-composite-lift__workbook.html][57. Divisibility of the composite lift]] \\
  For each prime and each embedding, on a submodule of small colength, the composite lifts of level prime to that prime are divisible by the predicted gain up to an error; the errors are small in total.
- [[file:20261126T050100--the-count-congruences-directions-and-factorials__workbook.html][58. The count: congruences, directions and factorials]] \\
  The jets of a series in the normal form along products of minors: the credit of the factorials against the debit of the couplings, for two places and in general, with charges on the rows for the columns removed at marked places.
- [[file:20261126T050200--one-lift-at-a-small-non-split-prime__workbook.html][59. One lift at a small non-split prime]] \\
  The kernel of one lift is divisible by the stage bound of its diagram, which falls short of $\mathrm{gain}(p)/m$ only by the collision sum and a uniform error.
- [[file:20261126T070800--the-normal-form-of-theta-derivatives-at-a-non-split-prime__workbook.html][60. The normal form of theta derivatives at a non-split prime]] \\
  The theta function of one CM curve gives a two-place model at 5. Its normal form and derivative count illustrate the theorem for general jet series.
- [[file:20261126T080600--frames-and-couplings-the-normal-form-at-all-places__workbook.html][61. Frames and couplings: the normal form at all places]] \\
  Powers of the linear forms of a frame give the couplings at all infinite places. The one-curve expansion then gives the normal form for jet series of standard data.
- [[file:20261126T051700--exceptional-primes-a-full-mark__workbook.html][62. Exceptional primes: a full mark]] \\
  At an exceptional prime the collisions can exceed the local loss allowance; a scaled Hecke operator of a full mark, applied before the chain, removes them at a bounded cost.
- [[file:20261126T060100--a-hecke-operator-is-nearly-onto__workbook.html][63. A Hecke operator is nearly onto]] \\
  The theorem on elementary divisors behind the cost of a full mark, stated from the Lean proof: its objects and its accounting, a model on two letters with the operator and its elementary divisors computed, and the deep forms that bound the two ends. The next chapter proves the root lemma for one letter and in the two-letter field model.
- [[file:20261126T080500--least-terms-and-pieris-inequalities__workbook.html][64. Least terms and Pieri's inequalities]] \\
  A count of roots gives the inequalities for least terms of binary forms and for the two-letter model over $\mathbb F_{25}$.
- [[file:20261126T051600--the-level-prime-and-the-ramified-forms__workbook.html][65. The level prime and the ramified forms]] \\
  At the level prime $N$ the glued pairs of the composite lift carry poles; on ramified forms an operator at $N$ turns each pair into two direct lifts, and the loss is bounded on a submodule of small colength.
- [[file:20261126T050300--the-size-of-the-composite-lift__workbook.html][66. The size of the composite lift]] \\
  At the trivial twist the composite lift multiplies conjugate norms by at most $\exp(h_{\mathrm{main}} - m\,\mathrm{rem} + 2\sum_p \mathrm{gain}(p)\log p + U)$: one factorial for each row of each lift, and the level $N$ pays for the long rows.

* Part IX. Theorem C: the lower bound
- [[file:20261126T040200--theorem-c__workbook.html][67. Theorem C]] \\
  A family spanning almost all target forms cannot have small covolume and small index together: the twisted lattice of the target, completed by auxiliary forms, with the cost of the single lifts and the factorization of the chain.
- [[file:20261126T030700--the-determinant-of-a-chain__workbook.html][68. The determinant of a chain]] \\
  The ratio of the Gram determinants of a chain of lifts at a twist and at the trivial twist factors into the same ratios for single lifts, ratios of Hecke $L$-values and a power of the norm of the modulus of the twist; the ratios have small height at a family with small ratios.
- [[file:20261126T050400--the-norm-of-one-lift__workbook.html][69. The norm of one lift]] \\
  The squared norm of a theta lift is a theta sum on the doubled space; by the Rallis inner product formula it is a scalar times a Hecke series, and the twist enters through one factor.
- [[file:20261126T050500--equal-multiplicities__workbook.html][70. Equal multiplicities]] \\
  One Hecke algebra acts on the good forms of two spaces of the same rank with different signs, with equal traces; hence equal dimensions, characteristic polynomials and joint eigenspaces.
- [[file:20261126T050600--the-exchange-identity__workbook.html][71. The exchange identity]] \\
  A theta integral over a large unitary group against one over a small group: removing a hyperbolic plane costs a product of partial Hecke $L$-values.  The identity is stated; the sheet works out its scalar.
- [[file:20261126T050700--squares-and-the-triangle__workbook.html][72. Squares and the triangle]] \\
  The Gram operator of the chain of lifts on the target, from a triangle of lifts, the inner product formula on its first edges and the comparison at the last level (three statements read from the proof).
- [[file:20261126T060200--turns-and-the-auxiliary-forms__workbook.html][73. Turns and the auxiliary forms]] \\
  The scalar of the auxiliary forms has $m^2$ turn ratios and the determinant has $m(m-1)/2$ pairwise factors.  A period is moved through two grids of spaces along paths, one exchange square for each grid square.  The sheet works out the paths, the hypotheses of each square and the deduction of the auxiliary forms; the transfer of periods and the forms with short lifts are stated from the proof.
- [[file:20261126T030800--the-cost-of-one-lift__workbook.html][74. The cost of one lift]] \\
  For a regular family of twists, one for each prime and each isomorphism above it, the ratios of the determinants of one lift at the trivial twist and at the twist have bounded height as a family.
- [[file:20261126T050800--compressions-and-false-lifts__workbook.html][75. Compressions and false lifts]] \\
  The Gram determinant of a lift at a twist, times the determinant of the compression of a comparison operator to the image of the lift, is a fixed number times a product of Hecke values; false lifts and clearing the projection.
- [[file:20261126T060300--the-comparison-operator__workbook.html][76. The comparison operator]] \\
  The adjoint of a gluing map takes the lift at every twist to one period map, up to a scalar whose modulus carries the Hecke values; a model with a cubic twist over the Gaussian integers; the general operator from statements read from the proof.
- [[file:20261126T050900--hecke-values-as-norms-of-lifts__workbook.html][77. Hecke values as norms of lifts]] \\
  For a critical pair, the squared norm of a theta lift of a character from $\mathrm U(1)$ is a partial Hecke value, up to a power of the norm of the twist; the product of Hecke values of one lift.
- [[file:20261126T051000--the-bound-is-attained-at-many-places__workbook.html][78. The bound is attained at many places]] \\
  On twelve copies of the variables, one normalizing scalar makes a Taylor coefficient attain the divisibility predicted by the prime sum at every prime prime to the twist.
- [[file:20261126T051100--test-forms-and-the-height-of-a-hecke-ratio__workbook.html][79. Test forms and the height of a Hecke ratio]] \\
  Test forms bound the height of the inverse ratio of Hecke values for a main pair: the size from the jets at the trivial twist, the valuation from the attained divisibility; small ratios and regular families.
- [[file:20261126T030900--denominators-of-projections-to-lifts__workbook.html][80. Denominators of projections to lifts]] \\
  Denominators of projections of integral forms to lifts, bounded by local losses, Selmer lengths, odd classes and congruences between characters; a running instance in rank one.
- [[file:20261126T070000--clearing-a-projection-two-classes__workbook.html][81. Clearing a projection: two classes]] \\
  A denominator measured by a clearing operator, and why false lifts must be excluded; for a class set with two elements the denominator is $5$, explained by a congruence and the costs on the Galois side that enter the clearing bound.
- [[file:20261126T051800--the-local-loss-and-the-sparse-places__workbook.html][82. The local loss and the sparse places]] \\
  The local loss in the clearing bound is a fixed multiple of the valuation of one integer at each prime; because the $b$-places are sparse, its sum over all primes is far below the budget.
- [[file:20261126T060400--congruences-bounded-by-lengths__workbook.html][83. Congruences bounded by lengths]] \\
  The denominators theorem from a bound for the eigenvalues of one clearing operator: lengths of modules of cocycles bound a Fitting ideal and through it the order of a Hecke element at the lift, worked out on $S_3$ at $3$; the costs of separating characters and of tame ramification.
- [[file:20261126T060500--auxiliary-primes-even-classes-at-no-cost__workbook.html][84. Auxiliary primes: even classes at no cost]] \\
  Auxiliary places chosen one after another, so that vanishing at the earlier ones forces vanishing at the next tame generator up to a power of $\ell$: a triangle of primes, in a model over $\mathbb{Q}$ and as the proof states it; why the clearing bound has a term for odd classes and none for even ones.
- [[file:20261126T031000--twists-with-few-congruences__workbook.html][85. Twists with few congruences]] \\
  The twisting theorem: a twist for each prime and each isomorphism above it, with small Selmer lengths, inverse ratios of Hecke $L$-values of bounded height for its first $m$ powers, and denominators within a common budget.  The twisting theorem is deduced in [[file:20261126T081300--from-a-representation-to-a-selmer-datum__workbook.html][/From a representation to a Selmer datum/]].
- [[file:20261126T040300--roots-of-unity-for-many-polynomials__workbook.html][86. Roots of unity for many polynomials]] \\
  Finitely many polynomials, each small at some point, are small at once at roots of unity of $\ell$-power order, with a loss as small as one likes.
- [[file:20261126T060600--lengths-as-values-of-polynomials__workbook.html][87. Lengths as values of polynomials]] \\
  The length of a Selmer module of a twist by roots of unity $\zeta_w$ is at most the valuation of one polynomial at the point $(\zeta_w - 1)_w$, a minor that is sharp on a line; the values of Stickelberger systems are values of polynomials too.  The polynomial input of the twisting theorem.
- [[file:20261126T060700--tied-primes__workbook.html][88. Tied primes]] \\
  Boundedly many auxiliary primes, each with a tie between the values of a cocycle at its tame generator and at its Frobenius element, bound the tied Selmer modules by their global and local terms, whatever the level of the characters: a descent on the total length, a count, and a mirror form.
- [[file:20261126T081200--a-measure-for-hecke-values__workbook.html][89. A measure for Hecke values]] \\
  A measure gives the Stickelberger systems for the Hecke ratios.  The models over $\mathbb{Q}(i)$ check the insertion law and compare the valuations with and without ramification.
- [[file:20261126T081300--from-a-representation-to-a-selmer-datum__workbook.html][90. From a representation to a Selmer datum]] \\
  Bounds on the global, local and first-prime terms give a Selmer datum on a lattice.  Together with the Stickelberger systems and the small-twists theorem, this gives the twisting theorem.
