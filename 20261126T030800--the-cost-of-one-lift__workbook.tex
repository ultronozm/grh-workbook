\medskip
Chapter~\ref{c37-chap} expresses the ratio of the chain Gram determinant at an \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\) to its value at the trivial twist as a product of single-lift Gram determinant ratios and a correction factor.  The determinant \(Z_j(\vartheta)\) uses the \(j\)-th lift at \(\vartheta^j\).  The proof needs a height bound for \(Z_j(1)/Z_j(\vartheta)\) when \(\vartheta\) varies with the prime \(\ell\) and the isomorphism \(\iota \mid \ell\).  It multiplies the Gram determinant of a single lift by the determinant of the compression of a second operator to its image.  The product is a twist-independent nonzero constant times a power of the ratio of the Hecke value product at the twist to its value at the trivial twist.  Chapter~\ref{l1-chap} gives an archimedean bound for the compression determinant at the trivial twist and a local bound at a twist when \(\ell\) is prime to its modulus norm and a clearing bound is given.  The Hecke quotient is a power of the modulus norm times the square of a product of ratios attached to critical pairs (Chapter~\ref{l3-chap}).  For a regular family the inverse ratios have bounded height, with the estimate for the selected main factors in Chapter~\ref{l5-chap} using Chapter~\ref{l4-chap}.  This chapter deduces Theorem~\ref{thm:main} from these inputs and Theorem~\ref{thm:operator}, which is read from the code.  Chapters~\ref{l1-chap}, \ref{l3-chap}, \ref{l4-chap} and~\ref{l5-chap} give the other arguments from the inputs listed there.

\section{The inputs}\label{sec:general}

We use the data of Chapter~\ref{c32-chap}: the number \(f\) of infinite places, the scales \(x < x_1 < b\) and \(a = xb\), the number \(m\) of lifts, the ranks \(r\) and \(k\), the direction amount \(\hdir\) and the denominator budget \(B = U/(mk) = fa\log^3 b/(mk)\) (Definition~\ref{c32-def:budget}); and the objects of Chapter~\ref{ob-chap}: twists \(\vartheta\) and \(m\)-fold twists, with modulus \(Q_\vartheta\) and \(\Nm Q_\vartheta\), the target dimension \(d_T\), the single lifts \(u_{\vartheta,j}\), and \(Z_j(\vartheta) = \det(u_{\vartheta^j,j}^*u_{\vartheta^j,j})\) for an \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\).

Fix \(1 \le j \le m\).  The Hecke value product \(\Hc_j(\vartheta)\) of lift \(j\) at a twist \(\vartheta\) is that of Definition~\ref{l1-def:hecke-product}.  Comparison operators \((\Upsilon_\vartheta)\), one operator on the target space of \(u_{\vartheta,j}\) for every twist \(\vartheta\), are defined in Definition~\ref{l1-def:comparison}, their compression determinants \(D_\vartheta\) in~\eqref{l1-eq:D}, and the statement that a real \(\mathrm{cl}(\ell)\) clears the projection at \((\iota, \vartheta, j)\) in Definition~\ref{l1-def:clears}.  A family \(\theta = (\theta_\iota)\) of \(m\)-fold twists, one for each prime \(\ell\) and each \(\iota \mid \ell\), is regular if it satisfies clauses (i) and (ii) of Definition~\ref{l5-def:small-ratios} and clause (iii) of Definition~\ref{l5-def:regular}.  Chapter~\ref{c39-chap} treats the denominators of the projection onto the image of a single lift and the sum of the clearing bounds in clause (iii).  Chapter~\ref{c40-chap} states the existence of regular families.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:operator}
For every \(1 \le j \le m\) the operators \(\Upsilon_\vartheta := \mathcal N h^2\, g_\vartheta g_\vartheta^*\), one for each twist \(\vartheta\), form a comparison operator.  Here \(g_\vartheta\) is a gluing map from tuples of forms on a smaller definite space to the target space of \(u_{\vartheta,j}\), \(h\) is the hook number of the diagram of the weight of those forms, and \(\mathcal N\) is the order of Section~\ref{ob-sec:chosen}, a common multiple of the orders of the stabilizers at the levels used.
\end{theorem}

This theorem is read from the code and is not proved in this workbook.  For clause (1) of Definition~\ref{l1-def:comparison} the proof uses \(\Hc_j(\vartheta)/\Hc_j(1)=\lvert c'_1\rvert^2/\lvert c'_\vartheta\rvert^2\), with \(c'_\vartheta\ne0\) for every twist.  For (2) it also uses \(g_\vartheta^*u_{\vartheta,j}=(c'_\vartheta)^{-1}u^\circ\), with \(u^\circ\) injective and independent of \(\vartheta\), as in Problem~\ref{l1-pr:rank-one}.  For (4) and (5) it writes \(\Upsilon_\vartheta\) as a sum of operators of rank at most one over a tight frame of tuples.

By Theorem~\ref{l1-thm:compression}, for every comparison operator every \(D_\vartheta\) is nonzero,
\[
  \lvert\iota(D_\vartheta)\rvert_\ell \le e^{d_T r\,\mathrm{cl}(\ell)}
  \qquad\text{and}\qquad
  \lvert\sigma(D_1)\rvert \le e^{3d_T fmb\log^2 b} ,
\]
the first for every twist \(\vartheta\), every \(\iota \mid \ell\) with \(\ell\) prime to \(\Nm Q_\vartheta\) and every real \(\mathrm{cl}(\ell)\) that clears the projection at \((\iota,\vartheta,j)\), the second for every \(\sigma \in \Aut(\C)\); and by Problem~\ref{l1-pr:quotient}, for every \(m\)-fold twist \(\vartheta\),
\[
  \frac{Z_j(1)}{Z_j(\vartheta)} = \frac{D_{\vartheta^j}}{D_1}\Bigl(\frac{\Hc_j(\vartheta^j)}{\Hc_j(1)}\Bigr)^{-d_T} .
\]
By Problem~\ref{l3-pr:hecke-product}, for the fixed \(j\) there are a nonnegative integer \(e\), a list of \(k/2\) critical pairs (Definition~\ref{ob-def:pair}) main for \(j\) (Definition~\ref{l3-def:main}), and a list of \(5m\) ramified critical pairs with a sign \(\epsilon = \pm 1\) for each entry.  For every twist \(\vartheta\), the quotient \(\Hc_j(\vartheta)/\Hc_j(1)\) is \((\Nm Q_\vartheta)^e\) times the square of the product of the ratios \(R_P(\vartheta,1)\) from the first list and \(R_P(\vartheta,\epsilon)\) from the second.  By Theorem~\ref{l5-thm:main-pair}, for a regular family \(\theta\) and a critical pair \(P\) main for \(j\), the family \(\iota \mapsto R_P(\theta_\iota^j)^{-1}\) has height at most \(6B - \hdir\).

\section{The deduction}\label{sec:deduction}

Let \(\theta\) be regular and \(1 \le j \le m\), let \((\Upsilon_\vartheta)\) be the comparison operator of Theorem~\ref{thm:operator}, and write \(\vartheta_\iota := \theta_\iota^j\).

\begin{problem}\label{pr:delta-height}
The family \(\iota \mapsto D_{\vartheta_\iota}/D_1\) has height at most \(d_T(3fmb\log^2 b + rB)\).
\end{problem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:hecke-height}
The family \(\iota \mapsto \bigl(\Hc_j(\vartheta_\iota)/\Hc_j(1)\bigr)^{-1}\) has height at most \(10mx_1 + k(6B - \hdir)\).
\end{problem}

It remains to compare the constants.  The following inequality is derived here from the sizes of the data.

\begin{problem}\label{pr:budget}
Let \(f \ge 1\), \(b > 1\) with \(\log b \ge 16\), \(x \ge 1\), \(\sqrt x \le m \le 2\sqrt x\), \(r \ge 2m\), \(k = r + 1 - m\), \(1 \le x_1 \le b\), \(a = xb\) and \(B = fa\log^3 b/(mk)\).  Then for every real \(\hdir\)
\[
  3fmb\log^2 b + rB + 10mx_1 + k(6B - \hdir) \le k(B\log b - \hdir) .
\]
The data satisfy these hypotheses.
\end{problem}

Problems~\ref{pr:delta-height}, \ref{pr:hecke-height}, \ref{l1-pr:quotient}, \ref{l5-pr:height-ops} and~\ref{pr:budget}, with Problem~\ref{c5-pr:height-mul}, prove the following.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
For the data, every regular family \(\theta\) of \(m\)-fold twists, and every \(1 \le j \le m\), the family
\[
  \iota \longmapsto \frac{Z_j(1)}{Z_j(\theta_\iota)}
\]
has height at most \(d_T\, k\,(B\log b - \hdir)\).
\end{theorem}

\begin{problem}\label{pr:negative}
For the data, \(B\log b < \hdir\).  So the bound of Theorem~\ref{thm:main} is \(-d_Tk(\hdir - B\log b)\) with \(\hdir - B\log b > 0\).
\end{problem}

So the bound of Theorem~\ref{thm:main} is, per unit of \(d_T\), \(k\) times the budget \(B\log b\) less \(k\) times the direction amount.  Chapter~\ref{tc-chap} combines these bounds for \(1\le j\le m\) with the factorization of Chapter~\ref{c37-chap} and the height bound there for the inverse of the scalar whose \(d_T\)-th power is the correction factor.  Theorem~\ref{thm:operator} is read from the code.  The other arguments are given in Chapters~\ref{l1-chap}, \ref{l3-chap}, \ref{l4-chap} and~\ref{l5-chap} from the inputs listed there.
