\paragraph{Prerequisites.}
Chapters~\ref{c18-chap}, \ref{c22-chap}, \ref{c28-chap} and~\ref{c37-chap}.

\medskip
The ratio of the determinant of one lift at the trivial twist to its value at a twist has bounded height.  The chapter introduces false lifts and comparison operators, bounds the denominators and the conjugates of the projection determinant, and counts the main pairs of each lift.  The size comes from the jets, the valuation from the sharpness of Chapter~\ref{c18-chap}: the bound for the composite lift read backwards.

This chapter is being drafted.
