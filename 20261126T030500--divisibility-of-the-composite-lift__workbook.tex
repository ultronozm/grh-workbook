\paragraph{Prerequisites.}
Chapters~\ref{c14-chap}--\ref{c17-chap}, \ref{c23-chap}, \ref{c24-chap} and~\ref{c32-chap}.

\medskip
The composite lift is divisible at each prime \(p\) by the gain that the count of Part~III predicts, on a submodule of small colength, up to an error whose weighted sum over all primes is small.  The chapter states the count in general, separates split primes, collisions, exceptional primes and the level prime, and assembles the errors.  The simplest cases are those of Chapters~\ref{c14-chap}--\ref{c17-chap} and~\ref{c23-chap}--\ref{c24-chap}.

This chapter is being drafted.
